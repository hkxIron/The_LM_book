<reasoning>
To find out how much money Bill would save by using raspberries instead of blueberries, we'll first need to determine how much fruit he needs for 4 batches of muffins and then calculate the cost for both fruits.

1. **Calculate the total amount of fruit needed**:
   Each batch takes 12 ounces of fruit, and Bill is making 4 batches. 
   \[
   \text{Total fruit needed} = 12 \text{ ounces/batch} \times 4 \text{ batches} = 48 \text{ ounces}
   \]

2. **Determine the number of cartons needed for blueberries**:
   Blueberries are sold in 6-ounce cartons. To find out how many cartons Bill needs:
   \[
   \text{Number of cartons for blueberries} = \frac{48 \text{ ounces}}{6 \text{ ounces/carton}} = 8 \text{ cartons}
   \]

3. **Calculate the cost for blueberries**:
   Blueberries cost $5.00 per carton. Therefore, the total cost for the blueberries is:
   \[
   \text{Cost of blueberries} = 8 \text{ cartons} \times 5 \text{ dollars/carton} = 40 \text{ dollars}
   \]

4. **Determine the number of cartons needed for raspberries**:
   Raspberries are sold in 8-ounce cartons. To find out how many cartons Bill needs:
   \[
   \text{Number of cartons for raspberries} = \frac{48 \text{ ounces}}{8 \text{ ounces/carton}} = 6 \text{ cartons}
   \]

5. **Calculate the cost for raspberries**:
   Raspberries cost $3.00 per carton. Therefore, the total cost for the raspberries is:
   \[
   \text{Cost of raspberries} = 6 \text{ cartons} \times 3 \text{ dollars/carton} = 18 \text{ dollars}
   \]

6. **Calculate the savings**:
   Now we can determine how much money Bill would save by using raspberries instead of blueberries:
   \[
   \text{Savings} = \text{Cost of blueberries} - \text{Cost of raspberries} = 40 \text{ dollars} - 18 \text{ dollars} = 22 \text{ dollars}
   \]

Therefore, Bill would save $22 by using raspberries instead of blueberries.
