<reasoning>
To find out Nancy's weight based on her daily water intake, we know that she drinks 60% of her body weight in water, and her daily intake is 54 pounds.

1. Let's denote Nancy's weight as \( W \) (in pounds).
2. According to the problem, we can express her water intake in terms of her weight:
   \[
   \text{Water intake} = 0.6 \times W
   \]
   This means that 60% of her weight is equal to her daily water intake.

3. We know her daily water intake is 54 pounds, so we can set up the equation:
   \[
   0.6 \times W = 54
   \]

4. To find \( W \), we need to isolate it. We can do this by dividing both sides of the equation by 0.6:
   \[
   W = \frac{54}{0.6}
   \]

5. Now, we perform the division:
   \[
   W = 54 \div 0.6
   \]
   To simplify this, we can convert 0.6 to a fraction. Since \( 0.6 = \frac{6}{10} = \frac{3}{5} \), we can rewrite the division:
   \[
   W = 54 \times \frac{5}{3}
   \]

6. Now, calculate \( 54 \times \frac{5}{3} \):
   \[
   W = \frac{54 \times 5}{3} = \frac{270}{3} = 90
   \]

Therefore, Nancy weighs 90 pounds.
