To find the total number of hours Bob logs in a month, we first need to determine how many hours he works in a week, and then how many weeks are typically in a month.

1. **Calculate weekly hours:**
   Bob logs 10 hours of work each day and works 5 days a week.
   \[
   \text{Weekly hours} = \text{Daily hours} \times \text{Number of days}
   \]
   Here, the daily hours are 10 and the number of days is 5.
   \[
   \text{Weekly hours} = 10 \text{ hours/day} \times 5 \text{ days} = 50 \text{ hours/week}
   \]

2. **Calculate monthly hours:**
   There are typically about 4 weeks in a month. To find the total monthly hours, we multiply the weekly hours by the number of weeks in a month.
   \[
   \text{Monthly hours} = \text{Weekly hours} \times \text{Number of weeks}
   \]
   Using the calculated weekly hours:
   \[
   \text{Monthly hours} = 50 \text{ hours/week} \times 4 \text{ weeks} = 200 \text{ hours/month}
   \]

Therefore, Bob logs a total of 200 hours in a month.
