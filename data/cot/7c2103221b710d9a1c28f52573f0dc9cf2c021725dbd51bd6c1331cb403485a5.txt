To find the total number of pieces of clothing that Mr. Jones owns, we need to first determine how many shirts he has, given that he has 6 shirts for every pair of pants.

2. **Identify the number of pants**: According to the problem, Mr. Jones has 40 pairs of pants.

3. **Calculate the total number of shirts**: To find the total number of shirts, we multiply the number of pants by the number of shirts per pair of pants:
   \[
   \text{Total shirts} = \text{Number of pants} \times \text{Shirts per pair of pants}
   \]
   Plugging in the values:
   \[
   \text{Total shirts} = 40 \, \text{pants} \times 6 \, \text{shirts/pair of pants} = 240 \, \text{shirts}
   \]

4. **Calculate the total number of pieces of clothing**: Now we add the total number of shirts to the total number of pants to get the total pieces of clothing:
   \[
   \text{Total clothing} = \text{Total shirts} + \text{Total pants}
   \]
   Substituting the values we found:
   \[
   \text{Total clothing} = 240 \, \text{shirts} + 40 \, \text{pants} = 280 \, \text{pieces of clothing}
   \]

Therefore, Mr. Jones owns a total of 280 pieces of clothing.
