   \text{Total water needed} = 100 \text{ balloons} \times 3 \text{ ounces/balloon} = 300 \text{ ounces}

   \[

   \[
   \text{Total money} = 2 \times 10 \text{ dollars} = 20 \text{ dollars}
   \]

5. **Calculate the change Marcus will have after buying the water.**

   \[
   \text{Change} = 20 \text{ dollars} - 15 \text{ dollars} = 5 \text{ dollars}
