To determine how long it would take Jason to eat all 27 potatoes, we first need to find the rate at which he eats the potatoes.

1. **Calculate the rate of eating potatoes**: We know Jason eats 3 potatoes in 20 minutes. 
   - To find out how long it takes him to eat 1 potato, we divide the time by the number of potatoes:
     \[
     \text{Time per potato} = \frac{20 \text{ minutes}}{3 \text{ potatoes}} \approx 6.67 \text{ minutes per potato}
     \]

2. **Calculate the total time to eat 27 potatoes**: Now that we know it takes approximately 6.67 minutes to eat one potato, we can find out how long it would take him to eat 27 potatoes by multiplying:
   \[
   \text{Total time (in minutes)} = 27 \text{ potatoes} \times 6.67 \text{ minutes per potato} \approx 180 \text{ minutes}
   \]

3. **Convert minutes to hours**: Since we need the answer in hours, we convert 180 minutes to hours by dividing by 60 (the number of minutes in an hour):
   \[
   \text{Total time (in hours)} = \frac{180 \text{ minutes}}{60} = 3 \text{ hours}
   \]

Therefore, the total time it would take for Jason to eat all 27 potatoes is 3 hours.
