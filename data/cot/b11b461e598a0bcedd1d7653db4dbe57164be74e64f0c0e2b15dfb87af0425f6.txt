   \[
   32 + x = 2(5 + x)

   \[
   32 + x = 10 + 2x
   \]

   To isolate x, we can move all terms involving x to one side and constant terms to the other side:
   \[
   32 - 10 = 2x - x
   \]
   This simplifies to:
   \[
   22 = x
   \]

   \[
   \text{Joel's age in 22 years} = 5 + 22 = 27
