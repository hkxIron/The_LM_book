To find out how many hours Roy spent on sports activities in school that week, we can follow these steps:

   \[
   \text{Days attended} = 5 - 2 = 3 \text{ days}
   \]

2. **Calculate the total hours spent on sports**: Since Roy spends 2 hours on sports activities each day he attends school, we multiply the number of days he attended by the hours he spends each day:
   \[
   \text{Total hours spent} = \text{Days attended} \times \text{Hours per day} = 3 \text{ days} \times 2 \text{ hours/day}
   \]
   Performing this multiplication gives:
   \[
   \text{Total hours spent} = 3 \times 2 = 6 \text{ hours}
   \]

Therefore, Roy spent a total of 6 hours on sports activities in school that week.
