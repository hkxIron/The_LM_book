To find out how much time Joseph will save by taking route B for his round trip to Boston, we need to calculate the total time for both routes and then find the difference.

1. **Calculate the total time for Route A:**
   - Route A takes 5 hours to Boston.
   - Since this is a round trip, he will take the same amount of time to return home.
   - Total time for Route A = Time to Boston + Time back home = 5 hours + 5 hours = 10 hours.

2. **Calculate the total time for Route B:**
   - Route B takes 2 hours to Boston.
   - Similarly, the time to return home is also 2 hours.
   - Total time for Route B = Time to Boston + Time back home = 2 hours + 2 hours = 4 hours.

3. **Calculate the time saved by taking Route B:**
   - Time saved = Total time for Route A - Total time for Route B.
   - Time saved = 10 hours - 4 hours = 6 hours.

Therefore, by taking Route B for his trip to Boston and back, Joseph will save 6 hours.
