   \[

   \]

   \[
   6 \text{ miles} - 4 \text{ miles} = 2 \text{ miles}

   \[
   \text{Time} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Speed}} = \frac{2 \text{ miles}}{4 \text{ MPH}} = \frac{1}{2} \text{ hours}
   \]

   Since we want the answer in minutes, we can convert \(\frac{1}{2}\) hours to minutes by multiplying by 60.
   \[
   \frac{1}{2} \text{ hours} \times 60 = 30 \text{ minutes}
   \]
