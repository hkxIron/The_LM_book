<reasoning>
To find out how much money the winner of the lottery will receive, we first need to determine how much Lily makes from selling all her tickets.

1. **Determine the selling price of each ticket**:
   - The first ticket is sold for $1.
   - Each subsequent ticket is sold for $1 more than the previous ticket.
   - Thus, the selling prices for the tickets are:
     - Ticket 1: $1
     - Ticket 2: $1 + $1 = $2
     - Ticket 3: $2 + $1 = $3
     - Ticket 4: $3 + $1 = $4
     - Ticket 5: $4 + $1 = $5

2. **Calculate the total amount Lily collects from selling all tickets**:
   - Now, we add the prices of all tickets together:
     \[
     \text{Total} = 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5
     \]
   - This can be simplified:
     \[
     \text{Total} = 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 = 15
     \]

3. **Calculate the total amount given as the prize**:
   - Lily keeps a profit of $4 from the total sales.
   - Therefore, the amount given away as the prize is:
     \[
     \text{Prize} = \text{Total} - \text{Profit} = 15 - 4
     \]
   - Performing the subtraction:
     \[
     \text{Prize} = 15 - 4 = 11
     \]

Now we have determined that the winner of the lottery will receive $11.
