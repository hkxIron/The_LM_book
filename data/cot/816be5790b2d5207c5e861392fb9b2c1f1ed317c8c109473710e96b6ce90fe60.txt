   \[
   \text{Students who participated} = \frac{39}{3}
   \]

   \[
   \frac{39}{3} = 13
   \]

   To find the number of students who did not participate, we subtract the number of students who participated from the total number of students:
   \[
   \text{Students who did not participate} = \text{Total students} - \text{Students who participated}
   \]
   Plugging in the numbers:
   \[
   \text{Students who did not participate} = 39 - 13
   \]
   Performing the subtraction:
   \[
   39 - 13 = 26
   \]

Therefore, the number of students who did not participate in the competition is 26.
