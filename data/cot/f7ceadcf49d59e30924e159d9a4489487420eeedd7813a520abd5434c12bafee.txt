     \text{Half of } 18 = \frac{18}{2} = 9

     \[

     \]

     \text{Total earnings including previous weekend} = \text{Total from this weekend} + \text{Earnings from previous weekend}
     \]

     \text{Total earnings including previous weekend} = 27 + 20 = 47
     \]
   - Therefore, John's total earnings now amount to $47.

   - To find out how much more he needs, we subtract his current total earnings from his goal:
     \[
     \text{Amount needed} = \text{Goal} - \text{Total earnings}
     \]
