Tim's work is 20 miles away, and he bikes to and from work. This means that every workday, he is biking 20 miles to work and 20 miles back home for a total of 40 miles per day. 

Step 1: Calculate the total miles Tim bikes for workdays
He works for 5 days, so the total number of miles biked to work in a week is 40 miles/day * 5 days = 200 miles.

Step 2: Add the weekend bike ride
On the weekend, Tim goes for a bike ride of 200 miles. So, the total miles biked in a week is 200 miles (workdays) + 200 miles (weekend) = 400 miles.

Step 3: Calculate the total time he spends biking
Given that Tim can bike at a speed of 25 mph, we can find out how long he spends biking by dividing the total distance by his speed. The time taken is 400 miles / 25 mph = 16 hours.

So, Tim spends 16 hours biking in a week.
