1. **Identify the total number of marbles**: Eric has a total of 20 marbles.

3. **Calculate the total number of non-green marbles**: 
   We add the number of white and blue marbles together:
   \[
   \text{Total non-green marbles} = \text{White marbles} + \text{Blue marbles} = 12 + 6 = 18
   \]
   This tells us that Eric has 18 marbles that are either white or blue.

4. **Determine the number of green marbles**: 
   To find the number of green marbles, we subtract the total number of non-green marbles from the total number of marbles:
   \[
   \text{Green marbles} = \text{Total marbles} - \text{Total non-green marbles} = 20 - 18 = 2
   \]

So, Eric has 2 green marbles.
