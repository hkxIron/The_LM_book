<reasoning>
To find out how much money Tina earns from selling her postcards, we'll follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of postcards made in 6 days**: 
   - Tina makes 30 postcards in one day.
   - Over 6 days, the total number of postcards she makes is:
     \[
     30 \text{ postcards/day} \times 6 \text{ days} = 180 \text{ postcards}
     \]

2. **Calculate the total earnings from selling the postcards**: 
   - Each postcard is sold for $5.
   - Therefore, the total money earned from selling all postcards is calculated by multiplying the number of postcards by the price per postcard:
     \[
     180 \text{ postcards} \times 5 \text{ dollars/postcard} = 900 \text{ dollars}
     \]
