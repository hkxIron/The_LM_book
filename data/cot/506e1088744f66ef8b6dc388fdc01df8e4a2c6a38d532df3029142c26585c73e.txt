<reasoning>
To find out how many strawberries Hannah has at the end of April, we will follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of strawberries harvested in April.**
   - Hannah harvests 5 strawberries daily.
   - April has 30 days.
   - We can calculate the total strawberries harvested by multiplying the daily harvest by the number of days:
     \[
     \text{Total strawberries harvested} = 5 \text{ strawberries/day} \times 30 \text{ days} = 150 \text{ strawberries}
     \]

2. **Account for the strawberries given away and stolen.**
   - Hannah gives away 20 strawberries to her friends.
   - Additionally, 30 strawberries are stolen.
   - We need to calculate the total number of strawberries lost:
     \[
     \text{Total strawberries lost} = 20 + 30 = 50 \text{ strawberries}
     \]

3. **Calculate the remaining strawberries.**
   - To find out how many strawberries Hannah has left, we subtract the total strawberries lost from the total harvested:
     \[
     \text{Remaining strawberries} = \text{Total strawberries harvested} - \text{Total strawberries lost}
     \]
     \[
     \text{Remaining strawberries} = 150 - 50 = 100 \text{ strawberries}
     \]

Now we have the final count of strawberries Hannah has at the end of April.

Therefore, the final count of strawberries is:
</reasoning>
<answer>100</answer>
