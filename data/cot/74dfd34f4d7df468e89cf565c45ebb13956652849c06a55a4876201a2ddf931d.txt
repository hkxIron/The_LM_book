**Step 1: Calculate the rate at which four kids wash whiteboards.**

We know that four kids can wash three whiteboards in 20 minutes. To find out how many whiteboards one kid can wash in one minute, we first find the combined rate of the four kids.

The total number of whiteboards washed by four kids in 20 minutes is 3. Therefore, the rate for four kids is:
\[ \text{Rate of 4 kids} = \frac{3 \text{ whiteboards}}{20 \text{ minutes}} = 0.15 \text{ whiteboards per minute} \]

**Step 2: Calculate the rate for one kid.**

Since the rate we found is for four kids, we can find the rate for one kid by dividing the rate of four kids by 4:
\[ \text{Rate of 1 kid} = \frac{0.15 \text{ whiteboards per minute}}{4} = 0.0375 \text{ whiteboards per minute} \]

**Step 3: Calculate how long it takes one kid to wash six whiteboards.**

Now, we need to find out how long it would take for one kid to wash six whiteboards. We can set up the equation:
\[ \text{Time} = \frac{\text{Total whiteboards}}{\text{Rate of 1 kid}} \]
Substituting in the values we have:
\[ \text{Time} = \frac{6 \text{ whiteboards}}{0.0375 \text{ whiteboards per minute}} \]

**Step 4: Perform the calculation.**

Now we divide 6 by 0.0375:
\[ \text{Time} = 6 \div 0.0375 = 160 \text{ minutes} \]

This indicates that it takes one kid 160 minutes to wash six whiteboards.
