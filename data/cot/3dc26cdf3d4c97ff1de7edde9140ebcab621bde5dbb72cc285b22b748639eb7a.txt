<reasoning>
First, let's determine the total number of pages John needs to write for 3 books, with each book being 400 pages.

1. Calculate the total number of pages:
   \[
   \text{Total pages} = \text{Number of books} \times \text{Pages per book} = 3 \times 400 = 1200 \text{ pages}
   \]
   Here, I multiplied the number of books (3) by the number of pages per book (400) to find the total pages he needs to write.

2. Next, we need to find out how many days it will take John to write 1200 pages if he writes 20 pages a day.
   \[
   \text{Days required} = \frac{\text{Total pages}}{\text{Pages per day}} = \frac{1200}{20}
   \]
   In this step, I divided the total number of pages (1200) by the number of pages John writes each day (20) to find out how many days it will take.

3. Now, performing the division:
   \[
   \text{Days required} = \frac{1200}{20} = 60
   \]
   This gives us the total number of days John will need to write the 3 books.

Therefore, it will take John a total of 60 days to write 3 books of 400 pages each.
</reasoning>
<answer>60</answer>
