To find out how many pages each book has on average, we can follow these steps:

1. **Determine the total number of pages in the stack of books**: Since Jack has a stack of books that is 12 inches thick, and we know that 80 pages are equivalent to 1 inch, we can calculate the total number of pages in the entire stack.

   \[
   \text{Total pages} = \text{Thickness of stack} \times \text{Pages per inch} = 12 \text{ inches} \times 80 \text{ pages/inch}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Total pages} = 960 \text{ pages}
   \]

2. **Calculate the average number of pages per book**: Now that we know there are 960 pages in total and that Jack has 6 books, we can find the average number of pages per book by dividing the total number of pages by the number of books:
   \[
   \text{Average pages per book} = \frac{\text{Total pages}}{\text{Number of books}} = \frac{960 \text{ pages}}{6 \text{ books}}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Average pages per book} = 160 \text{ pages}
   \]

After performing these calculations, we conclude that each book has an average of 160 pages.
