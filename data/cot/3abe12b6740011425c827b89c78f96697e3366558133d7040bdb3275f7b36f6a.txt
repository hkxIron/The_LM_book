1. **Calculate the number of bricks in one wall**:

   - There are 50 rows in each wall.
   - To find the total number of bricks in one wall, we multiply the number of bricks per row by the number of rows:
     \[
     \text{Bricks in one wall} = \text{Bricks per row} \times \text{Number of rows}
     \]
     \[
     \text{Bricks in one wall} = 30 \text{ bricks/row} \times 50 \text{ rows} = 1500 \text{ bricks}
     \]

2. **Calculate the total number of bricks for both walls**:
   - Since there are two walls, we multiply the number of bricks in one wall by 2:
     \[
     \text{Total bricks} = \text{Bricks in one wall} \times 2
     \]
     \[
     \text{Total bricks} = 1500 \text{ bricks} \times 2 = 3000 \text{ bricks}
     \]

Therefore, the total number of bricks used to make both walls is 3000.
