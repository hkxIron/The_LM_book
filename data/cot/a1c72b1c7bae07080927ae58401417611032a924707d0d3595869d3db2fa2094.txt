     \[
     \text{Total cupcakes} = \text{Number of packs} \times \text{Cupcakes per pack} = 4 \times 15

     \[

     \[
     \text{Total needed} = 100 \text{ children} \times 1 \text{ cupcake/child} = 100
     \]
   - Thus, she needs 100 cupcakes.

   - We already found that she has 60 cupcakes, but she needs 100.
   - We can find out how many more cupcakes she needs by subtracting the number of cupcakes she has from the number she needs:
     \[
     \text{Additional cupcakes needed} = \text{Total needed} - \text{Total cupcakes} = 100 - 60
     \]
   - Performing the subtraction:
     \[
     100 - 60 = 40
     \]
   - So, she needs 40 more cupcakes.

4. **Determine how many packs of 10 cupcakes she needs to buy:**

     \[
     \text{Packs needed} = \frac{\text{Additional cupcakes needed}}{\text{Cupcakes per pack}} = \frac{40}{10}
     \]

     \[
     \frac{40}{10} = 4
     \]
