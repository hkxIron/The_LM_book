To solve this problem, we need to determine how much the paying students need to contribute to cover the cost of feeding all students, including those who receive free lunches.

1. **Determine the total number of students**: 
   We know that 40% of students receive a free lunch. This means that 60% of the students are paying students. Let's denote the total number of students as \( N \).

2. **Calculate the number of students receiving free lunch**:
   Since 40% receive free lunch, the number of students receiving free lunch is \( 0.4N \).

3. **Calculate the number of paying students**:
   The number of paying students would then be \( 0.6N \).

4. **Total cost to feed students**:
   We are given that it costs $210 to feed 50 students. This means that the total cost for feeding all students is $210.

5. **Set up the equation for total cost**:
   The total cost ($210) must be covered by the contributions of the paying students. Let \( P \) be the price each paying student pays for lunch. The total contribution from paying students can be expressed as:
   \[
   \text{Total contribution from paying students} = P \times (0.6N)
   \]
   Since this total contribution must equal the total cost of $210, we can set up the equation:
   \[
   P \times (0.6N) = 210
   \]

6. **Determine \( N \) from the number of students**:
   We know that 50 students are mentioned, and since 40% are receiving free lunches, we can find \( N \):
   \[
   0.4N = 0.4 \times 50 = 20 \text{ (students on free lunch)}
   \]
   Therefore, the number of paying students is:
   \[
   0.6N = 50 - 20 = 30 \text{ (paying students)}
   \]

7. **Substituting \( N \) into the equation**:
   Now we can substitute \( N = 50 \) into our earlier equation:
   \[
   P \times (0.6 \times 50) = 210
   \]
   Simplifying the left side:
   \[
   P \times 30 = 210
   \]

8. **Solve for \( P \)**:
   To find \( P \), we divide both sides of the equation by 30:
   \[
   P = \frac{210}{30}
   \]
   Performing the division:
   \[
   P = 7
   \]

Thus, each paying student pays $7 for lunch.
