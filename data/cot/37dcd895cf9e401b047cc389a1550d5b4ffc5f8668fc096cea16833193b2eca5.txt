   \[

   - The leaves fell over a total period of 3 hours (first hour + second hour + third hour).

3. **Calculate the average number of leaves per hour:**
   The average is calculated by dividing the total number of leaves by the total number of hours. The formula for average is:
   \[
   \text{Average} = \frac{\text{Total leaves}}{\text{Total hours}} = \frac{15 \text{ leaves}}{3 \text{ hours}} = 5 \text{ leaves per hour}
   \]
