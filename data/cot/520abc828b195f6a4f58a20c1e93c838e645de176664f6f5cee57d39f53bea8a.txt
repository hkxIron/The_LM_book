<reasoning>
To find out how much profit Tom earned last month, we need to calculate his total income from mowing lawns and pulling weeds and then subtract his expenses. 

1. **Calculate the income from mowing lawns:**
   - Tom mows 3 lawns and charges $12 per lawn.
   - Income from mowing = Number of lawns mowed × Charge per lawn
   - Income from mowing = 3 × $12 = $36

2. **Calculate the income from pulling weeds:**
   - Tom earns $10 from pulling weeds.
   - Income from pulling weeds = $10

3. **Calculate total income:**
   - Total income = Income from mowing + Income from pulling weeds
   - Total income = $36 + $10 = $46

4. **Calculate expenses:**
   - Tom spends $17 on gas.
   - Expenses = $17

5. **Calculate profit:**
   - Profit = Total income - Expenses
   - Profit = $46 - $17 = $29

Therefore, Tom earned a profit of $29 last month.
</reasoning>
