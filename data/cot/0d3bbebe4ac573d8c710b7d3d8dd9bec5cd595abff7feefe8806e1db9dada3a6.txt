<reasoning>
To find out how much Harry earns in a week, we need to calculate the total number of dogs he walks throughout the week and then multiply that by the amount he is paid per dog.

1. **Calculate the total number of dogs walked each day**:
   - On Monday, Wednesday, and Friday, he walks 7 dogs.
   - On Tuesday, he walks 12 dogs.
   - On Thursday, he walks 9 dogs.

2. **Sum the number of dogs walked each day**:
   - Total dogs walked on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday: 
     \(7 \text{ dogs} \times 3 \text{ days} = 21 \text{ dogs}\)
   - Total dogs walked on Tuesday: 
     \(12 \text{ dogs}\)
   - Total dogs walked on Thursday: 
     \(9 \text{ dogs}\)

3. **Add all the dogs together**:
   \[
   21 \text{ dogs (Mon, Wed, Fri)} + 12 \text{ dogs (Tue)} + 9 \text{ dogs (Thu)} = 21 + 12 + 9 = 42 \text{ dogs}
   \]

4. **Calculate Harry's total earnings**:
   He earns $5 for each dog he walks.
   \[
   \text{Total earnings} = 42 \text{ dogs} \times 5 \text{ dollars/dog} = 210 \text{ dollars}
   \]

Therefore, Harry earns a total of $210 in a week.
