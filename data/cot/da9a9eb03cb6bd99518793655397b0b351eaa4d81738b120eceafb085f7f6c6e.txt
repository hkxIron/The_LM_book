     \[
     \text{Relative speed} = \text{Husband's speed} - \text{Yolanda's speed} = 40 \text{ miles/hour} - 20 \text{ miles/hour} = 20 \text{ miles/hour}.
     \]

   - Since Yolanda has a 5-mile head start, we can find out how long it will take her husband to close this gap at a relative speed of 20 miles per hour:
     \[
     \text{Time} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Relative speed}} = \frac{5 \text{ miles}}{20 \text{ miles/hour}} = 0.25 \text{ hours}.
     \]

4. **Convert the time from hours to minutes.**
   - We need to convert 0.25 hours into minutes:
     \[
     0.25 \text{ hours} \times 60 \text{ minutes/hour} = 15 \text{ minutes}.
     \]
