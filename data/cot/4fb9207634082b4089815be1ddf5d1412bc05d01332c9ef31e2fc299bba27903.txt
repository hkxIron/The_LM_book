To find out how many papayas Jake needs to buy from the farmer's market for 4 weeks, we first need to determine how many papayas he and his family consume in one week.

1. **Calculate the total number of papayas consumed in one week:**
   - Jake eats 3 papayas in one week.
   - His brother eats 5 papayas in one week.
   - His father eats 4 papayas in one week.

   To find the total for one week, we add these amounts together:

   \[
   \text{Total papayas in one week} = \text{Jake's papayas} + \text{Brother's papayas} + \text{Father's papayas}
   \]

   Substituting the values:

   \[
   \text{Total papayas in one week} = 3 + 5 + 4
   \]

   Now, performing the addition:

   \[
   3 + 5 = 8
   \]
   \[
   8 + 4 = 12
   \]

   So, the total number of papayas consumed by the family in one week is 12.

2. **Calculate the total number of papayas consumed in 4 weeks:**
   To find out how many papayas are consumed over 4 weeks, we multiply the weekly total by 4:

   \[
   \text{Total papayas in 4 weeks} = \text{Total papayas in one week} \times 4
   \]

   Substituting the known value:

   \[
   \text{Total papayas in 4 weeks} = 12 \times 4
   \]

   Now, performing the multiplication:

   \[
   12 \times 4 = 48
   \]

Therefore, Jake needs to buy a total of 48 papayas from the farmer's market to account for the consumption of his family over 4 weeks.
