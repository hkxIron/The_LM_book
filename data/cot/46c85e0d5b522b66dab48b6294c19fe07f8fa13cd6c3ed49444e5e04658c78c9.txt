<reasoning>
To determine how many pies Eddie, his sister, and his mother can bake together in 7 days, we first need to find the total number of pies each person can bake in one day.

1. **Calculate Eddie's daily pie production:**
   Eddie bakes 3 pies a day.

2. **Calculate his sister's daily pie production:**
   His sister bakes 6 pies a day.

3. **Calculate his mother's daily pie production:**
   His mother bakes 8 pies a day.

Now, we will add their daily pie productions together to find the total number of pies they can bake in one day:

\[
\text{Total pies per day} = \text{Eddie's pies} + \text{Sister's pies} + \text{Mother's pies}
\]
\[
\text{Total pies per day} = 3 + 6 + 8
\]

Calculating the total:

\[
3 + 6 = 9
\]
\[
9 + 8 = 17
\]

So, together, they can bake 17 pies in one day.

4. **Calculate the total pies they can bake in 7 days:**
Now, we multiply the total number of pies they can bake in one day by the number of days (7):

\[
\text{Total pies in 7 days} = \text{Total pies per day} \times 7
\]
\[
\text{Total pies in 7 days} = 17 \times 7
\]

Calculating this:

\[
17 \times 7 = 119
\]

Therefore, Eddie, his sister, and his mother can bake a total of 119 pies in 7 days.
</reasoning>
