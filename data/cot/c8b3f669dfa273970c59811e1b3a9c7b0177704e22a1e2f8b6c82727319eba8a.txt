To determine how many cookies each child will eat, we will follow the steps below:

1. **Calculate the total number of cookies.** 
   - Aku's mother bought 3 packages of cookies, and each package contains 25 cookies.

     \[
     3 \text{ packages} \times 25 \text{ cookies/package} = 75 \text{ cookies}
     \]

2. **Determine the total number of children.**
   - Aku invited 4 friends, and we must include Aku himself in the total count.
   - Thus, the total number of children is:
     \[
     4 \text{ friends} + 1 \text{ Aku} = 5 \text{ children}
     \]

3. **Calculate the number of cookies each child will eat.**
   - Since the cookies are shared equally among all children, we divide the total number of cookies by the number of children:
     \[
     \frac{75 \text{ cookies}}{5 \text{ children}} = 15 \text{ cookies/child}
     \]
