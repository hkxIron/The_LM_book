   The formula for the area of a triangle is given by:
   \[
   \text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height}
   \]
   Here, the base of the triangle is 40 centimeters and the height is 20 centimeters.

   Plugging in the values:
   \[
   \text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times 40 \, \text{cm} \times 20 \, \text{cm}
   \]

   \[
   40 \times 20 = 800
   \]
   
   Now, we apply the \( \frac{1}{2} \):
   \[
   \text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times 800 = 400 \, \text{cm}^2
   \]

   \[
   \text{Total Area} = \text{Area of one house} \times \text{Number of houses}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Total Area} = 400 \, \text{cm}^2 \times 3
   \]

   Now calculating the total area:
   \[
   400 \times 3 = 1200 \, \text{cm}^2
   \]
