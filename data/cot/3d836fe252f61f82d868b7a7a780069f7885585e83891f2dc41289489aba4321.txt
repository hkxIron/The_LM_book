<reasoning>
To find out how much Mr. Smith will pay after incurring a 2% finance charge on his balance of $150, we need to calculate the finance charge first and then add it to the original balance.

1. **Calculate the finance charge**: 
   - The finance charge is calculated as a percentage of the original balance. Here, the percentage is 2%, and the original balance is $150.
   - We can find the finance charge using the formula:
     \[
     \text{Finance Charge} = \text{Original Balance} \times \left(\frac{\text{Percentage}}{100}\right)
     \]
   - Substituting the values:
     \[
     \text{Finance Charge} = 150 \times \left(\frac{2}{100}\right) = 150 \times 0.02 = 3
     \]
   - So, the finance charge is $3.

2. **Calculate the total amount Mr. Smith will pay**: 
   - To find the total amount Mr. Smith will pay, we add the finance charge to the original balance.
   - The total amount is calculated as:
     \[
     \text{Total Amount} = \text{Original Balance} + \text{Finance Charge}
     \]
   - Substituting the values we have:
     \[
     \text{Total Amount} = 150 + 3 = 153
     \]

Therefore, after calculating the finance charge and adding it to the original balance, Mr. Smith will pay $153 to settle his balance today.
</reasoning>
<answer>153</answer>
