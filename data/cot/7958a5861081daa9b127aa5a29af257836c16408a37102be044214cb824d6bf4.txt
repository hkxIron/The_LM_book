   \[

   \[

   Now, we add the coffee brewed on weekdays and the weekend:
   \[
   \text{Total brewed in a week} = \text{Total brewed on weekdays} + \text{Total brewed on weekend} = 250 \text{ cups} + 120 \text{ cups} = 370 \text{ cups}
