     \[

     \]

     \[
     \text{Red marbles given away} = \text{Total red marbles} - \text{Red marbles kept} = 6 - 1 = 5
     \]

   - For each red marble traded, Pete receives 2 blue marbles. So, for 5 red marbles, he will receive:
     \[
     \text{Blue marbles received} = 5 \times 2 = 10
     \]

   - Before the trade, Pete had 10 marbles (4 blue and 6 red).
   - After trading, he will have:
     - Initially, he has 4 blue marbles.
     - After the trade, he receives 10 more blue marbles:
     \[
     \text{Total blue marbles after trade} = 4 + 10 = 14
     \]
   - He is keeping 1 red marble, and since he started with 6 red marbles and gave away 5, he has:
     \[
     \text{Total red marbles after trade} = 1
     \]
   - Therefore, the total number of marbles Pete has after the trade is:
     \[
     \text{Total marbles} = \text{Total blue marbles} + \text{Total red marbles} = 14 + 1 = 15
