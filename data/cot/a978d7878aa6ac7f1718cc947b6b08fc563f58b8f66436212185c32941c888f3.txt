<reasoning>
To find how many black cars are in the parking lot, we need to first determine how many cars are blue and red, and then subtract those from the total number of cars.

1. **Calculate the number of blue cars:**
   We know that one-third of the cars are blue.
   \[
   \text{Number of blue cars} = \frac{1}{3} \times 516
   \]
   Calculating that gives:
   \[
   \frac{516}{3} = 172
   \]
   So, there are 172 blue cars.

2. **Calculate the number of red cars:**
   Next, we find out how many cars are red. We know that one-half of the cars are red.
   \[
   \text{Number of red cars} = \frac{1}{2} \times 516
   \]
   Calculating that gives:
   \[
   \frac{516}{2} = 258
   \]
   So, there are 258 red cars.

3. **Calculate the total number of blue and red cars:**
   Now, we add the number of blue and red cars together:
   \[
   \text{Total of blue and red cars} = 172 + 258 = 430
   \]

4. **Calculate the number of black cars:**
   To find the number of black cars, we subtract the total number of blue and red cars from the total number of cars in the lot:
   \[
   \text{Number of black cars} = 516 - 430
   \]
   Calculating that gives:
   \[
   516 - 430 = 86
   \]
   Thus, there are 86 black cars in the parking lot.

Therefore, the number of black cars is:
</reasoning>
<answer>86</answer>
