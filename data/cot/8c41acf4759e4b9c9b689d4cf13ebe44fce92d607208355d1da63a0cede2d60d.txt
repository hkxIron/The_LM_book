To find out how much money Sandy earned in total over the three days, we need to calculate her earnings for each day separately and then sum them up.

1. **Calculate earnings for Friday:**

   - To find out her earnings for Friday, we multiply the number of hours worked by her hourly rate:
     \[
     \text{Earnings on Friday} = 10 \, \text{hours} \times 15 \, \text{dollars/hour} = 150 \, \text{dollars}
     \]

2. **Calculate earnings for Saturday:**
   - Sandy worked for 6 hours on Saturday.

     \[
     \text{Earnings on Saturday} = 6 \, \text{hours} \times 15 \, \text{dollars/hour} = 90 \, \text{dollars}
     \]

3. **Calculate earnings for Sunday:**
   - Sandy worked for 14 hours on Sunday.

     \[
     \text{Earnings on Sunday} = 14 \, \text{hours} \times 15 \, \text{dollars/hour} = 210 \, \text{dollars}
     \]

4. **Sum up all the earnings:**
   - Now, we need to add the earnings from all three days to find the total amount Sandy earned:
     \[
     \text{Total Earnings} = \text{Earnings on Friday} + \text{Earnings on Saturday} + \text{Earnings on Sunday}
     \]
     \[
     \text{Total Earnings} = 150 \, \text{dollars} + 90 \, \text{dollars} + 210 \, \text{dollars} = 450 \, \text{dollars}
     \]
