   - For 6 hours of babysitting, we multiply the hourly rate by the number of hours:
     \[
     \text{Current babysitter cost} = 16 \text{ dollars/hour} \times 6 \text{ hours} = 96 \text{ dollars}
     \]

     \[
     \text{New babysitter cost (base)} = 12 \text{ dollars/hour} \times 6 \text{ hours} = 72 \text{ dollars}
     \]

     \[
     \text{Extra charges} = 3 \text{ dollars} \times 2 \text{ screams} = 6 \text{ dollars}
     \]
   - Now we add the extra charges to the base cost of the new babysitter:
     \[
     \text{Total cost for new babysitter} = 72 \text{ dollars} + 6 \text{ dollars} = 78 \text{ dollars}
     \]

3. **Calculate the difference in cost between the two babysitters.**
   - We subtract the total cost of the new babysitter from the total cost of the current babysitter:
     \[
     \text{Difference} = 96 \text{ dollars} - 78 \text{ dollars} = 18 \text{ dollars}
     \]
