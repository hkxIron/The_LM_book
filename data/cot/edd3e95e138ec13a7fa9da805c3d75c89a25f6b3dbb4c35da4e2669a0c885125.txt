To determine how much fencing Bob will need to enclose his garden, we first need to calculate the perimeter of the rectangular plot. The formula for the perimeter \( P \) of a rectangle is given by:

\[
P = 2 \times (length + width)
\]

1. **Calculate the perimeter of the garden:**
   - Length = 225 feet
   - Width = 125 feet

Substituting the values into the perimeter formula:

\[
P = 2 \times (225 + 125)
\]

Calculating the sum inside the parentheses first:

\[
225 + 125 = 350
\]

Now, we multiply by 2:

\[
P = 2 \times 350 = 700 \text{ feet}
\]

   - Bob has a 3-foot wide gate and a 10-foot wide gate. We need to subtract the width of these gates from the total perimeter to find out how much actual fencing is needed.

First, we calculate the total width of the gates:

\[
\text{Total gate width} = 3 + 10 = 13 \text{ feet}
\]

3. **Calculate the total amount of fencing needed:**
Now we subtract the total width of the gates from the perimeter:

\[
\text{Fencing needed} = P - \text{Total gate width}
\]
\[
\text{Fencing needed} = 700 - 13 = 687 \text{ feet}
\]

Therefore, the amount of fencing Bob will need to use is 687 feet.
