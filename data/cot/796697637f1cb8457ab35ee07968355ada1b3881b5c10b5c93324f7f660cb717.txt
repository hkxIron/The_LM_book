To find out how long it will take farmer Steven to plow 55 acres of farmland and mow 30 acres of grassland, we need to break the problem down into two parts: the time spent plowing and the time spent mowing.

1. **Calculate Time to Plow 55 Acres of Farmland:**
   - Farmer Steven can plow 10 acres per day.
   - To find out how many days it will take to plow 55 acres, we divide the total acres by the number of acres he can plow in a day:
     \[
     \text{Time to plow} = \frac{55 \text{ acres}}{10 \text{ acres/day}} = 5.5 \text{ days}
     \]
   - This means it will take him 5.5 days to plow all the farmland.

2. **Calculate Time to Mow 30 Acres of Grassland:**
   - Farmer Steven can mow 12 acres per day.
   - To find out how many days it will take to mow 30 acres, we divide the total acres by the number of acres he can mow in a day:
     \[
     \text{Time to mow} = \frac{30 \text{ acres}}{12 \text{ acres/day}} = 2.5 \text{ days}
     \]
   - This means it will take him 2.5 days to mow all the grassland.

3. **Calculate Total Time:**
   - Now, we need to add the time spent plowing and the time spent mowing:
     \[
     \text{Total time} = \text{Time to plow} + \text{Time to mow} = 5.5 \text{ days} + 2.5 \text{ days} = 8 \text{ days}
     \]

Therefore, it will take farmer Steven a total of 8 days to complete both tasks.
