To find out how much more Gary is spending per month by paying his dad back in 2 years instead of 5 years, we need to determine the monthly payments for both scenarios.

1. **Calculate the monthly payment for 5 years:**

   - Total loan amount = $6,000
   - Number of months in 5 years = 5 years × 12 months/year = 60 months
   
   Now, we divide the total amount by the number of months to find the monthly payment:

   \[
   \text{Monthly payment for 5 years} = \frac{6000}{60} = 100
   \]

2. **Calculate the monthly payment for 2 years:**

   - Total loan amount = $6,000
   - Number of months in 2 years = 2 years × 12 months/year = 24 months
   
   Again, we divide the total amount by the number of months:

   \[
   \text{Monthly payment for 2 years} = \frac{6000}{24} = 250
   \]

3. **Calculate the difference in monthly payments:**

   Now, we find out how much more Gary is paying each month by subtracting the monthly payment for 5 years from the monthly payment for 2 years:

   \[
   \text{Difference} = 250 - 100 = 150
   \]

Therefore, Gary is spending $150 more per month to pay off the loan in 2 years instead of 5 years.
