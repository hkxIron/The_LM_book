   \[
   \text{Number of cats} = \frac{2}{3} \times 300
   \]

   \[
   \text{Number of cats} = 200
   \]

2. **Determine the number of dogs:**
   To find the number of dogs, we subtract the number of cats from the total number of animals.

   \[
   \text{Number of dogs} = 300 - \text{Number of cats}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Number of dogs} = 300 - 200 = 100
   \]

   Therefore, there are 100 dogs on the street.

3. **Calculate the total number of legs of dogs:**
   Each dog has 4 legs. We can calculate the total number of legs of the dogs by multiplying the number of dogs by the number of legs per dog.

   \[
   \text{Total legs of dogs} = \text{Number of dogs} \times 4
   \]
   \[
   \text{Total legs of dogs} = 100 \times 4 = 400
   \]

Thus, the total number of legs of the dogs on the street is 400.
