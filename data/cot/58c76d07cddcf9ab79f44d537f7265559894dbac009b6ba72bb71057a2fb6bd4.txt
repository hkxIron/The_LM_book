<reasoning>
To find out how much money Miranda saved per month, we need to first determine how much she saved in total before receiving the $50 from her sister.

1. **Identify the total cost of the heels**: Miranda paid a total of $260 for the heels.

2. **Subtract the amount given by her sister**: Since her sister gave her $50, we need to subtract this from the total cost of the heels to find out how much Miranda saved by herself.

   \[
   \text{Amount saved by Miranda} = \text{Total cost} - \text{Amount from sister}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Amount saved by Miranda} = 260 - 50 = 210
   \]

3. **Calculate the amount saved per month**: Miranda saved this amount over 3 months, so we divide the total amount she saved by the number of months.

   \[
   \text{Amount saved per month} = \frac{\text{Amount saved by Miranda}}{\text{Number of months}}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Amount saved per month} = \frac{210}{3} = 70
   \]

Therefore, Miranda saved $70 each month.
</reasoning>
