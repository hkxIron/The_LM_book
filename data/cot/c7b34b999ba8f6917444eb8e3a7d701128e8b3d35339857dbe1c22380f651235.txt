To calculate Irene's total income for the week, we will break it down into two parts: her regular income for the first 40 hours and her income from overtime hours.

1. **Calculate Regular Income:**
   Irene earns $500 for working 40 hours a week. This means that for the first 40 hours, her income is simply $500. 

   So, 
   \[
   \text{Regular Income} = 500
   \]

2. **Calculate Overtime Hours:**
   Last week, Irene worked a total of 50 hours. To find her overtime hours, we subtract her regular hours from the total hours worked:
   \[
   \text{Overtime Hours} = 50 - 40 = 10 \text{ hours}
   \]

3. **Calculate Overtime Income:**
   For every hour of overtime, Irene earns an additional $20. Therefore, we can calculate her total overtime income:
   \[
   \text{Overtime Income} = \text{Overtime Hours} \times \text{Overtime Rate}
   \]
   Substituting the values we found:
   \[
   \text{Overtime Income} = 10 \text{ hours} \times 20 = 200
   \]

4. **Calculate Total Income:**
   Now, we add her regular income and her overtime income to find her total income for the week:
   \[
   \text{Total Income} = \text{Regular Income} + \text{Overtime Income}
   \]
   Substituting the values:
   \[
   \text{Total Income} = 500 + 200 = 700
   \]

Thus, Irene's total income for the week is $700.
