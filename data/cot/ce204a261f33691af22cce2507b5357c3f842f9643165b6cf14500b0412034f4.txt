<reasoning>
To find out how much faster Mac will run a 24-mile race compared to Apple, we need to calculate the time each runner takes to complete the race.

1. **Calculate Apple's time to complete the race:**
   - Apple runs at a speed of 3 miles per hour. 
   - To find the time it takes to run 24 miles, we use the formula: 
     \[
     \text{Time} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Speed}}
     \]
   - For Apple:
     \[
     \text{Time}_{\text{Apple}} = \frac{24 \text{ miles}}{3 \text{ miles per hour}} = 8 \text{ hours}
     \]

2. **Calculate Mac's time to complete the race:**
   - Mac runs at a speed of 4 miles per hour.
   - Again using the same formula:
     \[
     \text{Time}_{\text{Mac}} = \frac{24 \text{ miles}}{4 \text{ miles per hour}} = 6 \text{ hours}
     \]

3. **Determine the difference in time:**
   - Now we subtract the time taken by Mac from the time taken by Apple to find out how much faster Mac is:
     \[
     \text{Difference in time} = \text{Time}_{\text{Apple}} - \text{Time}_{\text{Mac}} = 8 \text{ hours} - 6 \text{ hours} = 2 \text{ hours}
     \]

4. **Convert the time difference from hours to minutes:**
   - Since we need the answer in minutes, we multiply the difference in hours by 60 (since there are 60 minutes in an hour):
     \[
     \text{Difference in time (minutes)} = 2 \text{ hours} \times 60 \text{ minutes/hour} = 120 \text{ minutes}
     \]

Therefore, Mac runs the 24-mile race 120 minutes faster than Apple.
