<reasoning>
To find out how much Jim paid for the package of 20 car washes, we will first calculate the total cost of the car washes without the discount and then apply the discount percentage.

1. **Calculate the total cost without the discount:**
   - A single car wash costs $15.
   - Jim bought a package of 20 car washes.
   - Therefore, the total cost without the discount is calculated as follows:
   
   \[
   \text{Total cost without discount} = \text{Number of car washes} \times \text{Cost per car wash} = 20 \times 15
   \]
   - Multiplying these values gives us:
   
   \[
   20 \times 15 = 300
   \]

2. **Apply the discount:**
   - Jim only has to pay 60% of the total cost because he bought a package.
   - To find out how much he actually pays, we calculate 60% of $300.
   - To do this, we multiply the total cost by 0.60 (which is the decimal representation of 60%):
   
   \[
   \text{Amount paid} = 300 \times 0.60
   \]
   - Performing the multiplication gives us:
   
   \[
   300 \times 0.60 = 180
   \]

Therefore, Jim paid $180 for the package of 20 car washes.
</reasoning>
<answer>180</answer>
