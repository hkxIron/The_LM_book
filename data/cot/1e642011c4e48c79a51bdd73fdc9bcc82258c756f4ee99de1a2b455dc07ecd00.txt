     \[
     30\% \text{ of } 10 = 0.30 \times 10 = 3
     \]

     \[
     10 + 3 = 13
     \]

2. **Calculate the total number of family members**:
   - Now we add the number of members from both sides:
     \[
     10 \text{ (father's side)} + 13 \text{ (mother's side)} = 23
     \]
