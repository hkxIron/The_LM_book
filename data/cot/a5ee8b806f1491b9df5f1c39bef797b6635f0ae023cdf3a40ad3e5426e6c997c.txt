To solve the problem, we need to determine the total amount of fertilizer needed for Janet's 20 acres of farmland and how much fertilizer she produces daily with her 80 horses. Then we will calculate how long it will take for her to spread that fertilizer over her fields.

### Step 1: Calculate total fertilizer needed
Each acre requires 400 gallons of fertilizer, and Janet has 20 acres. We can find the total fertilizer needed by multiplying the number of acres by the amount needed per acre.

\[
\text{Total fertilizer needed} = \text{Number of acres} \times \text{Fertilizer per acre}
\]
\[
\text{Total fertilizer needed} = 20 \text{ acres} \times 400 \text{ gallons/acre} = 8000 \text{ gallons}
\]

### Step 2: Calculate daily fertilizer production
Each horse produces 5 gallons of fertilizer per day, and Janet has 80 horses. We can calculate the total amount of fertilizer produced daily.

\[
\text{Daily fertilizer production} = \text{Number of horses} \times \text{Fertilizer per horse}
\]
\[
\text{Daily fertilizer production} = 80 \text{ horses} \times 5 \text{ gallons/horse} = 400 \text{ gallons/day}
\]

### Step 3: Calculate the number of days to collect enough fertilizer
Now that we know the total amount of fertilizer needed (8000 gallons) and the daily production (400 gallons), we can find out how many days it will take to collect enough fertilizer.

\[
\text{Days to collect fertilizer} = \frac{\text{Total fertilizer needed}}{\text{Daily fertilizer production}}
\]
\[
\text{Days to collect fertilizer} = \frac{8000 \text{ gallons}}{400 \text{ gallons/day}} = 20 \text{ days}
\]

### Step 4: Calculate how many days it takes to spread the fertilizer
Janet can spread fertilizer over 4 acres per day. Since she has 20 acres total, we can calculate how many days it will take to spread the fertilizer once she has collected it.

\[
\text{Days to spread fertilizer} = \frac{\text{Number of acres}}{\text{Acres spread per day}}
\]
\[
\text{Days to spread fertilizer} = \frac{20 \text{ acres}}{4 \text{ acres/day}} = 5 \text{ days}
\]

### Step 5: Calculate total time until all fields are fertilized
Finally, we add the days required to collect the fertilizer and the days required to spread it.

\[
\text{Total time} = \text{Days to collect fertilizer} + \text{Days to spread fertilizer}
\]
\[
\text{Total time} = 20 \text{ days} + 5 \text{ days} = 25 \text{ days}
\]

Therefore, it will take Janet a total of 25 days until all her fields are fertilized.
