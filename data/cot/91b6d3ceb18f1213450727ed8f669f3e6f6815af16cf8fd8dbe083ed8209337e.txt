To determine how many quarters are needed to buy the specified items, I will first calculate the total cost of all the items in cents, and then convert that total into the number of quarters.

1. **Calculate the cost of three candy bars:**
   Each candy bar costs ¢25. Therefore, for three candy bars:
   \[
   3 \text{ candy bars} \times 25 \text{ cents/candy bar} = 75 \text{ cents}
   \]

   Each piece of chocolate costs ¢75. Therefore, for two pieces of chocolate:
   \[
   2 \text{ pieces} \times 75 \text{ cents/piece} = 150 \text{ cents}
   \]

3. **Calculate the cost of one pack of juice:**
   Each pack of juice costs ¢50. Therefore, for one pack of juice:
   \[
   1 \text{ pack} \times 50 \text{ cents/pack} = 50 \text{ cents}
   \]

4. **Add all the costs together:**
   Now, I will sum the costs of all the items:
   \[
   \text{Total cost} = 75 \text{ cents} + 150 \text{ cents} + 50 \text{ cents}
   \]
   \[
   = 275 \text{ cents}
   \]

5. **Convert the total cost in cents to quarters:**
   Since one quarter is worth ¢25, to find out how many quarters are needed, I divide the total cost by 25:
   \[
   \text{Number of quarters} = \frac{275 \text{ cents}}{25 \text{ cents/quarter}} = 11 \text{ quarters}
   \]
