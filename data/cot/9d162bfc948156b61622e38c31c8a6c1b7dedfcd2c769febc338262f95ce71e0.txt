   - Each person eats 3 meals a day.
   - Over 4 days, the total meals per person is:
     \[
     3 \text{ meals/day} \times 4 \text{ days} = 12 \text{ meals/person}
     \]

3. **Calculate the total number of meals for all people**:
   - Since there are 6 people, the total number of meals for everyone is:
     \[
     6 \text{ people} \times 12 \text{ meals/person} = 72 \text{ total meals}
     \]

4. **Calculate the total number of plates used**:
   - Each person uses 2 plates per meal.
   - Therefore, the total number of plates used is:
     \[
     72 \text{ meals} \times 2 \text{ plates/meal} = 144 \text{ plates}
     \]
