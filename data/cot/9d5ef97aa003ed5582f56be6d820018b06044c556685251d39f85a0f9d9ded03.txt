   \[
   \text{Hannah's current age} = 6 + 20 = 26 \text{ years old.}
   \]
   - July's current age:
   \[
   \text{July's current age} = 3 + 20 = 23 \text{ years old.}
   \]

   The problem states that July's husband is 2 years older than her. Therefore, we can calculate her husband's age as follows:
   \[
   \text{Husband's age} = \text{July's current age} + 2 = 23 + 2 = 25 \text{ years old.}
