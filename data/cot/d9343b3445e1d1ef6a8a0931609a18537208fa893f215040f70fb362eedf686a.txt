   - Mary is 1 adult.
   - She has 3 children.

   - The cost of an adult ticket is $2.
   - Cost for Mary = 1 adult ticket × $2 = $2.

3. **Calculate the cost of the children's tickets:**
   - The cost of a children's ticket is $1.
   - Cost for 3 children = 3 children × $1 = $3.

4. **Calculate the total cost of all tickets:**
   - Total cost = Cost of adult ticket + Cost of children's tickets.
   - Total cost = $2 + $3 = $5.

5. **Calculate the change from the $20 bill:**
   - Change = Amount paid - Total cost.
   - Change = $20 - $5 = $15.

Therefore, Mary will receive $15 in change after purchasing the tickets.
