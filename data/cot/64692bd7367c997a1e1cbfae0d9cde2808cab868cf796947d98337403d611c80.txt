To determine how much money Norris has saved in total and how it compares to what Hugo spent, we will first calculate the total amount Norris saved over the three months.

1. **Calculate Norris's total savings:**
   - In September, Norris saved $29.
   - In October, he saved $25.
   - In November, he saved $31.

   We add these amounts together to find his total savings:
   \[
   \text{Total savings} = 29 + 25 + 31
   \]

   Let's perform the addition step-by-step:
   - First, add September and October savings:
     \[
     29 + 25 = 54
     \]
   - Next, add November savings to the previous total:
     \[
     54 + 31 = 85
     \]

   Therefore, Norris's total savings over the three months is $85.

   - Hugo spent $75 on an online game. We need to find out how much money Norris has left after considering this expense.

3. **Calculate Norris's remaining money:**
   We subtract the amount Hugo spent from Norris's total savings:
   \[
   \text{Remaining money} = \text{Total savings} - \text{Hugo's spending}
   \]
   Substituting the values we found:
   \[
   \text{Remaining money} = 85 - 75
   \]

   Now perform the subtraction:
   \[
   85 - 75 = 10
   \]

Thus, after Hugo's spending, Norris has $10 left.
