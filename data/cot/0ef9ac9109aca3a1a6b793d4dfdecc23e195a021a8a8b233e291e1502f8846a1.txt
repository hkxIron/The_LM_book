To determine how many bookshelves Jonas can fit in his library, we will follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total space available for bookshelves**: 
   The total area of the room is 400 square feet. Jonas is reserving 160 square feet for a desk and walking space. Therefore, we need to subtract this reserved space from the total room area.

   \[
   \text{Available space for bookshelves} = \text{Total room space} - \text{Reserved space}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Available space for bookshelves} = 400 \text{ square feet} - 160 \text{ square feet} = 240 \text{ square feet}
   \]

   Each bookshelf occupies 80 square feet. To find out how many bookshelves can fit, we divide the available space by the space each bookshelf takes.

   \[
   \text{Number of bookshelves} = \frac{\text{Available space for bookshelves}}{\text{Space per bookshelf}}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Number of bookshelves} = \frac{240 \text{ square feet}}{80 \text{ square feet}} = 3
   \]

3. **Conclusion**: 
   After performing the calculations, we find that Jonas can fit 3 bookshelves in the remaining space of his library.
