   - Here, Speed = 30 miles per hour and Time = 3 hours.
   - Distance = 30 miles/hour × 3 hours = 90 miles.

2. **Calculate the distance traveled at 25 miles per hour for 4 hours:**
   - Again using Distance = Speed × Time,
   - Speed = 25 miles per hour and Time = 4 hours.
   - Distance = 25 miles/hour × 4 hours = 100 miles.
   
   Therefore, the driver travels 100 miles at 25 miles per hour.

3. **Calculate the total distance traveled in one day:**
   - Total Distance = Distance at 30 mph + Distance at 25 mph
   - Total Distance = 90 miles + 100 miles = 190 miles.
   
   Thus, the driver travels 190 miles in one day.

4. **Calculate the total distance traveled in a week (Monday to Saturday):**
   - The driver works for 6 days a week.
   - Total weekly distance = Distance per day × Number of days
   - Total weekly distance = 190 miles/day × 6 days = 1140 miles.
   
   Therefore, the driver travels a total of 1140 miles in a week.
