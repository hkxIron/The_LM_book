1. **Determine the initial number of crayons Mary has**:

     \[
     \text{Total initial crayons} = \text{Number of green crayons} + \text{Number of blue crayons} = 5 + 8 = 13
     \]

   - Mary gives out 3 green crayons and 1 blue crayon.
   - To find the total number of crayons given to Becky, add the number of green and blue crayons she gives:
     \[
     \text{Total crayons given} = \text{Green crayons given} + \text{Blue crayons given} = 3 + 1 = 4
     \]

3. **Calculate how many crayons Mary has left**:
   - To find the remaining crayons, subtract the number of crayons given from the total initial number of crayons:
     \[
     \text{Crayons left} = \text{Total initial crayons} - \text{Total crayons given} = 13 - 4 = 9
     \]

Therefore, after giving away the crayons, Mary has 9 crayons left.
