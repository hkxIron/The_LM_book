<reasoning>
To find out how much the fisherman earns every day, we need to calculate the total earnings from both the Red snappers and the Tunas he catches.

1. **Calculate the earnings from Red snappers:**
   - The fisherman catches 8 Red snappers.
   - Each Red snapper costs $3.
   - To find the total earnings from the Red snappers, we multiply the number of Red snappers by the cost of each:
     \[
     \text{Earnings from Red snappers} = 8 \text{ Red snappers} \times 3 \text{ dollars/Red snapper} = 24 \text{ dollars}
     \]

2. **Calculate the earnings from Tunas:**
   - The fisherman catches 14 Tunas.
   - Each Tuna costs $2.
   - To find the total earnings from the Tunas, we multiply the number of Tunas by the cost of each:
     \[
     \text{Earnings from Tunas} = 14 \text{ Tunas} \times 2 \text{ dollars/Tuna} = 28 \text{ dollars}
     \]

3. **Calculate the total earnings:**
   - Now we add the earnings from the Red snappers and the Tunas to find the total earnings for the day:
     \[
     \text{Total Earnings} = \text{Earnings from Red snappers} + \text{Earnings from Tunas} = 24 \text{ dollars} + 28 \text{ dollars} = 52 \text{ dollars}
     \]

Therefore, the fisherman earns a total of $52 every day.
