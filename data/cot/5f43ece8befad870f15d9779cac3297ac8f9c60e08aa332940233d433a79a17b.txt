   - Distance = Speed × Time
   - Distance = 3 mph × 2 hours = 6 miles

   Now, we know Diana has 4 miles left to bike.

   - Speed = 1 mph
   - Distance to cover = 4 miles
   - Time = Distance ÷ Speed
   - Time = 4 miles ÷ 1 mph = 4 hours

   - Time spent biking at 3 mph = 2 hours
   - Time spent biking at 1 mph = 4 hours
   - Total time = Time at 3 mph + Time at 1 mph
   - Total time = 2 hours + 4 hours = 6 hours

Therefore, it will take Diana a total of 6 hours to get home.
