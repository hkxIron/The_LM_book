To find out how much Mark will spend on visits to the theater in 6 weeks, we need to follow these steps:

   \[
   \text{Cost for one visit} = \text{Hours} \times \text{Price per hour} = 3 \text{ hours} \times 5 \text{ dollars/hour} = 15 \text{ dollars}
   \]

4. **Calculate the total cost for 6 visits**: Now we multiply the cost of one visit by the number of visits:
   \[
   \text{Total cost} = \text{Cost per visit} \times \text{Number of visits} = 15 \text{ dollars} \times 6 = 90 \text{ dollars}
   \]
