   \[
   20 \text{ cents} = 0.20 \text{ dollars}
   \]

   \[
   \text{Total sales} = \text{Number of eggs sold} \times \text{Price per egg}
   \]
   We want the total sales to equal $5:
   \[
   5 = x \times 0.20
   \]
   where \( x \) is the number of eggs sold.

4. **Solve for \( x \):** Rearranging the equation to find \( x \):
   \[
   x = \frac{5}{0.20}
   \]
   Now, calculate:
   \[
   x = \frac{5}{0.20} = 5 \div 0.20 = 5 \times 5 = 25
   \]
   Therefore, she needs to sell 25 eggs to recover her $5.

5. **Calculate how many eggs are left:** Since Samantha started with 30 eggs and sold 25, we need to find out how many are remaining:
   \[
   \text{Eggs left} = 30 - 25 = 5
   \]

Thus, after selling enough eggs to recover her capital, Samantha will have 5 eggs left.
