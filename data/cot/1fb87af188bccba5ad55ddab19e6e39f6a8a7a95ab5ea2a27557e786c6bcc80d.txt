     \[

     \[
     \text{Food left} = 27000 \text{ person-days} - 9000 \text{ person-days} = 18000 \text{ person-days}

     \[
     \text{Remaining people} = 300 \text{ people} - 100 \text{ people} = 200 \text{ people}
     \]

     \[
     \text{Days left} = \frac{\text{Food left}}{\text{Remaining people}} = \frac{18000 \text{ person-days}}{200 \text{ people}} = 90 \text{ days}
