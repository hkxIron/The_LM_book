\[
\text{Total candies} = C + G + A = C + 3C + 6C = 10C

\[
\text{Total cost} = \text{Total candies} \times \text{Cost per candy}

200 = (10C) \times 2.5

\[

\[
C = \frac{200}{25} = 8

\[
G = 3C = 3 \times 8 = 24
