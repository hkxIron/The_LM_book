     \text{Cost of dried fruits} = 2.5 \, \text{kg} \times 8 \, \text{USD/kg} = 20 \, \text{USD}
     \]

   - Now, we add the total cost of the nuts and the total cost of the dried fruits to find the overall cost:
     \[
     \text{Total cost} = \text{Cost of nuts} + \text{Cost of dried fruits}
     \]
