   \[
   \text{Number of rooms} = \frac{\text{Total time to vacuum the house}}{\text{Time to vacuum each room}} = \frac{120 \text{ minutes}}{20 \text{ minutes/room}}
   \]

3. Now, we perform the division:
   \[
   \frac{120}{20} = 6
   \]
   This calculation shows that Susie has 6 rooms in her house.
