<reasoning>
To determine how much money Karen would save over 30 years by buying the more expensive coat, we will calculate the total cost of each coat over that time period.

1. **Calculate the total cost of the $300 coat:**
   - The $300 coat lasts for 15 years, so in 30 years, she will need to buy two of these coats (since 30 years divided by 15 years per coat equals 2).
   - Total cost for the $300 coat over 30 years:
     \[
     2 \text{ coats} \times 300 \text{ dollars/coat} = 600 \text{ dollars}
     \]

2. **Calculate the total cost of the $120 coat:**
   - The $120 coat lasts for 5 years, so in 30 years, she will need to buy six of these coats (since 30 years divided by 5 years per coat equals 6).
   - Total cost for the $120 coat over 30 years:
     \[
     6 \text{ coats} \times 120 \text{ dollars/coat} = 720 \text{ dollars}
     \]

3. **Calculate the difference in cost between the two options:**
   - To find out how much money Karen saves by choosing the more expensive coat, we subtract the total cost of the $300 coat from the total cost of the $120 coat:
     \[
     720 \text{ dollars} - 600 \text{ dollars} = 120 \text{ dollars}
     \]

This means that by choosing the more expensive coat, Karen would actually spend $120 less over the 30 years compared to the cheaper option.

Therefore, the final answer is:
</reasoning>
<answer>120</answer>
