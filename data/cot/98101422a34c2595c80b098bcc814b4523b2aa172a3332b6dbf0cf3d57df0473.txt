<reasoning>
First, we need to calculate the total cost of tickets for the students. We know that each student ticket costs $6 and there are 20 students.

1. **Calculate the total for students:**
   \[
   \text{Total for students} = \text{Number of students} \times \text{Cost per student ticket}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Total for students} = 20 \times 6 = 120
   \]
   This means that the total amount collected from student tickets is $120.

Next, we will calculate the total cost of tickets for the adults. Each adult ticket costs $8 and there are 12 adults.

2. **Calculate the total for adults:**
   \[
   \text{Total for adults} = \text{Number of adults} \times \text{Cost per adult ticket}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Total for adults} = 12 \times 8 = 96
   \]
   Thus, the total amount collected from adult tickets is $96.

Now, to find the total amount of money earned from ticket sales, we need to add the total from students and the total from adults.

3. **Calculate the overall total:**
   \[
   \text{Total revenue} = \text{Total for students} + \text{Total for adults}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Total revenue} = 120 + 96 = 216
   \]

Therefore, the total dollar amount of tickets sold is $216.
</reasoning>
