To find the difference between the number of words Cameron could type in 5 minutes before and after he broke his arm, we will follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the number of words typed before the injury:**

   \[
   \text{Words before} = \text{Typing speed} \times \text{Time} = 10 \text{ words/minute} \times 5 \text{ minutes}
   \]
   Performing the multiplication:
   \[
   \text{Words before} = 50 \text{ words}
   \]

2. **Calculate the number of words typed after the injury:**

   \[
   \text{Words after} = 8 \text{ words/minute} \times 5 \text{ minutes}
   \]
   Performing the multiplication:
   \[
   \text{Words after} = 40 \text{ words}
   \]

3. **Calculate the difference in the number of words typed:**
   Now we find the difference between the number of words typed before and after his injury:
   \[
   \text{Difference} = \text{Words before} - \text{Words after} = 50 \text{ words} - 40 \text{ words}
   \]
   Performing the subtraction:
   \[
   \text{Difference} = 10 \text{ words}
   \]

Thus, the difference between the number of words Cameron could type in 5 minutes before and after he broke his arm is 10 words.
