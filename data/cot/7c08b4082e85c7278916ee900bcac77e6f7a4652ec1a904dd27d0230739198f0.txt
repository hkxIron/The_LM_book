1. **Calculate the number of teachers who ate pizza:**

   - We calculate the number of teachers who ate pizza by multiplying the total number of teachers by 2/3:
     \[
     \text{Teachers who ate pizza} = 30 \times \frac{2}{3} = 20
     \]

2. **Calculate the number of staff members who ate pizza:**
   - The total number of staff members is 45.

   - We calculate the number of staff members who ate pizza by multiplying the total number of staff members by 4/5:
     \[
     \text{Staff members who ate pizza} = 45 \times \frac{4}{5} = 36
     \]
   - This means 36 staff members ate pizza.

3. **Calculate the total number of pizza eaters:**
   - Now we add the number of teachers and staff members who ate pizza:
     \[
     \text{Total pizza eaters} = \text{Teachers who ate pizza} + \text{Staff members who ate pizza} = 20 + 36 = 56
     \]

4. **Calculate the total number of people at the school:**
   - The total number of teachers and staff members at Esme's school is:
     \[
     \text{Total people} = 30 + 45 = 75
     \]

5. **Calculate the number of non-pizza eaters:**
   - We subtract the total number of pizza eaters from the total number of people to find the number of non-pizza eaters:
     \[
     \text{Non-pizza eaters} = \text{Total people} - \text{Total pizza eaters} = 75 - 56 = 19
     \]
