<reasoning>
To find the total number of legs among the animals, we need to determine how many legs each type of animal has and then sum them up.

1. **Determine the number of legs for chickens:**
   Chickens typically have 2 legs. Since Farmer Brown fed 7 chickens, we can calculate the total number of legs from the chickens:
   \[
   \text{Legs from chickens} = \text{Number of chickens} \times \text{Legs per chicken} = 7 \times 2 = 14
   \]

2. **Determine the number of legs for sheep:**
   Sheep typically have 4 legs. Since Farmer Brown fed 5 sheep, we can calculate the total number of legs from the sheep:
   \[
   \text{Legs from sheep} = \text{Number of sheep} \times \text{Legs per sheep} = 5 \times 4 = 20
   \]

3. **Calculate the total number of legs:**
   Now, we will add the legs from the chickens and the legs from the sheep to find the total number of legs:
   \[
   \text{Total legs} = \text{Legs from chickens} + \text{Legs from sheep} = 14 + 20 = 34
   \]

So, the total number of legs among the animals Farmer Brown fed is 34.
</reasoning>
<answer>34</answer>
