To find out how many adults are on the bus, we need to first determine how many children are on the bus. 

1. **Calculate the number of children:**
   - We know that children make up 25% of the total passengers.
   - The total number of passengers on the bus is 60.
   - To find the number of children, we multiply the total number of passengers by the percentage of children in decimal form:
     \[
     \text{Number of children} = 60 \times 0.25
     \]

2. **Perform the multiplication:**
   - Calculating this gives:
     \[
     60 \times 0.25 = 15
     \]
   - Thus, there are 15 children on the bus.

3. **Calculate the number of adults:**
   - Now that we know there are 15 children, we can find the number of adults by subtracting the number of children from the total number of passengers:
     \[
     \text{Number of adults} = 60 - 15
     \]

4. **Perform the subtraction:**
   - Calculating this gives:
     \[
     60 - 15 = 45
     \]
   - Therefore, there are 45 adults on the bus.

In conclusion, after determining the number of children and subtracting that from the total number of passengers, we find that there are 45 adults on the bus.
