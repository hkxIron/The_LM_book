     \text{Total pages in 5 days} = 10 \text{ hours} \times 50 \text{ pages/hour} = 500 \text{ pages}

     \[

     \]

   - Now, we can find out how many pages he reads in 7 days:
     \[
     \text{Total pages in 7 days} = \text{Pages per day} \times 7 \text{ days}
     \]
     \[
     \text{Total pages in 7 days} = 100 \text{ pages/day} \times 7 \text{ days} = 700 \text{ pages}
     \]
