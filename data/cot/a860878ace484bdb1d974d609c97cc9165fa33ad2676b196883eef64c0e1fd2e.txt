To find out how much a customer will pay for 3 pairs of jeans with a 10% discount on every two pairs, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Determine the original price for 3 pairs of jeans**:
   Each pair of jeans costs $40. Therefore, the total price for 3 pairs without any discount is:
   \[
   3 \text{ pairs} \times 40 \text{ dollars/pair} = 120 \text{ dollars}
   \]

2. **Calculate the discount on the first two pairs**:
   A 10% discount is applied to the first two pairs. The cost of two pairs is:
   \[
   2 \text{ pairs} \times 40 \text{ dollars/pair} = 80 \text{ dollars}
   \]
   Now, we calculate the 10% discount on this amount:
   \[
   10\% \text{ of } 80 \text{ dollars} = 0.10 \times 80 = 8 \text{ dollars}
   \]
   Therefore, the price after the discount for the first two pairs is:
   \[
   80 \text{ dollars} - 8 \text{ dollars} = 72 \text{ dollars}
   \]

3. **Add the cost of the third pair without a discount**:
   The third pair does not receive any discount, so it still costs $40. We add this to the discounted price of the first two pairs:
   \[
   72 \text{ dollars} + 40 \text{ dollars} = 112 \text{ dollars}
   \]

4. **Final calculation**:
   The total amount the customer has to pay for 3 pairs of jeans, considering the discount on the first two pairs, is $112.

Therefore, the total amount the customer will pay for 3 pairs of jeans is:
</reasoning>
<answer>112</answer>
