To find the radius of the ash cloud, we begin by determining the diameter of the ash cloud based on the height of the eruption.

1. We know that the ash cloud's diameter is eighteen times the height the ash erupted into the sky. The height of the eruption is given as 300 feet.
   
   So, we calculate the diameter:
   \[
   \text{Diameter} = 18 \times \text{height} = 18 \times 300 \text{ feet}
   \]
   Now, performing the multiplication:
   \[
   18 \times 300 = 5400 \text{ feet}
   \]
   This means that the diameter of the ash cloud is 5400 feet.

2. Next, we need to find the radius of the ash cloud. The radius is half of the diameter. Therefore, we calculate the radius as follows:
   \[
   \text{Radius} = \frac{\text{Diameter}}{2} = \frac{5400 \text{ feet}}{2}
   \]
   Again, we perform the division:
   \[
   \frac{5400}{2} = 2700 \text{ feet}
   \]

Thus, the radius of the ash cloud is 2700 feet.
