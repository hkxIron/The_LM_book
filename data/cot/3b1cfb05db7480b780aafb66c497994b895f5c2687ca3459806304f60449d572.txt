<reasoning>
To find out how many hours Tod drove, we first need to determine the total distance he traveled. 

1. **Calculate the total distance:**
   - Tod drives 55 miles north and 95 miles west.
   - We add these two distances together: 
   \[
   \text{Total Distance} = 55 \text{ miles} + 95 \text{ miles} = 150 \text{ miles}
   \]

2. **Determine the speed:**
   - Tod drives at a constant speed of 25 miles per hour.

3. **Calculate the time taken to drive the total distance:**
   - We can use the formula for time, which is:
   \[
   \text{Time} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Speed}}
   \]
   - Plugging in the values:
   \[
   \text{Time} = \frac{150 \text{ miles}}{25 \text{ miles/hour}} = 6 \text{ hours}
   \]

Therefore, Tod drove for 6 hours in total.
</reasoning>
