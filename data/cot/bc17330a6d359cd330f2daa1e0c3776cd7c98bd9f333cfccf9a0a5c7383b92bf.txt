<reasoning>
To find out how much more money Bea earned than Dawn, we will first calculate the total earnings for each of them and then find the difference.

1. **Calculate Bea's earnings:**
   - Bea sells her lemonade for 25 cents per glass.
   - She sold 10 glasses.
   - To find Bea's total earnings, we multiply the price per glass by the number of glasses sold:
     \[
     \text{Bea's earnings} = 25 \text{ cents/glass} \times 10 \text{ glasses} = 250 \text{ cents}
     \]

2. **Calculate Dawn's earnings:**
   - Dawn sells her lemonade for 28 cents per glass.
   - She sold 8 glasses.
   - To find Dawn's total earnings, we multiply the price per glass by the number of glasses sold:
     \[
     \text{Dawn's earnings} = 28 \text{ cents/glass} \times 8 \text{ glasses} = 224 \text{ cents}
     \]

3. **Find the difference in earnings between Bea and Dawn:**
   - To find out how much more money Bea earned than Dawn, we subtract Dawn's earnings from Bea's earnings:
     \[
     \text{Difference} = \text{Bea's earnings} - \text{Dawn's earnings} = 250 \text{ cents} - 224 \text{ cents} = 26 \text{ cents}
     \]

Therefore, Bea earned 26 cents more than Dawn.
</reasoning>
