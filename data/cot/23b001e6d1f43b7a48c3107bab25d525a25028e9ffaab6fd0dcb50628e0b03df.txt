   \]
   This step gives us the total amount of money Azalea earns from selling the wool.

   Azalea paid the shearer $2000 for his work. This is the only cost we need to consider in this scenario, as no other costs are mentioned.
   \[
   \text{Total costs} = 2000 \text{ dollars}
   \]

4. **Calculate the profit**:
   Profit is calculated by subtracting the total costs from the total revenue.
   \[
   \text{Profit} = \text{Total revenue} - \text{Total costs} = 40000 \text{ dollars} - 2000 \text{ dollars} = 38000 \text{ dollars}
   \]
