     \[
     \text{Total candy for Roger} = 11 \text{ pieces} + 3 \text{ pieces} = 14 \text{ pieces}
     \]

3. **Calculate how much more candy Roger has than Sandra:**
   - To find the difference in the amount of candy they have, we subtract Sandra's total from Roger's total:
     \[
     \text{Difference} = \text{Total for Roger} - \text{Total for Sandra} = 14 \text{ pieces} - 12 \text{ pieces} = 2 \text{ pieces}
     \]
