- Hours worked per week: 25
- Weeks worked per year: 52

To find the annual income for the old job, we multiply the hourly wage by the number of hours worked per week and then multiply that result by the number of weeks worked per year:

\[
\text{Annual income (old job)} = \text{Hourly wage} \times \text{Hours per week} \times \text{Weeks per year}
\]
\[
\text{Annual income (old job)} = 16 \text{ dollars/hour} \times 25 \text{ hours/week} \times 52 \text{ weeks/year}
\]

\[
16 \times 25 = 400 \text{ dollars/week}
\]
\[
400 \text{ dollars/week} \times 52 \text{ weeks/year} = 20800 \text{ dollars/year}
\]

- Weeks worked per year: 52

\[

\[
20 \times 40 = 800 \text{ dollars/week}
\]
\[
800 \text{ dollars/week} \times 52 \text{ weeks/year} = 41600 \text{ dollars/year}
\]

So Jame's annual income from his new job is $41,600.

**Step 3: Calculate the difference in annual income between the new job and the old job.**

To find out how much more he makes in the new job compared to the old job, we subtract the annual income of the old job from the annual income of the new job:

\[
\text{Difference} = \text{Annual income (new job)} - \text{Annual income (old job)}
