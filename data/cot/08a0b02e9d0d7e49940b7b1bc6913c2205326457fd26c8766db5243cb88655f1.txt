   \[
   \text{Logs from walnut trees} = 4 \, \text{trees} \times 100 \, \text{logs/tree} = 400 \, \text{logs}
   \]

   \[
   \text{Total logs} = \text{Logs from pine trees} + \text{Logs from maple trees} + \text{Logs from walnut trees}

   \[
   \text{Total logs} = 640 \, \text{logs} + 180 \, \text{logs} + 400 \, \text{logs} = 1220 \, \text{logs}
