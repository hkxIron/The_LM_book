   \text{Number of parents who agree} = 20\% \times 800

   \[
   \text{Number of parents who agree} = 0.20 \times 800 = 160
   \]

2. **Calculate the number of parents who disagree.**
   To find the number of parents who disagree, we subtract the number of parents who agree from the total number of parents:
   \[
   \text{Number of parents who disagree} = \text{Total parents} - \text{Number of parents who agree}
   \]
   So we can calculate:
   \[
   \text{Number of parents who disagree} = 800 - 160 = 640
   \]

Thus, the number of parents who disagree with the tuition fee increase is 640.
