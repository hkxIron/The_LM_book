   To find out how many pages Yasna needs to read each day, we will divide the total number of pages by the total number of days.
   \[
   \text{Pages per day} = \frac{\text{Total pages}}{\text{Total days}} = \frac{280}{14}

   \[
   \text{Pages per day} = 20
