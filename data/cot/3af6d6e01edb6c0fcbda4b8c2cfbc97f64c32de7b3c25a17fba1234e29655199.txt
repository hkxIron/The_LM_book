   \text{Number of red stripes among the remaining} = \frac{12}{2} = 6

   Now we add the first red stripe to the number of red stripes among the remaining:
   \[
   \text{Total red stripes on one flag} = 1 + 6 = 7

   Since John buys 10 flags, we multiply the number of red stripes on one flag by the number of flags:
   \[
   \text{Total red stripes for 10 flags} = 7 \times 10 = 70
