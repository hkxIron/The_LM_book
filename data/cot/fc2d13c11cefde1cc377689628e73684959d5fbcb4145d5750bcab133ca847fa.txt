To find the base length of the triangle, we can use the formula for the area of a triangle:

\[
\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height}
\]

We know the area and the height of the triangle. We are given:

- Area = 24 square units
- Height = 8 units

First, we will substitute the known values into the area formula:

\[
24 = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times 8
\]

Now, we need to simplify the right side of the equation. Let's calculate \(\frac{1}{2} \times 8\):

\[
\frac{1}{2} \times 8 = 4
\]

So, the equation now looks like this:

\[
24 = 4 \times \text{base}
\]

Next, to solve for the base, we will divide both sides of the equation by 4:

\[
\text{base} = \frac{24}{4}
\]

Calculating that gives us:

\[
\text{base} = 6
\]

Thus, the base length of the triangle is 6 units.
