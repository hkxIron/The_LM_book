<reasoning>
To find out how many hours James spends meditating in a week, we will follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total minutes spent meditating in a day**:
   Since James meditates for 30 minutes twice a day, we first need to find out how many minutes that is in total for one day.
   \[
   30 \text{ minutes} \times 2 = 60 \text{ minutes per day}
   \]

2. **Calculate the total minutes spent in a week**:
   There are 7 days in a week, so we will multiply the daily meditation time by 7.
   \[
   60 \text{ minutes per day} \times 7 \text{ days} = 420 \text{ minutes per week}
   \]

3. **Convert minutes into hours**:
   We need to convert the total minutes into hours, knowing that there are 60 minutes in an hour.
   \[
   \frac{420 \text{ minutes}}{60 \text{ minutes per hour}} = 7 \text{ hours}
   \]

Thus, James spends a total of 7 hours meditating each week.
</reasoning>
<answer>7</answer>
