<reasoning>
First, let's calculate the total cost of the hotel room before applying the discount.

1. **Calculate the total cost without the discount:**
   John is booking 3 nights at a rate of $250 per night. 
   To find the total cost, we multiply the number of nights by the price per night:
   \[
   \text{Total cost} = \text{Number of nights} \times \text{Price per night} = 3 \times 250
   \]
   Performing the multiplication:
   \[
   3 \times 250 = 750
   \]
   So, the total cost without the discount is $750.

2. **Apply the discount:**
   John has a $100 discount. To find out how much he will actually pay, we subtract the discount from the total cost:
   \[
   \text{Amount paid} = \text{Total cost} - \text{Discount} = 750 - 100
   \]
   Performing the subtraction:
   \[
   750 - 100 = 650
   \]
   Therefore, the amount John will pay after applying the discount is $650.
</reasoning>
