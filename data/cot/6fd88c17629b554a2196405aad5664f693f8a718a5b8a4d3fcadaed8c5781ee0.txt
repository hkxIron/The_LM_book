<reasoning>
To find out how many students can be accommodated by the 6 school buses, we first need to determine how many seats are available in one bus.

1. **Calculate the number of seats in one bus**:
   - There are 4 columns of seats and 10 rows of seats in each bus.
   - To find the total number of seats in one bus, we multiply the number of columns by the number of rows: 
     \[
     \text{Seats in one bus} = \text{Number of columns} \times \text{Number of rows} = 4 \times 10
     \]
   - Performing the multiplication:
     \[
     4 \times 10 = 40
     \]
   - Thus, one bus can accommodate 40 students.

2. **Calculate the total number of seats in 6 buses**:
   - Now that we know one bus can hold 40 students, we will multiply that by the number of buses (6):
     \[
     \text{Total seats in 6 buses} = \text{Seats in one bus} \times \text{Number of buses} = 40 \times 6
     \]
   - Performing the multiplication:
     \[
     40 \times 6 = 240
     \]
   - Therefore, the total number of students that can be accommodated in 6 buses is 240.

Putting it all together:
- We calculated that one bus holds 40 seats.
- We then multiplied by the number of buses to find that 6 buses can hold 240 seats.
