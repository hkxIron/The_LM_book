<reasoning>
To find out how much money Carrie can make by selling all of her tomatoes and carrots, we need to calculate the total revenue from selling each type of vegetable separately and then add those amounts together.

1. **Calculate the revenue from tomatoes:**
   - Carrie harvested 200 tomatoes.
   - Each tomato can be sold for $1.
   - Therefore, the total revenue from tomatoes is calculated by multiplying the number of tomatoes by the price per tomato.
   - Total revenue from tomatoes = 200 tomatoes × $1/tomato = $200.

2. **Calculate the revenue from carrots:**
   - Carrie harvested 350 carrots.
   - Each carrot can be sold for $1.50.
   - Therefore, the total revenue from carrots is calculated by multiplying the number of carrots by the price per carrot.
   - Total revenue from carrots = 350 carrots × $1.50/carrot = $525.

3. **Calculate the total revenue from both tomatoes and carrots:**
   - We now add the revenue from the tomatoes and the revenue from the carrots together to find the total revenue.
   - Total revenue = Total revenue from tomatoes + Total revenue from carrots = $200 + $525 = $725.

Thus, the total amount of money Carrie can make by selling all her tomatoes and carrots is $725.
