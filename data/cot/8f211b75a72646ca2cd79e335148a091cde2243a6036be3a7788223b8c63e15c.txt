- Let \( P_{2020} \) be the number of participants in 2020

   P_{2019} = 2 \times P_{2018} + 20

   \[
   P_{2019} = 2 \times 150 + 20 = 300 + 20 = 320
   \]

   \[
   P_{2020} = \frac{1}{2} \times P_{2019} - 40
   \]
   Substituting the value of \( P_{2019} \):
   \[
   P_{2020} = \frac{1}{2} \times 320 - 40 = 160 - 40 = 120
   \]
   Thus, there were 120 participants in 2020.

   We are asked to find how many more participants there were in 2019 than in 2020:
   \[
   \text{Difference} = P_{2019} - P_{2020} = 320 - 120 = 200

Thus, the number of more participants in 2019 than in 2020 is 200.
