1. **Calculate the revenue from matinee tickets:**

     \[
     \text{Revenue from matinee tickets} = 200 \times 5

     \[

     \text{Revenue from evening tickets} = 300 \times 12

     \[
     \text{Revenue from 3D tickets} = 100 \times 20

     \[
     100 \times 20 = 2000
     \]

4. **Calculate the total revenue:**

     \[
     \text{Total Revenue} = \text{Revenue from matinee tickets} + \text{Revenue from evening tickets} + \text{Revenue from 3D tickets}
