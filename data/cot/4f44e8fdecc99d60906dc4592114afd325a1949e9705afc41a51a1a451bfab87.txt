   \[
   \text{Cost of first 10 pens} = 10p

   \[
   \text{Cost of second 10 pens} = 10 \times \left(\frac{p}{2}\right) = 5p
   \]

3. **Adding the costs together**: The total cost for 20 pens is the sum of the costs for the first and second batches:
   \[
   \text{Total cost} = 10p + 5p = 15p
   \]

4. **Setting up the equation**: We know from the problem that the customer paid $30 for the 20 pens. Therefore, we can set up the equation:
   \[
   15p = 30
   \]

5. **Solving for \( p \)**: To find the regular price \( p \), we divide both sides of the equation by 15:
   \[
   p = \frac{30}{15} = 2
   \]
