   \[
   \text{Total cost} = \text{Cost per pie} \times \text{Number of pies} = 0.5 \times 12 = 6 \text{ dollars}
   \]

4. **Calculate the profit:**
   The profit is calculated by subtracting the total cost from the total revenue.
   \[
   \text{Profit} = \text{Revenue} - \text{Total cost} = 144 - 6 = 138 \text{ dollars}
