<reasoning>
To find out how much profit John makes in a year from subletting his apartment, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total income from subletting**: 
   John has 3 people subletting his apartment, and each pays $400 per month. So, we multiply the number of people by the payment per person.
   \[
   \text{Total Income} = 3 \text{ people} \times 400 \text{ dollars/person} = 1200 \text{ dollars/month}
   \]
   This means John earns $1200 each month from the subletters.

2. **Calculate the total expenses**: 
   John pays $900 per month to rent the apartment. Therefore, the total expenses for a month are simply the rent he pays.
   \[
   \text{Total Expenses} = 900 \text{ dollars/month}
   \]

3. **Calculate the monthly profit**: 
   To find the profit for one month, we subtract the total expenses from the total income.
   \[
   \text{Monthly Profit} = \text{Total Income} - \text{Total Expenses} = 1200 \text{ dollars} - 900 \text{ dollars} = 300 \text{ dollars}
   \]
   So, John makes a profit of $300 each month.

4. **Calculate the annual profit**: 
   Since there are 12 months in a year, we multiply the monthly profit by 12 to find the annual profit.
   \[
   \text{Annual Profit} = \text{Monthly Profit} \times 12 = 300 \text{ dollars/month} \times 12 \text{ months} = 3600 \text{ dollars}
   \]

Therefore, John's total profit from subletting his apartment in a year is $3600.
</reasoning>
<answer>3600</answer>
