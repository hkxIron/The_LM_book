To find out how many school days it will take Jessica to meet the 50-hour driving requirement, we first need to determine how much time she spends driving each day.

1. **Calculate the total driving time for one round trip to school**:
   - It takes Jessica 20 minutes to drive to school and another 20 minutes to drive back home.
   - Therefore, the total driving time for one day (round trip) is:
     \[
     20 \text{ minutes} + 20 \text{ minutes} = 40 \text{ minutes}
     \]

2. **Convert 50 hours into minutes**:
   - Since there are 60 minutes in an hour, we convert 50 hours into minutes:
     \[
     50 \text{ hours} \times 60 \text{ minutes/hour} = 3000 \text{ minutes}
     \]

3. **Determine how many days are needed to accumulate 3000 minutes**:
   - We know from step 1 that Jessica drives for 40 minutes each day. To find the number of school days needed, we divide the total minutes required by the minutes she drives each day:
     \[
     \frac{3000 \text{ minutes}}{40 \text{ minutes/day}} = 75 \text{ days}
     \]

Therefore, it will take Jessica 75 school days to meet the 50-hour driving requirement.
