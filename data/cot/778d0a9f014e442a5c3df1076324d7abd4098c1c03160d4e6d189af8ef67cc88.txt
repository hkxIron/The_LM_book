   \[
   \text{Weekly Water Intake} = \text{Daily Water Intake} \times 7 = 20 \times 7 = 140 \text{ ounces}
   \]

   \[
   \text{Number of Fills} = \frac{\text{Weekly Water Intake}}{\text{Bottle Capacity}} = \frac{140}{35}

   \[
   \frac{140}{35} = 4
