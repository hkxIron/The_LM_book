<reasoning>
First, we need to determine how many jellybeans Caleb has. We know that Caleb has 3 dozen jellybeans. 

1. **Calculate the number of jellybeans Caleb has:**
   - A dozen is equal to 12 items.
   - So, if Caleb has 3 dozen jellybeans, we calculate:
     \[
     3 \text{ dozen} = 3 \times 12 = 36 \text{ jellybeans}
     \]
   Therefore, Caleb has 36 jellybeans.

2. **Calculate how many jellybeans Sophie has:**
   - We are told that Sophie has half as many jellybeans as Caleb.
   - To find out how many jellybeans Sophie has, we divide the number of jellybeans Caleb has by 2:
     \[
     \text{Sophie’s jellybeans} = \frac{36}{2} = 18 \text{ jellybeans}
     \]
   So, Sophie has 18 jellybeans.

3. **Calculate the total number of jellybeans:**
   - To find the total number of jellybeans Caleb and Sophie have together, we simply add the number of jellybeans each has:
     \[
     \text{Total jellybeans} = \text{Caleb's jellybeans} + \text{Sophie's jellybeans} = 36 + 18 = 54
     \]

Thus, the total number of jellybeans Caleb and Sophie have together is 54.
</reasoning>
<answer>54</answer>
