To find out how many of the vaccinated people were children, we first need to determine how many were adults. 

1. **Calculate the number of adults vaccinated:**
   - We know that 80% of the 650 vaccinated people were adults. 
   - To find the number of adults, we can use the formula: 
     \[
     \text{Number of adults} = \text{Total vaccinated} \times \text{Percentage of adults}
     \]
   - Here, the total vaccinated is 650, and the percentage of adults in decimal form is 0.80. 
   - So, we calculate:
     \[
     \text{Number of adults} = 650 \times 0.80
     \]
   - Multiplying gives:
     \[
     \text{Number of adults} = 520
     \]
   - This means 520 of the vaccinated people were adults.

2. **Calculate the number of children vaccinated:**
   - To find the number of children, we subtract the number of adults from the total number of vaccinated people:
     \[
     \text{Number of children} = \text{Total vaccinated} - \text{Number of adults}
     \]
   - Substituting the values we found:
     \[
     \text{Number of children} = 650 - 520
     \]
   - Performing the subtraction gives:
     \[
     \text{Number of children} = 130
     \]

Therefore, the number of vaccinated people who were children is 130.
