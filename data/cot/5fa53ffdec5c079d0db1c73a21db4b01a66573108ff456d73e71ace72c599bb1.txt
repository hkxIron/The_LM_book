<reasoning>
To find out how many peaches Brenda has left after sorting and discarding some, we can follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the number of fresh peaches.** 
   - Brenda picked a total of 250 peaches, and 60% of them are fresh.
   - We can find the number of fresh peaches by multiplying the total number of peaches by the percentage of fresh peaches. 
   - In mathematical terms: 
     \[
     \text{Fresh peaches} = 250 \times 0.60
     \]

   Performing the calculation:
   \[
   \text{Fresh peaches} = 250 \times 0.60 = 150
   \]

   So, Brenda has 150 fresh peaches.

2. **Subtract the number of peaches that are too small.**
   - Brenda has to throw away 15 peaches for being too small. 
   - To find out how many peaches she has left, we subtract the number of peaches thrown away from the number of fresh peaches:
   \[
   \text{Peaches left} = \text{Fresh peaches} - \text{Peaches thrown away}
   \]
   Substituting the values we have:
   \[
   \text{Peaches left} = 150 - 15
   \]

   Performing the calculation:
   \[
   \text{Peaches left} = 150 - 15 = 135
   \]

Therefore, after sorting and discarding the small peaches, Brenda has 135 peaches left.
</reasoning>
<answer>135</answer>
