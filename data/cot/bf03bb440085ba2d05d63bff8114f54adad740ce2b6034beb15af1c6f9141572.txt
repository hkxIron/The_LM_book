   \]

   Now that we have the number of bottles ordered in both months, we can find the total by adding the bottles from April and May:
   \[
   \text{Total Bottles} = \text{Bottles in April} + \text{Bottles in May} = 400 \text{ bottles} + 600 \text{ bottles} = 1000 \text{ bottles}
   \]
