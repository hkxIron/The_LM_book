   There are 12 months in a year.

2. **Determine how many bars of soap Maria needs for a year**:
   We know that one bar of soap lasts her for 2 months. Therefore, to find out how many bars she needs for 12 months, we divide the total number of months in a year by the number of months each bar lasts:
   \[
   \text{Number of bars needed} = \frac{\text{Total months in a year}}{\text{Months per bar}} = \frac{12 \text{ months}}{2 \text{ months/bar}} = 6 \text{ bars}
   \]

3. **Calculate the total cost of the soap**:
   Since each bar of soap costs $8.00, we can calculate the total cost by multiplying the number of bars she needs by the cost per bar:
   \[
   \text{Total cost} = \text{Number of bars} \times \text{Cost per bar} = 6 \text{ bars} \times 8 \text{ dollars/bar} = 48 \text{ dollars}
   \]
