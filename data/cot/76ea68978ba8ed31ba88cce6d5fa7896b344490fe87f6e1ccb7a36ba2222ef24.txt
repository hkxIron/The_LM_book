     \[
     \text{Price per ounce for smaller box} = \frac{340 \text{ cents}}{20 \text{ ounces}} = 17 \text{ cents per ounce}
     \]

   - The price per ounce for the larger box is 16 cents per ounce.
   - The price per ounce for the smaller box is 17 cents per ounce.
   - Since 16 cents is less than 17 cents, the larger box provides the better value.
