<reasoning>
To find out how much money John has left after his purchases, we will follow these steps:

1. **Identify the initial amount of money John has**: John starts with €100.

2. **Calculate the total cost of his purchases**: He buys a roast for €17 and vegetables for €11. To find the total cost, we need to add these two amounts together:
   \[
   \text{Total Cost} = \text{Cost of Roast} + \text{Cost of Vegetables} = 17 + 11
   \]
   Performing the addition:
   \[
   17 + 11 = 28
   \]
   So, the total cost of his purchases is €28.

3. **Subtract the total cost from the initial amount**: Now we need to subtract the total cost of his purchases from the amount of money he had initially to find out how much he has left:
   \[
   \text{Money Left} = \text{Initial Amount} - \text{Total Cost} = 100 - 28
   \]
   Performing the subtraction:
   \[
   100 - 28 = 72
   \]

Therefore, after his purchases, John has €72 left.
</reasoning>
