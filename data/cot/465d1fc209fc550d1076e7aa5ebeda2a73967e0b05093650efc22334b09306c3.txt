   \[
   \text{Total cost for children} = 124 \text{ dollars} - 80 \text{ dollars} = 44 \text{ dollars}
   \]

   - There are 11 children, and we need to find the price of one child's ticket.
   - We can find the cost of one child's ticket by dividing the total cost for children's tickets by the number of children:

   \[
   \text{Price per child's ticket} = \frac{44 \text{ dollars}}{11 \text{ children}} = 4 \text{ dollars}
   \]
