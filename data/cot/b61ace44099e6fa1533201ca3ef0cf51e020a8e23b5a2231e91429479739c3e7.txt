To find out how many kilograms of dog food Elise already had, we need to determine the total amount of dog food she has after her purchases and then subtract the amounts she bought.

   - We perform the calculation:
     \( 40 \text{ kg} - 25 \text{ kg} = 15 \text{ kg} \).
