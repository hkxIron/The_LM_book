First, we need to determine how much storage Jeff has left on his phone after using 4 GB. 

1. **Calculate remaining storage**: 
   Jeff's phone has a total of 16 GB, and he is using 4 GB. 
   To find out how much storage is left, we subtract the used storage from the total storage:
   \[
   16 \text{ GB} - 4 \text{ GB} = 12 \text{ GB}
   \]
   This means Jeff has 12 GB of free storage available for more songs.

2. **Convert GB to MB**: 
   Since each GB contains 1000 MB, we convert the remaining storage from GB to MB:
   \[
   12 \text{ GB} \times 1000 \text{ MB/GB} = 12000 \text{ MB}
   \]
   Now, Jeff has 12000 MB of free storage.

3. **Determine the size of one song**:
   We know that one song takes up about 30 MB of storage.

4. **Calculate the number of songs Jeff can store**: 
   To find out how many songs can fit in the available 12000 MB, we divide the total available storage by the size of one song:
   \[
   \frac{12000 \text{ MB}}{30 \text{ MB/song}} = 400 \text{ songs}
   \]
   Therefore, Jeff can store a total of 400 songs on his phone.
