   \]

   Now, we add the total cost of the shirts and the sandals:
   \[
   \text{Total cost} = \text{Cost of shirts} + \text{Cost of sandals} = 50 + 9 = 59
   \]

   Yanna paid with a $100 bill, so we find the change by subtracting the total cost from the amount she paid:
   \[
   \text{Change} = 100 - 59 = 41
