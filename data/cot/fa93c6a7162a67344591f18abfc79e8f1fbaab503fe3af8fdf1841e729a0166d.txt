To find out how long John spends on all the pictures, we need to calculate the time he spends drawing and coloring each picture separately and then sum these times.

     \[
     \text{Total drawing time} = 10 \text{ pictures} \times 2 \text{ hours per picture} = 20 \text{ hours}

     \[
     30\% \text{ of } 2 \text{ hours} = 0.3 \times 2 \text{ hours} = 0.6 \text{ hours}
     \]

     \[
     \text{Time to color each picture} = 2 \text{ hours} - 0.6 \text{ hours} = 1.4 \text{ hours}
     \]
   - Now we calculate the total coloring time for all pictures:
     \[
     \text{Total coloring time} = 10 \text{ pictures} \times 1.4 \text{ hours per picture} = 14 \text{ hours}
     \]

3. **Calculate the total time spent on all pictures:**
   - Finally, we sum the total drawing time and the total coloring time:
     \[
     \text{Total time spent} = \text{Total drawing time} + \text{Total coloring time} = 20 \text{ hours} + 14 \text{ hours} = 34 \text{ hours}
     \]
