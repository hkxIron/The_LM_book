\[
x + 2x = 6
\]

Next, we will combine like terms on the left side of the equation:

\[
3x = 6
\]

Now, we need to solve for \( x \), which represents the cost of the pencil. We do this by dividing both sides of the equation by 3:

\[
x = \frac{6}{3} = 2
\]

Now that we have found \( x \), which is the cost of the pencil, we can find the cost of the pen. Since the pen costs twice as much as the pencil, we calculate:

\[
\text{Cost of the pen} = 2x = 2 \times 2 = 4
\]
