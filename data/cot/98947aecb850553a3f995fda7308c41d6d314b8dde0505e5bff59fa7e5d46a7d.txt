<reasoning>
To find out how much longer it takes Julia to run 5 miles in her new shoes compared to her old ones, we will calculate the total time it takes for each scenario and then find the difference.

1. **Calculate the time to run 5 miles with her old shoes:**
   - Julia runs 1 mile in 10 minutes with her old shoes.
   - Therefore, for 5 miles, the time taken would be:
     \[
     \text{Time with old shoes} = 5 \text{ miles} \times 10 \text{ minutes/mile} = 50 \text{ minutes}
     \]

2. **Calculate the time to run 5 miles with her new shoes:**
   - Julia runs 1 mile in 13 minutes with her new shoes.
   - Therefore, for 5 miles, the time taken would be:
     \[
     \text{Time with new shoes} = 5 \text{ miles} \times 13 \text{ minutes/mile} = 65 \text{ minutes}
     \]

3. **Find the difference in time between the two scenarios:**
   - Now, we subtract the time taken with her old shoes from the time taken with her new shoes:
     \[
     \text{Difference} = \text{Time with new shoes} - \text{Time with old shoes}
     \]
     \[
     \text{Difference} = 65 \text{ minutes} - 50 \text{ minutes} = 15 \text{ minutes}
     \]

Therefore, it takes Julia 15 minutes longer to run 5 miles in her new shoes than it would if she wore her old ones.
</reasoning>
<answer>15</answer>
