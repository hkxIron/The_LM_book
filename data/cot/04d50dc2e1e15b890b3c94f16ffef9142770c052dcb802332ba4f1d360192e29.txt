     \[

     \[

     \[
     6 \text{ refurbished T-shirts} \times 2.5 \text{ dollars/refurbished T-shirt} = 15 \text{ dollars}
     \]

5. **Calculate the total income:**

     \[
     \text{Total Income} = \text{Income from T-shirts} + \text{Income from pants} + \text{Income from skirts} + \text{Income from refurbished T-shirts}
