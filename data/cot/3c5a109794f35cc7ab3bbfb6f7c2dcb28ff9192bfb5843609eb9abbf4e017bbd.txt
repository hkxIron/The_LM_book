<reasoning>
First, we need to determine how much James pays for one semester. Since he takes 20 units and each unit costs $50, we can calculate the total cost for one semester by multiplying the number of units by the cost per unit.

1. **Calculate the cost for one semester:**
   \[
   \text{Cost for one semester} = \text{Number of units} \times \text{Cost per unit} = 20 \times 50
   \]
   This multiplication gives us the total cost for one semester.

Now let's compute that:
\[
20 \times 50 = 1000
\]
So, James pays $1000 for one semester.

2. **Calculate the cost for two semesters:**
   Since we know the cost for one semester is $1000, we simply need to multiply this amount by 2 to find out how much he pays for two semesters.
   \[
   \text{Cost for two semesters} = \text{Cost for one semester} \times 2 = 1000 \times 2
   \]

Now let's compute that:
\[
1000 \times 2 = 2000
\]
Therefore, James pays $2000 for two semesters.
</reasoning>
