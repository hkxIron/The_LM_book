To determine how many apples the farmer needs to order for a month, we first need to calculate the total number of apples Chandler and Lucy eat in a week and then multiply that by the number of weeks in a month.

1. **Calculate the weekly apple consumption**:
   - Chandler eats 23 apples per week.
   - Lucy eats 19 apples per week.
   - To find the total apples they consume per week, we add their consumption together:
     \[
     \text{Total apples per week} = \text{Chandler's apples} + \text{Lucy's apples} = 23 + 19
     \]
   - Performing the addition:
     \[
     23 + 19 = 42
     \]
   - So, together they eat 42 apples per week.

2. **Calculate the monthly consumption**:
   - There are typically 4 weeks in a month. To find out how many apples they need for the entire month, we multiply the total weekly consumption by 4:
     \[
     \text{Total apples per month} = \text{Total apples per week} \times 4 = 42 \times 4
     \]
   - Performing the multiplication:
     \[
     42 \times 4 = 168
     \]
   - Therefore, they will need a total of 168 apples for the month.

In conclusion, the farmer needs to order 168 apples to meet Chandler and Lucy's monthly consumption.
