To find the total number of students that the four primary schools can teach at a time, we need to calculate the capacity of each school and then sum them up.

   - The first two schools can each teach 400 students.

   - Since there are two schools that can each teach 400 students, we multiply:
     \[
     400 \, \text{students} \times 2 \, \text{schools} = 800 \, \text{students}
     \]

     \[
     340 \, \text{students} \times 2 \, \text{schools} = 680 \, \text{students}
     \]

   - Now we add the total capacities of the first two schools and the last two schools:
     \[
     800 \, \text{students} + 680 \, \text{students} = 1480 \, \text{students}
     \]
