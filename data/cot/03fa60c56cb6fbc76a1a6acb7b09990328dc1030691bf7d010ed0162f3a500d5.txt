<reasoning>
To find out how much money Hannah has left after her sales and purchases, we will follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total money earned from selling cookies.
2. Calculate the total money earned from selling cupcakes.
3. Add the total earnings from both cookies and cupcakes.
4. Calculate the total cost of the measuring spoons.
5. Subtract the total cost of the measuring spoons from the total earnings to find out how much money she has left.

Let's break it down step by step:

**Step 1: Calculate the total money earned from selling cookies.**
Hannah sold 40 cookies at $0.8 each. To find the total, we multiply the number of cookies by the price per cookie:
\[
\text{Total from cookies} = 40 \times 0.8 = 32
\]
This means she earned $32 from selling cookies.

**Step 2: Calculate the total money earned from selling cupcakes.**
Hannah sold 30 cupcakes at $2 each. Again, we multiply the number of cupcakes by the price per cupcake:
\[
\text{Total from cupcakes} = 30 \times 2 = 60
\]
Thus, she earned $60 from selling cupcakes.

**Step 3: Add the total earnings from both cookies and cupcakes.**
Now, we add the earnings from cookies and cupcakes:
\[
\text{Total earnings} = \text{Total from cookies} + \text{Total from cupcakes} = 32 + 60 = 92
\]
So, Hannah earned a total of $92 from selling cookies and cupcakes.

**Step 4: Calculate the total cost of the measuring spoons.**
Hannah bought 2 sets of measuring spoons for $6.5 each. To find the total cost, we multiply the number of sets by the price per set:
\[
\text{Total cost of measuring spoons} = 2 \times 6.5 = 13
\]
This means she spent $13 on measuring spoons.

**Step 5: Subtract the total cost of the measuring spoons from the total earnings.**
Finally, we find out how much money she has left by subtracting the total cost from the total earnings:
\[
\text{Money left} = \text{Total earnings} - \text{Total cost of measuring spoons} = 92 - 13 = 79
\]

Therefore, after all the sales and purchases, Hannah has $79 left.

<answer>79</answer>
