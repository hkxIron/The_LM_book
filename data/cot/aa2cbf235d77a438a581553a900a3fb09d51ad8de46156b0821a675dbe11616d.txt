<reasoning>
To find out how much money was made from selling the cookies, we will calculate the total income from each type of cookie separately and then sum the two amounts.

1. **Calculate the income from chocolate cookies:**
   - We have 220 chocolate cookies.
   - Each chocolate cookie is sold for $1.
   - So, the total income from chocolate cookies is calculated as follows:
     \[
     \text{Income from chocolate cookies} = \text{Number of chocolate cookies} \times \text{Price per chocolate cookie}
     \]
     \[
     = 220 \times 1 = 220
     \]

2. **Calculate the income from vanilla cookies:**
   - We have 70 vanilla cookies.
   - Each vanilla cookie is sold for $2.
   - So, the total income from vanilla cookies is calculated as follows:
     \[
     \text{Income from vanilla cookies} = \text{Number of vanilla cookies} \times \text{Price per vanilla cookie}
     \]
     \[
     = 70 \times 2 = 140
     \]

3. **Sum the incomes from both types of cookies:**
   - Now, we will add the income from chocolate cookies and vanilla cookies to find the total income:
     \[
     \text{Total Income} = \text{Income from chocolate cookies} + \text{Income from vanilla cookies}
     \]
     \[
     = 220 + 140 = 360
     \]

Therefore, the total money made from selling the cookies is $360.
</reasoning>
<answer>360</answer>
