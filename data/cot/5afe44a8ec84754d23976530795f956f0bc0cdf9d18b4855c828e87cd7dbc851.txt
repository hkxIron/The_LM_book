1. **Calculate Mr. Grey's selling price to Mr. Brown:**
   Mr. Grey's original house value = $100,000
   Mr. Grey sells the house at a profit of 10%. 
   To find the selling price, we calculate 10% of $100,000 and add it to the original value.

   \[
   \text{Profit} = 10\% \text{ of } 100,000 = 0.10 \times 100,000 = 10,000
   \]
   \[
   \text{Selling price} = 100,000 + 10,000 = 110,000
   \]

   Therefore, Mr. Brown buys the house for $110,000.

2. **Calculate Mr. Brown's selling price after one year:**
   Now, Mr. Brown sells the house at a loss of 10%. 
   First, we need to calculate 10% of $110,000 and subtract it from the price Mr. Brown paid.

   \[
   \text{Loss} = 10\% \text{ of } 110,000 = 0.10 \times 110,000 = 11,000
   \]
   \[
   \text{Selling price} = 110,000 - 11,000 = 99,000
   \]

   Therefore, Mr. Brown's selling price is $99,000.
