<reasoning>
To determine how much money Susie earned from selling pizza, we need to calculate the total income from both the slices and the whole pizzas she sold.

1. **Calculate income from slices sold:**  
   Susie sells each slice of pizza for $3. She sold 24 slices.  
   To find the total income from the slices, we multiply the number of slices by the price per slice:  
   \[
   \text{Income from slices} = \text{Number of slices} \times \text{Price per slice} = 24 \times 3
   \]  
   Now, calculating that:  
   \[
   24 \times 3 = 72
   \]  
   So, Susie earned $72 from selling slices.

2. **Calculate income from whole pizzas sold:**  
   Susie sells each whole pizza for $15. She sold 3 whole pizzas.  
   To find the total income from the whole pizzas, we multiply the number of pizzas by the price per pizza:  
   \[
   \text{Income from whole pizzas} = \text{Number of pizzas} \times \text{Price per pizza} = 3 \times 15
   \]  
   Now, calculating that:  
   \[
   3 \times 15 = 45
   \]  
   So, Susie earned $45 from selling whole pizzas.

3. **Calculate total income:**  
   Now we need to add the income from slices and the income from whole pizzas to find the total earnings:  
   \[
   \text{Total Income} = \text{Income from slices} + \text{Income from whole pizzas} = 72 + 45
   \]  
   Now, calculating that:  
   \[
   72 + 45 = 117
   \]  
   Therefore, Susie earned a total of $117.
</reasoning>
<answer>117</answer>
