To find out how much Tim makes in a week, we need to calculate his daily earnings and then multiply that by the number of days he works in a week.

1. **Calculate Tim's daily earnings**:
   - Tim does 100 tasks a day.
   - Each task pays $1.2.
   - To find his daily earnings, we multiply the number of tasks by the payment per task:
     \[
     \text{Daily Earnings} = \text{Number of tasks} \times \text{Payment per task} = 100 \times 1.2
     \]
   - Performing the multiplication:
     \[
     100 \times 1.2 = 120
     \]
   - So, Tim earns $120 each day.

2. **Calculate Tim's weekly earnings**:
   - Tim works 6 days a week.
   - To find his weekly earnings, we multiply his daily earnings by the number of days he works:
     \[
     \text{Weekly Earnings} = \text{Daily Earnings} \times \text{Number of days worked} = 120 \times 6
     \]
   - Performing the multiplication:
     \[
     120 \times 6 = 720
     \]
   - Thus, Tim earns $720 in a week.

Therefore, after calculating both his daily and weekly earnings step by step, we find that Tim makes a total of $720 in a week.
