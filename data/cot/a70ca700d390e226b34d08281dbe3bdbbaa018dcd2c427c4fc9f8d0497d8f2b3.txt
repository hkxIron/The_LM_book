   \[
   5x = 3x + 4
   \]

   \[
   5x - 3x = 4
   \]
   This simplifies to:
   \[
   2x = 4
   \]
   To isolate \(x\), we divide both sides by 2:
   \[
   x = 2
   \]

   - Number of boys:
     \[
     3x = 3(2) = 6
     \]
   - Number of girls:
     \[
     5x = 5(2) = 10
     \]

   \[
   \text{Total students} = 6 + 10 = 16
