<reasoning>

1. **Calculate the cost of one candy bar:**
   - The price of one candy bar is $2.

     \[
     \text{Cost of 1 candy bar} = 2 \text{ dollars}
     \]

2. **Calculate the cost of two bags of chips:**
   - The price of one bag of chips is $0.50.
   - For two bags of chips, the cost will be:
     \[
     \text{Cost of 2 bags of chips} = 2 \times 0.50 = 1 \text{ dollar}
     \]

3. **Calculate the total cost for one student:**
   - Now, let's add the cost of the candy bar and the cost of the chips:
     \[
     \text{Total cost for one student} = \text{Cost of 1 candy bar} + \text{Cost of 2 bags of chips} = 2 + 1 = 3 \text{ dollars}
     \]

4. **Calculate the total cost for 5 students:**
   - Since each of the 5 students will spend $3, we multiply the total cost for one student by 5:
     \[
     \text{Total cost for 5 students} = 5 \times 3 = 15 \text{ dollars}
     \]

</reasoning>
