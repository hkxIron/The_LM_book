To find the total amount of the mixture obtained by mixing water and vinegar, we'll calculate the volumes of water and vinegar separately and then add them together.

**Step 1: Calculate the volume of water.**
The problem states that the young lady mixes 3/5 of 20 liters of water.

\[
\text{Volume of water} = \frac{3}{5} \times 20
\]
Now, performing the multiplication:
\[
\text{Volume of water} = \frac{3 \times 20}{5} = \frac{60}{5} = 12 \text{ liters}
\]

Next, we find the volume of vinegar, which is 5/6 of 18 liters.

Using a similar calculation:
\[
\text{Volume of vinegar} = \frac{5}{6} \times 18
\]
Performing the multiplication:
\[
\text{Volume of vinegar} = \frac{5 \times 18}{6} = \frac{90}{6} = 15 \text{ liters}
\]

**Step 3: Calculate the total volume of the mixture.**
Now, we add the volumes of water and vinegar together to find the total volume of the mixture:
\[
\text{Total volume} = \text{Volume of water} + \text{Volume of vinegar}
\]

\[
\text{Total volume} = 12 + 15 = 27 \text{ liters}
\]
