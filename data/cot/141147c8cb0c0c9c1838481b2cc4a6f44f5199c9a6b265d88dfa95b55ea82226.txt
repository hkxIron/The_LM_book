<reasoning>
To determine how many ounces of fluid Tom drinks in a week, we need to calculate the total fluid he consumes in a day and then multiply that by the number of days in a week.

1. **Calculate the total ounces of soda Tom drinks each day:**
   - Tom drinks 5 cans of soda, and each can is 12 ounces.
   - To find the total ounces of soda, we multiply the number of cans by the ounces per can:
     \[
     5 \, \text{cans} \times 12 \, \text{oz/can} = 60 \, \text{oz}
     \]
   - So, Tom drinks 60 ounces of soda each day.

2. **Add the ounces of water Tom drinks each day:**
   - In addition to soda, Tom drinks 64 ounces of water each day.
   - To find the total daily fluid intake, we add the ounces of soda to the ounces of water:
     \[
     60 \, \text{oz (soda)} + 64 \, \text{oz (water)} = 124 \, \text{oz}
     \]
   - Thus, Tom drinks a total of 124 ounces of fluid each day.

3. **Calculate the total fluid intake for a week:**
   - There are 7 days in a week, so we multiply Tom's daily intake by 7:
     \[
     124 \, \text{oz/day} \times 7 \, \text{days} = 868 \, \text{oz}
     \]
   - Therefore, Tom drinks a total of 868 ounces of fluid in a week.
</reasoning>
<answer>868</answer>
