<reasoning>
To find out how many minutes Larry spends on his dog each day, we need to calculate the time he spends walking and playing with his dog, as well as the time he spends feeding his dog. 

1. **Calculate the time spent walking and playing with the dog:**
   - Larry spends half an hour twice a day. 
   - We can express half an hour in minutes: 
     \(\frac{1}{2} \text{ hour} = 30 \text{ minutes}\).
   - Since he does this twice a day, we multiply:
     \[
     30 \text{ minutes} \times 2 = 60 \text{ minutes}.
     \]

2. **Calculate the time spent feeding the dog:**
   - Larry spends a fifth of an hour feeding his dog every day.
   - We convert a fifth of an hour into minutes:
     \(\frac{1}{5} \text{ hour} = 12 \text{ minutes}\) (since \(1 \text{ hour} = 60 \text{ minutes}\), and \(60 \div 5 = 12\)).
   
3. **Total time spent on the dog each day:**
   - Now, we add the time spent walking and playing with the dog to the time spent feeding the dog:
     \[
     60 \text{ minutes} + 12 \text{ minutes} = 72 \text{ minutes}.
     \]

Therefore, Larry spends a total of 72 minutes on his dog each day.
</reasoning>
<answer>72</answer>
