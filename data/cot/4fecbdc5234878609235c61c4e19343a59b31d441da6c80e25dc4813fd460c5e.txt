   - Total from SUVs and trucks = Total from SUVs + Total from trucks
   - Total from SUVs and trucks = $35 + $30 = $65

4. **Determine how much money was raised from cars:**
   - The total amount raised from all washes is $100.
   - Amount raised from cars = Total raised - Total from SUVs and trucks
   - Amount raised from cars = $100 - $65 = $35

5. **Calculate how many cars were washed:**
   - Laura charges $5 for each car.
   - Number of cars washed = Amount raised from cars / Price per car
   - Number of cars washed = $35 / $5 = 7
