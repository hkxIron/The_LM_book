   3 \text{ people} \times 3 \text{ sodas per person} = 9 \text{ sodas}

   \[
   2 \text{ people} \times 4 \text{ sodas per person} = 8 \text{ sodas}

   \[
   1 \text{ person} \times 5 \text{ sodas} = 5 \text{ sodas}

   Now, we need to sum the sodas consumed by all groups:
   \[
   9 \text{ sodas} + 8 \text{ sodas} + 5 \text{ sodas} = 22 \text{ sodas}
   \]
