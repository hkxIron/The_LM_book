   \text{Total animals} = 3 \text{ (lions)} + 2 \text{ (rhinos)} = 5 \text{ (total animals)}

3. **Calculate the total recovery time**: Now, we multiply the total number of animals by the time it takes to recover each one to find the total time spent recovering all animals.
   \[
   \text{Total recovery time} = \text{Total animals} \times \text{Time per animal} = 5 \text{ (animals)} \times 2 \text{ (hours/animal)} = 10 \text{ hours}
   \]
