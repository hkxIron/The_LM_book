To find the odds that a marble drawn at random from Cara's bag is blue, we first need to determine how many marbles of each color are in the bag.

1. **Identify the total number of marbles**: 
   Cara has a total of 60 marbles.

2. **Determine the number of yellow marbles**: 
   It is given that there are 20 yellow marbles.

3. **Calculate the number of green marbles**: 
   Half as many marbles are green as there are yellow marbles. Therefore:
   \[
   \text{Number of green marbles} = \frac{20}{2} = 10
   \]

4. **Calculate the total number of yellow and green marbles**: 
   Adding the yellow and green marbles together:
   \[
   \text{Total yellow and green} = 20 + 10 = 30
   \]

5. **Determine the number of remaining marbles**: 
   The remaining marbles can be calculated by subtracting the number of yellow and green marbles from the total number of marbles:
   \[
   \text{Remaining marbles} = 60 - 30 = 30
   \]

6. **Divide the remaining marbles between red and blue**: 
   The remaining 30 marbles are equally divided between red and blue marbles, so:
   \[
   \text{Number of red marbles} = \frac{30}{2} = 15
   \]
   \[
   \text{Number of blue marbles} = \frac{30}{2} = 15
   \]

7. **Calculate the probability of picking a blue marble**: 
   The probability of picking a blue marble is the number of blue marbles divided by the total number of marbles:
   \[
   P(\text{blue}) = \frac{\text{Number of blue marbles}}{\text{Total number of marbles}} = \frac{15}{60}
   \]

8. **Convert the probability to a percentage**: 
   To express this probability as a percentage, we multiply by 100:
   \[
   P(\text{blue}) \times 100 = \frac{15}{60} \times 100 = 25
   \]

Thus, the odds that a marble picked at random is blue, expressed as a percentage, is 25%.
