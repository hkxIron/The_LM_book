   The insurance covers 80% of the total cost. We can calculate that by multiplying the total cost by 0.80.
   \[
   \text{Insurance Coverage} = 0.80 \times \text{Total Cost} = 0.80 \times 1000 = 800
   \]

4. **Calculate how much Mike has to pay**:
   To find out how much Mike has to pay, we subtract the insurance coverage from the total cost:
   \[
   \text{Mike's Payment} = \text{Total Cost} - \text{Insurance Coverage} = 1000 - 800 = 200
   \]
