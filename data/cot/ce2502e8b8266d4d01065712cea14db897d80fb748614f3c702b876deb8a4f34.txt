   \[
   \text{Price of one pair of ballet slippers} = \frac{2}{3} \times 60
   \]

   \[
   \frac{2 \times 60}{3} = \frac{120}{3} = 40
   \]

   Since Fern buys five pairs, we multiply the price of one pair by the number of pairs:

   \[
   \text{Total cost for ballet slippers} = 5 \times 40 = 200
   \]

3. **Calculate the total cost for both the high heels and the ballet slippers.**  
   Now, we need to add the cost of the high heels to the total cost of the ballet slippers:

   \[
   \text{Total cost} = \text{Cost of high heels} + \text{Total cost of ballet slippers}

   \[
