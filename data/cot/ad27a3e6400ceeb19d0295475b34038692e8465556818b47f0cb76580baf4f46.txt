     \[
     \text{Fraction made by Janet} = \frac{\text{Janet's snowballs}}{\text{Total snowballs}} = \frac{50}{200}.

   - To convert a fraction to a percentage, we multiply it by 100.
     \[
     \text{Percentage made by Janet} = \frac{50}{200} \times 100.
     \]

4. **Simplify the fraction and perform the multiplication.**
   - First, simplify \(\frac{50}{200}\):
     \[
     \frac{50}{200} = \frac{1}{4} \quad (\text{dividing both numerator and denominator by 50})
     \]
   - Now, multiply by 100:
     \[
     \frac{1}{4} \times 100 = 25.
     \]
