<reasoning>
To determine how many roses Hanna gives to her friends, we first need to find out how many roses she can buy with her $300.

1. **Calculate the total number of roses Hanna can buy**: 
   Since each rose costs $2, we divide the total amount of money she has by the price of one rose.

   \[
   \text{Total roses} = \frac{\text{Total money}}{\text{Price per rose}} = \frac{300}{2} = 150
   \]

   Hanna can buy 150 roses with her $300.

2. **Distributing the roses**:
   - Jenna receives \( \frac{1}{3} \) of the total roses. 
   - Imma receives \( \frac{1}{2} \) of the total roses.

   Let's calculate how many roses each friend receives.
   
   - **Calculate Jenna's share**:
   \[
   \text{Jenna's roses} = \frac{1}{3} \times 150 = 50
   \]

   - **Calculate Imma's share**:
   \[
   \text{Imma's roses} = \frac{1}{2} \times 150 = 75
   \]

3. **Total roses given to friends**:
   Now, we add the number of roses given to Jenna and Imma together:

   \[
   \text{Total roses to friends} = \text{Jenna's roses} + \text{Imma's roses} = 50 + 75 = 125
   \]

Thus, the total number of roses that Hanna gives to her friends is 125.
</reasoning>
