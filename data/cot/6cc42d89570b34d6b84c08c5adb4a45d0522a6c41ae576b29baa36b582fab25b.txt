To find out how much John Smith paid after splitting the cost of the cakes with his brother, we can follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total cost of the cakes**: 
   John buys 3 cakes, each costing $12. We multiply the number of cakes by the price per cake to find the total cost.
   
   Total cost = Number of cakes × Price per cake
   Total cost = 3 × 12 = 36

   Here, we are multiplying the number of cakes (3) by the price of each cake ($12) to find the total amount he spent on cakes.

2. **Split the total cost with his brother**:
   Since John splits the cost with his brother, we need to divide the total cost by 2. 

   Amount paid by John = Total cost / 2
   Amount paid by John = 36 / 2 = 18

   This step calculates how much John will pay after sharing the total cost equally with his brother.

Therefore, John Smith paid $18 for the cakes.
