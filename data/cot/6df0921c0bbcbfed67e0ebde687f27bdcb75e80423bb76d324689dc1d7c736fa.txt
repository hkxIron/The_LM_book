To find out how many streets both officers patrol in one hour, we need to determine the rate at which each officer patrols the streets and then add their rates together.

1. **Calculate Officer A's rate:**
   - Officer A patrols 36 streets in 4 hours.
   - To find the rate per hour, we divide the number of streets by the number of hours:
     \[
     \text{Rate of Officer A} = \frac{36 \text{ streets}}{4 \text{ hours}} = 9 \text{ streets per hour}
     \]

2. **Calculate Officer B's rate:**
   - Officer B patrols 55 streets in 5 hours.
   - Similarly, we divide the number of streets by the number of hours:
     \[
     \text{Rate of Officer B} = \frac{55 \text{ streets}}{5 \text{ hours}} = 11 \text{ streets per hour}
     \]

3. **Add both officers' rates to find the total rate:**
   - Now we add the rates of both officers to find out how many streets they patrol together in one hour:
     \[
     \text{Total rate} = \text{Rate of Officer A} + \text{Rate of Officer B} = 9 \text{ streets per hour} + 11 \text{ streets per hour} = 20 \text{ streets per hour}
     \]

Thus, both officers together patrol 20 streets in one hour.
