<reasoning>
To determine how long it takes Cindy to travel a full mile by running and walking, we will calculate the time for each segment of her journey separately: the running part and the walking part.

1. **Calculate the time to run half a mile:**
   - Cindy's running speed is 3 miles per hour.
   - To find the time it takes to run half a mile, we use the formula:
     \[
     \text{Time} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Speed}}
     \]
   - Here, the distance is 0.5 miles (half a mile) and the speed is 3 miles per hour.
   - Plugging in these values:
     \[
     \text{Time to run} = \frac{0.5 \text{ miles}}{3 \text{ miles/hour}} = \frac{0.5}{3} \text{ hours}
     \]
   - To convert this to minutes, we multiply by 60 (since there are 60 minutes in an hour):
     \[
     \text{Time to run in minutes} = \frac{0.5}{3} \times 60 = \frac{30}{3} = 10 \text{ minutes}
     \]

2. **Calculate the time to walk half a mile:**
   - Cindy's walking speed is 1 mile per hour.
   - Again using the time formula:
     \[
     \text{Time to walk} = \frac{0.5 \text{ miles}}{1 \text{ mile/hour}} = 0.5 \text{ hours}
     \]
   - Converting this to minutes:
     \[
     \text{Time to walk in minutes} = 0.5 \times 60 = 30 \text{ minutes}
     \]

3. **Add the time for both segments:**
   - Now, we add the time taken to run and walk:
     \[
     \text{Total time} = \text{Time to run} + \text{Time to walk} = 10 \text{ minutes} + 30 \text{ minutes} = 40 \text{ minutes}
     \]

Therefore, it will take Cindy a total of 40 minutes to travel the full mile.
</reasoning>
<answer>40</answer>
