   \[
   \text{Number of sheep} = 10 \times \text{Number of cows} = 10 \times 40 = 400
   \]

   Each cow drinks 80 liters of water daily. 
   \[
   \text{Daily water for cows} = \text{Number of cows} \times \text{Water per cow} = 40 \times 80 = 3200 \text{ liters}
   \]

3. **Calculate the daily water consumption for sheep**:

   \[
   \text{Water per sheep} = \frac{1}{4} \times 80 = 20 \text{ liters}
   \]
   Now, calculate the total daily water consumption for all sheep:
   \[
   \text{Daily water for sheep} = \text{Number of sheep} \times \text{Water per sheep} = 400 \times 20 = 8000 \text{ liters}
   \]

4. **Calculate the total daily water consumption for all animals**: 
   Now we add the daily water consumption of cows and sheep:
   \[
   \text{Total daily water} = \text{Daily water for cows} + \text{Daily water for sheep} = 3200 + 8000 = 11200 \text{ liters}
   \]

5. **Calculate the total weekly water consumption**: 
   Since there are 7 days in a week, we multiply the total daily water consumption by 7:
   \[
   \text{Total weekly water} = \text{Total daily water} \times 7 = 11200 \times 7 = 78400 \text{ liters}
   \]
