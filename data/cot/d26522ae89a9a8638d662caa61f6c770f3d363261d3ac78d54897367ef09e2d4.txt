   \text{Basic Salary} = 7.50 \times 160

   \[
   \text{Commission} = 0.16 \times 25000
   \]
   \[
   \text{Commission} = 4000
   \]

   - Now we add her basic salary and her commission to find her total earnings:
   \[
   \text{Total Monthly Earnings} = \text{Basic Salary} + \text{Commission}

   \text{Total Monthly Earnings} = 1200 + 4000
   \]

   \text{Total Monthly Earnings} = 5200
   \]

   \[
   \text{Budget Allocation} = 0.95 \times 5200
   \]
   \[
   \text{Budget Allocation} = 4940
   \]

5. **Calculate the amount allocated to insurance:**
   - The remaining amount from her total monthly earnings after her budget allocation will be allocated to insurance. We find this by subtracting her budget allocation from her total monthly earnings:
   \[
   \text{Insurance Allocation} = \text{Total Monthly Earnings} - \text{Budget Allocation}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Insurance Allocation} = 5200 - 4940

   \text{Insurance Allocation} = 260
