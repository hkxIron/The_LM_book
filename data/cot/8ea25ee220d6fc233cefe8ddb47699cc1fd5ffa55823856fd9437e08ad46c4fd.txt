To find out how many trees can be planted in the park, we first need to calculate the area of the park in square feet. The area of a rectangle is found by multiplying its length by its width.

1. **Calculate the area of the park**:
   - Length of the park = 1000 feet
   - Width of the park = 2000 feet
   - Area = Length × Width = 1000 feet × 2000 feet

Calculating the area:
Area = 1000 × 2000 = 2,000,000 square feet

2. **Determine how many trees can be planted**:
   - We know that there is 1 tree for every 20 square feet. 
   - To find the total number of trees, we divide the total area of the park by the area required for one tree.

Calculating the number of trees:
Number of trees = Total Area ÷ Area per tree = 2,000,000 square feet ÷ 20 square feet/tree

Calculating the number of trees:
Number of trees = 2,000,000 ÷ 20 = 100,000 trees

Therefore, after calculating the area of the park and the number of trees that can be planted based on the area per tree, we find that the park can accommodate 100,000 trees.
