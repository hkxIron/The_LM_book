<reasoning>
Let's start by calculating the total number of animals in Fred the Farmer's barn before any transactions.

1. **Count the animals:**
   - Horses: 100
   - Sheep: 29
   - Chickens: 9

   Adding these together gives us:
   \[
   \text{Total animals} = 100 + 29 + 9 = 138
   \]

2. **Brian buys half of the animals:**
   To find out how many animals Brian bought, we need to calculate half of the total number of animals:
   \[
   \text{Animals bought by Brian} = \frac{138}{2} = 69
   \]

3. **Calculate the remaining animals after Brian's purchase:**
   After Brian buys 69 animals, we subtract this number from the total:
   \[
   \text{Remaining animals} = 138 - 69 = 69
   \]

4. **Jeremy gifts Fred 37 goats:**
   Now we need to add the goats that Jeremy gifted to Fred:
   \[
   \text{Total animals after the gift} = 69 + 37 = 106
   \]

5. **Determine the number of male animals:**
   According to the problem, exactly half of the animals in the barn are male. So, we calculate half of the total animals now:
   \[
   \text{Male animals} = \frac{106}{2} = 53
   \]

Thus, the total number of male animals in the barn is 53.
</reasoning>
