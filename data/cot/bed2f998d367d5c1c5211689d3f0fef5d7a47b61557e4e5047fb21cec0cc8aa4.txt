<reasoning>
First, we need to determine the total cost of the suit and the shoes before applying the discount.

1. **Calculate the total cost of the suit and shoes:**
   - The price of the suit is $430.
   - The price of the shoes is $190.
   - To find the total cost, we add these two amounts together:
     \[
     \text{Total Cost} = \text{Cost of Suit} + \text{Cost of Shoes} = 430 + 190
     \]
   - Performing the addition:
     \[
     430 + 190 = 620
     \]
   - So, the total cost before the discount is $620.

2. **Apply the discount:**
   - Michael receives a discount of $100 on his total purchase.
   - To find out how much he pays after the discount, we subtract the discount from the total cost:
     \[
     \text{Amount Paid} = \text{Total Cost} - \text{Discount} = 620 - 100
     \]
   - Performing the subtraction:
     \[
     620 - 100 = 520
     \]
   - Therefore, the amount that Michael paid to the seller after the discount is $520.
</reasoning>
<answer>520</answer>
