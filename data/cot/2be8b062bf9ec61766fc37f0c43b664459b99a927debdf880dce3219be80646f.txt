1. **Calculate the steps for a round trip:**
   - Raine takes 150 steps to get to school.
   - Since she also has to walk back home, we multiply the number of steps to school by 2:
     \[
     \text{Steps for one round trip} = 150 \text{ (to school)} + 150 \text{ (back home)} = 300 \text{ steps}
     \]

2. **Calculate the total steps in five days:**
   - Now that we know Raine takes 300 steps for one round trip, we need to find out how many steps she takes in total over the course of five days. We do this by multiplying the number of steps for one round trip by the number of days:
     \[
     \text{Total steps in five days} = 300 \text{ steps} \times 5 \text{ days} = 1500 \text{ steps}
     \]
