   - To find their combined sales, we add the two amounts together:
     \[
     \text{Total sales of Katya and Ricky} = 8 + 9 = 17 \text{ glasses}
     \]

     \[
     \text{Tina's sales} = 2 \times 17 = 34 \text{ glasses}
     \]
   This is a crucial step because it tells us the total number of glasses Tina sold, which is necessary for our final comparison.

3. **Calculate how many more glasses Tina sold than Katya:**
   - Now we need to find the difference in sales between Tina and Katya:
     \[
     \text{Difference} = \text{Tina's sales} - \text{Katya's sales} = 34 - 8 = 26
     \]
   This step allows us to determine the additional amount that Tina sold compared to Katya.

Therefore, Tina sold 26 more glasses of lemonade than Katya.
