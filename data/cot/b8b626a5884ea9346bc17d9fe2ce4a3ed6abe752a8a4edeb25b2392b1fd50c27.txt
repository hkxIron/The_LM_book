   \[
   \text{Revenue from pumpkin pie} = 32 \text{ slices} \times 5 \text{ dollars/slice} = 160 \text{ dollars}
   \]

   This step shows how we can determine the income generated from the pumpkin pie slices.

   \[
   \text{Total custard pie slices} = 5 \text{ pies} \times 6 \text{ slices/pie} = 30 \text{ slices}
   \]

   This calculation gives us the total number of custard pie slices available for sale.

   The price of a slice of custard pie is $6. Now we can calculate the total revenue from selling all the slices of custard pie:

   \[
   \text{Revenue from custard pie} = 30 \text{ slices} \times 6 \text{ dollars/slice} = 180 \text{ dollars}
   \]

   This step allows us to find out how much income is generated from the custard pie slices.

5. **Calculate the total revenue from both types of pies.**  
   Now that we have the revenue from both the pumpkin and custard pies, we can find the total revenue by adding both amounts together:

   \[
   \text{Total revenue} = \text{Revenue from pumpkin pie} + \text{Revenue from custard pie} = 160 \text{ dollars} + 180 \text{ dollars} = 340 \text{ dollars}
