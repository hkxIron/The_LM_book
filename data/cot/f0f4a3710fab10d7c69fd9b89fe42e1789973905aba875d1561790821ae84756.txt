   The sunset time on March 1st is 6:00 PM.

   \[
   \text{Total increase} = 1.2 \text{ minutes/day} \times 40 \text{ days} = 48 \text{ minutes}
   \]

   \[
   \text{New sunset time} = 6:00 \text{ PM} + 48 \text{ minutes} = 6:48 \text{ PM}
   \]

   It is currently 6:10 PM.

5. **Calculate the time until sunset**: 
   We need to find the difference between the new sunset time (6:48 PM) and the current time (6:10 PM):
   \[
   \text{Time until sunset} = 6:48 \text{ PM} - 6:10 \text{ PM}
   \]
   First, we convert both times to minutes from the start of the hour:
   - 6:48 PM = 48 minutes
   - 6:10 PM = 10 minutes
   Now, subtract:
   \[
   \text{Time until sunset} = 48 \text{ minutes} - 10 \text{ minutes} = 38 \text{ minutes}
   \]
