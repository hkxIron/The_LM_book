   \[
   \text{Insurance coverage} = 0.40 \times 90 \text{ dollars} = 36 \text{ dollars}
   \]

   To find out how much John pays out of pocket, we subtract the insurance coverage from the total cost:
   \[
   \text{John's payment} = \text{Total cost} - \text{Insurance coverage} = 90 \text{ dollars} - 36 \text{ dollars} = 54 \text{ dollars}
