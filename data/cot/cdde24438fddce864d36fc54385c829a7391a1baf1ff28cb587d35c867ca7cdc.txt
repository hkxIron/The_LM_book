1. **Calculate the number of pistachios with shells**: 
   We know that 95 percent of the pistachios have shells. 
   - Total pistachios = 80
   - Percentage with shells = 95% (which is 0.95 in decimal form)
   
   To find the number of pistachios with shells, we multiply the total number of pistachios by the percentage that have shells:
   \[
   \text{Pistachios with shells} = 80 \times 0.95 = 76
   \]
   This means there are 76 pistachios that have shells.

2. **Calculate the number of pistachios with opened shells**:
   Now we know that 75 percent of the pistachios that have shells are opened.
   - Percentage with opened shells = 75% (which is 0.75 in decimal form)
   
   We will find the number of opened shells by multiplying the number of pistachios with shells by the percentage that are opened:
   \[
   \text{Pistachios with opened shells} = 76 \times 0.75 = 57
   \]

Therefore, the number of pistachios in the bag that have shells and have an opened shell is 57.
