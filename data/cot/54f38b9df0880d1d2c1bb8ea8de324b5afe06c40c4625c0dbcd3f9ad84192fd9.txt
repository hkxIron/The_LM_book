To find out how many hours Mike trained during the first two weeks, we can break it down into two parts: the first week and the second week.

     \[
     \text{Hours in first week} = 2 \text{ hours/day} \times 7 \text{ days} = 14 \text{ hours}

     \[
     \text{Hours in second week} = 3 \text{ hours/day} \times 7 \text{ days} = 21 \text{ hours}
     \]

3. **Total Training Hours**:
   - Now we add the hours from both weeks to get the total training hours:
     \[
     \text{Total hours} = \text{Hours in first week} + \text{Hours in second week} = 14 \text{ hours} + 21 \text{ hours} = 35 \text{ hours}
     \]
