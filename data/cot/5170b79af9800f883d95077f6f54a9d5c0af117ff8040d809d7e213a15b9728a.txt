   \[
   \text{Cost of juice drinks} = 2 \times 2 = 4

   Now, we need to add the total costs of the pizzas and the juice drinks together.
   \[
   \text{Total cost} = \text{Cost of pizzas} + \text{Cost of juice drinks} = 24 + 4 = 28

   \[
