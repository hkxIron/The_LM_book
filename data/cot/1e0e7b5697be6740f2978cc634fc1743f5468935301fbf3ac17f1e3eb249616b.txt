To determine how many days Jane spent reading the 500-page book, we will break the problem into two parts: the first half of the book and the second half of the book.

1. **Calculate the number of pages in each half of the book:**
   Since the book has 500 pages, the first half consists of 250 pages, and the second half also consists of 250 pages.
   - Pages in first half: \( \frac{500}{2} = 250 \) pages
   - Pages in second half: \( \frac{500}{2} = 250 \) pages

2. **Calculate the time taken to read the first half:**
   Jane reads the first half at a speed of 10 pages per day. To find out how many days it took her to read the first half, we divide the number of pages by her reading speed:
   \[
   \text{Days for first half} = \frac{250 \text{ pages}}{10 \text{ pages/day}} = 25 \text{ days}
   \]

3. **Calculate the time taken to read the second half:**
   Jane reads the second half at a speed of 5 pages per day. Similarly, we calculate the number of days for the second half:
   \[
   \text{Days for second half} = \frac{250 \text{ pages}}{5 \text{ pages/day}} = 50 \text{ days}
   \]

4. **Calculate the total time spent reading the book:**
   To find the total number of days spent reading the entire book, we add the days spent on the first half and the second half:
   \[
   \text{Total days} = \text{Days for first half} + \text{Days for second half} = 25 \text{ days} + 50 \text{ days} = 75 \text{ days}
   \]

Therefore, Jane spent a total of 75 days reading the book.
