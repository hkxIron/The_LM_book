     \[
     5 \text{ medium pizzas} \times 8 \text{ slices per medium pizza} = 40 \text{ slices}
     \]

     \[
     15 \text{ total pizzas} - 4 \text{ small pizzas} - 5 \text{ medium pizzas} = 6 \text{ large pizzas}
     \]

4. **Calculating slices from large pizzas**:
   - Each large pizza has 12 slices, and we have 6 large pizzas.
   - Therefore, the total number of slices from large pizzas is:
     \[
     6 \text{ large pizzas} \times 12 \text{ slices per large pizza} = 72 \text{ slices}
     \]

5. **Calculating the total number of slices**:
   - Now, we will sum up all the slices from the small, medium, and large pizzas:
     \[
     24 \text{ slices from small} + 40 \text{ slices from medium} + 72 \text{ slices from large} = 136 \text{ slices}
