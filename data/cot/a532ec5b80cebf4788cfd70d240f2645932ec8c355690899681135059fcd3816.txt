To solve this problem, we need to determine how many slices of cake were kept after one-fourth of the cake was eaten by Lovely's visitors.

1. **Determine the total number of slices**: Lovely cut her birthday cake into 12 equal pieces. So, we have:
   \[
   \text{Total slices} = 12
   \]

   \[
   \text{Slices eaten} = \frac{1}{4} \times 12 = 3
   \]

3. **Calculate the number of slices kept**: To find out how many slices were kept, we subtract the number of slices eaten from the total number of slices:
   \[
   \text{Slices kept} = \text{Total slices} - \text{Slices eaten} = 12 - 3 = 9
   \]

Therefore, Lovely kept 9 slices of cake.
