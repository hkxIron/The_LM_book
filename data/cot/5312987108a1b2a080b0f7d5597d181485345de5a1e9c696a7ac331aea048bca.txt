To determine how many weeks Carrie needs to work before she can purchase the iPhone, we will follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the net cost of the iPhone after the trade-in:** 
   - The cost of the new iPhone is $800.
   - The trade-in value of her Samsung Galaxy is $240.
   - We subtract the trade-in value from the cost of the iPhone to find the amount she still needs to pay.

   \[
   \text{Net Cost} = \text{Cost of iPhone} - \text{Trade-in Value}
   \]

   Substituting the values:
   \[
   \text{Net Cost} = 800 - 240 = 560
   \]

   So, after trading in her Samsung Galaxy, Carrie needs $560 to buy the iPhone.

2. **Determine how much Carrie earns per week:** 
   - Carrie can earn $80 per week babysitting.

3. **Calculate how many weeks she needs to work to earn the remaining amount:**
   - To find out how many weeks Carrie needs to work, we divide the net cost by her weekly earnings.

   \[
   \text{Weeks Needed} = \frac{\text{Net Cost}}{\text{Weekly Earnings}}
   \]

   Substituting the values:
   \[
   \text{Weeks Needed} = \frac{560}{80} = 7
   \]

   Therefore, Carrie needs to work 7 weeks to earn enough money to buy the iPhone after trading in her Samsung Galaxy.
