<reasoning>
To find out how much money Vincent will earn after five days, we need to calculate the total earnings from both the fantasy books and the literature books over that period.

1. **Calculate the cost of the literature books:**
   Since the literature books cost half the price of fantasy books, we first need to find the price of one literature book.
   - The price of a fantasy book is $4.
   - Therefore, the price of a literature book is: 
     \[
     \text{Price of literature book} = \frac{4}{2} = 2 \text{ dollars}
     \]

2. **Calculate the daily earnings from fantasy books:**
   Vincent sells 5 fantasy books per day. The earnings from fantasy books each day would be:
   \[
   \text{Earnings from fantasy books} = \text{Number of fantasy books sold} \times \text{Price of one fantasy book} = 5 \times 4 = 20 \text{ dollars}
   \]

3. **Calculate the daily earnings from literature books:**
   Vincent also sells 8 literature books per day. The earnings from literature books each day would be:
   \[
   \text{Earnings from literature books} = \text{Number of literature books sold} \times \text{Price of one literature book} = 8 \times 2 = 16 \text{ dollars}
   \]

4. **Calculate the total daily earnings:**
   Now, we add the earnings from both types of books to find out the total earnings for one day:
   \[
   \text{Total daily earnings} = \text{Earnings from fantasy books} + \text{Earnings from literature books} = 20 + 16 = 36 \text{ dollars}
   \]

5. **Calculate the total earnings after five days:**
   Finally, to find out how much he will earn in five days, we multiply the total daily earnings by 5:
   \[
   \text{Total earnings after 5 days} = \text{Total daily earnings} \times 5 = 36 \times 5 = 180 \text{ dollars}
   \]

Therefore, after carrying out all the steps, we conclude that Vincent will earn a total of $180 after five days.
