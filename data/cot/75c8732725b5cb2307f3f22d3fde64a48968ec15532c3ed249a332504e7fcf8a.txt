<reasoning>
First, we need to determine how much money Alfonso still needs to save in order to buy the mountain bike helmet. The helmet costs $340, and he already has $40. 

1. Calculate the remaining amount needed:
   \[
   \text{Remaining amount} = \text{Cost of helmet} - \text{Amount saved}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Remaining amount} = 340 - 40 = 300
   \]
   So, Alfonso needs to save an additional $300.

Next, we calculate how much Alfonso earns each week by walking his aunt's dog.

2. Calculate his weekly earnings:
   \[
   \text{Earnings per day} = 6
   \]
   \[
   \text{Days worked per week} = 5
   \]
   \[
   \text{Weekly earnings} = \text{Earnings per day} \times \text{Days worked per week}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Weekly earnings} = 6 \times 5 = 30
   \]
   Therefore, Alfonso earns $30 each week.

Now, we need to determine how many weeks it will take for Alfonso to save the remaining $300.

3. Calculate the number of weeks needed:
   \[
   \text{Weeks needed} = \frac{\text{Remaining amount}}{\text{Weekly earnings}}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Weeks needed} = \frac{300}{30} = 10
   \]
   Therefore, Alfonso needs to work for 10 weeks to save up the additional amount needed for the helmet.
