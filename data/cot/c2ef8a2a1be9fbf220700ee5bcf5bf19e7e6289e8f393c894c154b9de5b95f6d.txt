<reasoning>
To find out how much money Roger still owes on his house, we will follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the down payment**: 
   Roger paid 20% of the total house price as a down payment. 
   \[
   \text{Down payment} = 20\% \text{ of } 100,000 = 0.20 \times 100,000 = 20,000
   \]
   This is the amount he paid upfront.

2. **Calculate the remaining balance after the down payment**: 
   After the down payment, we need to find out how much he has left to pay on the house.
   \[
   \text{Remaining balance} = \text{Total price} - \text{Down payment} = 100,000 - 20,000 = 80,000
   \]
   So, the remaining balance on the house is $80,000.

3. **Calculate how much his parents paid**: 
   Roger's parents paid off 30% of the remaining balance.
   \[
   \text{Parents' payment} = 30\% \text{ of } 80,000 = 0.30 \times 80,000 = 24,000
   \]
   This amount is what his parents contributed towards the house.

4. **Calculate how much Roger still owes**: 
   To find out how much Roger still owes, we subtract the amount his parents paid from the remaining balance.
   \[
   \text{Amount owed by Roger} = \text{Remaining balance} - \text{Parents' payment} = 80,000 - 24,000 = 56,000
   \]
   Therefore, the amount Roger still owes on his house is $56,000.
