   \[
   \text{Number of black cats} = 25\% \times 16 = \frac{25}{100} \times 16 = 0.25 \times 16 = 4
   \]

4. **Calculate the number of grey cats**: Now we can find the number of grey cats by subtracting the number of white and black cats from the total:
   \[
   \text{Number of grey cats} = \text{Total cats} - (\text{Number of white cats} + \text{Number of black cats})
   \]
   Plugging in the numbers:
   \[
   \text{Number of grey cats} = 16 - (2 + 4) = 16 - 6 = 10
   \]

Thus, there are 10 grey cats in the house.
