To determine how many more vacation days Andrew can still take, we can follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of vacation days Andrew earned:**

   \[
   \text{Vacation days earned} = \frac{\text{Days worked}}{10} = \frac{300}{10} = 30 \text{ vacation days}
   \]

   Andrew took 5 days off in March and twice as many in September. First, we calculate how many days he took in September:

   \[
   \text{Days taken in September} = 2 \times \text{Days taken in March} = 2 \times 5 = 10 \text{ days}
   \]

   Now, we can find the total vacation days taken:

   \[
   \text{Total vacation days taken} = \text{Days taken in March} + \text{Days taken in September} = 5 + 10 = 15 \text{ days}
   \]

3. **Determine the remaining vacation days:**
   To find out how many more vacation days Andrew can take, we subtract the total vacation days taken from the total vacation days earned:

   \[
   \text{Remaining vacation days} = \text{Vacation days earned} - \text{Total vacation days taken} = 30 - 15 = 15 \text{ days}
   \]

Therefore, Andrew can still take 15 more vacation days.
