<reasoning>
To find out how much Jewel will gain from selling the magazines, we need to calculate both the total cost of the magazines and the total revenue from selling them. Then we can find the gain by subtracting the total cost from the total revenue.

1. **Calculate the total cost of the magazines:**
   Jewel bought 10 magazines at a cost of $3 each. 
   To find the total cost, we multiply the number of magazines by the cost per magazine:
   \[
   \text{Total Cost} = \text{Number of Magazines} \times \text{Cost per Magazine} = 10 \times 3 = 30
   \]
   So, the total cost of the magazines is $30.

2. **Calculate the total revenue from selling the magazines:**
   Jewel plans to sell each magazine for $3.50. 
   To find the total revenue, we multiply the number of magazines by the selling price per magazine:
   \[
   \text{Total Revenue} = \text{Number of Magazines} \times \text{Selling Price per Magazine} = 10 \times 3.50 = 35
   \]
   Therefore, the total revenue from selling the magazines is $35.

3. **Calculate the gain:**
   Gain is calculated by subtracting the total cost from the total revenue:
   \[
   \text{Gain} = \text{Total Revenue} - \text{Total Cost} = 35 - 30 = 5
   \]
   Thus, Jewel's gain from selling the magazines is $5.
</reasoning>
<answer>5</answer>
