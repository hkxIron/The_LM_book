To determine how much you will give your friend after agreeing to return the borrowed money with a 10% increase, we will follow these steps:

1. **Identify the original amount borrowed**: You borrowed $100.

2. **Calculate the increase**: Since you agreed to return the money with a 10% increase, we need to find 10% of $100. 
   - To calculate 10%, we multiply the original amount by 0.10 (which is the decimal equivalent of 10%).
   - Calculation: \( 100 \times 0.10 = 10 \)

3. **Add the increase to the original amount**: Now, we add the increase back to the original amount to find the total amount to be returned.
   - Calculation: \( 100 + 10 = 110 \)

Thus, you will give your friend a total of $110.
