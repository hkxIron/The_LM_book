     \[
     \text{Cost for adults} = 2 \text{ adults} \times 22 \text{ dollars/adult} = 44 \text{ dollars}
     \]

   - There are 2 children, and each child ticket costs $7.
   - So, we multiply the number of child tickets by the cost of each ticket:
     \[
     \text{Cost for children} = 2 \text{ children} \times 7 \text{ dollars/child} = 14 \text{ dollars}
     \]

3. **Calculate the total cost for the family:**
   - Now, we add the cost for adults and the cost for children to find the total admission price:
     \[
     \text{Total cost} = \text{Cost for adults} + \text{Cost for children} = 44 \text{ dollars} + 14 \text{ dollars} = 58 \text{ dollars}
     \]
