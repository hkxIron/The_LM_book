<reasoning>
To find out how much John makes per day from his website visits, we will first calculate his total earnings for the month and then divide that by the number of days in the month.

1. **Calculate Total Earnings for the Month**:
   - John receives $0.01 (1 cent) for each visit to his website.
   - He gets 30,000 visits per month.
   - To find the total earnings for the month, we multiply the number of visits by the earnings per visit:
     \[
     \text{Total Earnings} = \text{Number of Visits} \times \text{Earnings per Visit}
     \]
     \[
     \text{Total Earnings} = 30000 \times 0.01 = 300
     \]
   - So, John makes $300 in total for the month.

2. **Calculate Daily Earnings**:
   - Now, we need to find out how much he makes per day. Since we are considering a normal month with 30 days, we divide the total monthly earnings by 30:
     \[
     \text{Daily Earnings} = \frac{\text{Total Earnings}}{\text{Number of Days in Month}}
     \]
     \[
     \text{Daily Earnings} = \frac{300}{30} = 10
     \]
   - Therefore, John makes $10 per day from his website visits.
</reasoning>
<answer>10</answer>
