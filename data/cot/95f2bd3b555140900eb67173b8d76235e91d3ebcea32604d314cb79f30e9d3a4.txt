1. **Determine the total number of jumps he needs to make.**

   - Mark can jump 3 times a second.

3. **Calculate how many seconds it will take him to reach 54,000 jumps.**

     \[
     \text{Time in seconds} = \frac{\text{Total jumps}}{\text{Jumps per second}} = \frac{54,000}{3} = 18,000 \text{ seconds}
     \]

   - There are 3600 seconds in one hour. To convert seconds to hours, we divide the total seconds by 3600:
     \[
     \text{Time in hours} = \frac{\text{Time in seconds}}{3600} = \frac{18,000}{3600}
     \]

5. **Perform the division to find the time in hours:**
   \[
   \frac{18,000}{3600} = 5 \text{ hours}
   \]
