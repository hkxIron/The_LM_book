To find out how many lambs Merry and her brother have together, we start by identifying how many lambs each of them is responsible for.

1. **Identify Merry's lambs**: We know from the problem that Merry is taking care of 10 lambs.

2. **Determine how many lambs her brother has**: The problem states that her brother takes care of 3 more lambs than Merry. Since Merry has 10 lambs, we add 3 to that number to find out how many lambs her brother has:
   \[
   \text{Brother's lambs} = \text{Merry's lambs} + 3 = 10 + 3 = 13
   \]

3. **Calculate the total number of lambs**: Now, we add the number of lambs Merry has and the number of lambs her brother has to find the total:
   \[
   \text{Total lambs} = \text{Merry's lambs} + \text{Brother's lambs} = 10 + 13 = 23
   \]
