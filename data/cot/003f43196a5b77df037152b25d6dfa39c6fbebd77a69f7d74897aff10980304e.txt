To find out how many pages Jane will read in a week, we can break this down step by step.

   - In the evening, she reads 10 pages.

     \[
     5 \text{ pages (morning)} + 10 \text{ pages (evening)} = 15 \text{ pages per day}
     \]

   \[
   15 \text{ pages per day} \times 7 \text{ days} = 105 \text{ pages in a week}
   \]

Thus, Jane will read a total of 105 pages in a week.
