1. **Calculate the number of boys:**
   We know that 30% of the 50 people at the party are boys. To find the number of boys, we can use the formula:
   \[
   \text{Number of boys} = \text{Total people} \times \text{Percentage of boys}
   \]
   Here, the total number of people is 50, and the percentage of boys is 30%, which we convert to a decimal (30% = 0.30).

   Substituting the values, we get:
   \[
   \text{Number of boys} = 50 \times 0.30 = 15
   \]

2. **Calculate the number of girls:**
   Now that we know there are 15 boys at the party, we can find the number of girls by subtracting the number of boys from the total number of people:
   \[
   \text{Number of girls} = \text{Total people} - \text{Number of boys}
   \]
   Substituting the values, we have:
   \[
   \text{Number of girls} = 50 - 15 = 35
   \]

Therefore, there are 35 girls at the party.
