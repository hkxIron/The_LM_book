   \[
   \text{Profit per orange} = \text{Selling price} - \text{Cost price}
   \]
   Plugging in the values:
   \[
   \text{Profit per orange} = 60 - 50
   \]

   \[
   \text{Profit per orange} = 10 \text{ cents}
   \]
