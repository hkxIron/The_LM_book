   \]

   - First, divide 200 by 5:
   \[

   - Then, multiply the result by 4:
   \[
   40 \times 4 = 160
   \]

   \[
   \text{Amount left} = 200 - 160
   \]

   Performing the subtraction:
   \[
   200 - 160 = 40
   \]
