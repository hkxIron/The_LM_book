To find out how many hours Jean's business is open in a week, we need to calculate the total hours for both weekdays (Monday through Friday) and weekends (Saturday and Sunday) separately. Then we will add those two totals together.

### Step 1: Calculate weekday hours
Jean's business operates from 4 pm to 10 pm on weekdays. 

- **Calculate the total hours for weekdays:**
  - From 4 pm to 10 pm is 6 hours (10 - 4 = 6).
  - Since the business is open from Monday to Friday, we have 5 weekdays.

Now, we multiply the number of hours per day by the number of weekdays:
- Total weekday hours = 6 hours/day * 5 days = 30 hours.

### Step 2: Calculate weekend hours
On weekends, the business operates from 6 pm to 10 pm.

- **Calculate the total hours for weekends:**
  - From 6 pm to 10 pm is 4 hours (10 - 6 = 4).
  - Since the business is open on both Saturday and Sunday, we have 2 weekend days.

Now, we multiply the number of hours per day by the number of weekend days:
- Total weekend hours = 4 hours/day * 2 days = 8 hours.

### Step 3: Calculate the total hours for the week
Now we add the total hours from weekdays and weekends:
- Total hours for the week = Total weekday hours + Total weekend hours = 30 hours + 8 hours = 38 hours.

Thus, Jean's business is open for a total of 38 hours in a week.
