     \[
     \text{Total teeth} = \text{Tiger shark teeth} + \text{Hammerhead teeth} = 180 + 30
     \]
   - Performing the addition:
     \[
     \text{Total teeth} = 210
     \]

3. **Determine the number of teeth in a great white shark.**
   - According to the problem, a great white shark has double the sum of teeth of a tiger shark and a hammerhead shark.
   - Therefore, we calculate the number of teeth for the great white shark as follows:
     \[
     \text{Great white teeth} = 2 \times \text{Total teeth} = 2 \times 210
     \]
   - Performing the multiplication:
     \[
     \text{Great white teeth} = 420
     \]
