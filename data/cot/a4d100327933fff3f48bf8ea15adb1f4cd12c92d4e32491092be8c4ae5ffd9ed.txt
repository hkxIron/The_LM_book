To solve this problem, we will first determine how many players on Ben's hockey team are girls. 

1. **Calculate the number of boys on the team:** 
   The team is composed of 60% boys. To find the number of boys, we calculate 60% of 50 players. 

   \[
   \text{Number of boys} = 0.60 \times 50 = 30
   \]

2. **Calculate the number of girls on the team:** 
   Since the rest of the team consists of girls, we subtract the number of boys from the total number of players.

   \[
   \text{Number of girls} = 50 - 30 = 20
   \]

3. **Determine how many of the girls are juniors:** 
   We know that half of the girls are juniors and the other half are seniors. Since there are 20 girls, we will calculate half of that:

   \[
   \text{Number of junior girls} = \frac{20}{2} = 10
   \]

Thus, the total number of junior girls on Ben's hockey team is 10.
