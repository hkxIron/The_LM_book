<reasoning>
To find the total daily salary of all the employees in the grocery store, we need to calculate the daily salary for both the managers and the clerks separately, and then sum these amounts.

1. **Calculate the total salary for the managers:**
   - The daily salary of one manager is $5.
   - There are 2 managers employed.
   - Therefore, the total salary for the managers is calculated as follows:
   
   \[
   \text{Total salary for managers} = \text{Number of managers} \times \text{Salary per manager}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Total salary for managers} = 2 \times 5 = 10
   \]

2. **Calculate the total salary for the clerks:**
   - The daily salary of one clerk is $2.
   - There are 3 clerks employed.
   - Therefore, the total salary for the clerks is calculated as follows:
   
   \[
   \text{Total salary for clerks} = \text{Number of clerks} \times \text{Salary per clerk}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Total salary for clerks} = 3 \times 2 = 6
   \]

3. **Calculate the total daily salary for all employees:**
   - Now we need to add the total salaries of the managers and clerks together:
   
   \[
   \text{Total daily salary} = \text{Total salary for managers} + \text{Total salary for clerks}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Total daily salary} = 10 + 6 = 16
   \]

Therefore, the total daily salary of all the employees of the grocery store is $16.
</reasoning>
