To determine how many weeks the watermelons will last, we first need to calculate how many watermelons Jeremy consumes and gives away each week.

1. **Calculate the total number of watermelons eaten and given away per week:**
   - Jeremy eats 3 watermelons per week.
   - He gives away 2 watermelons to his dad per week.
   - Therefore, the total number of watermelons consumed or given away each week is:
     \[
     3 \text{ (eaten)} + 2 \text{ (given away)} = 5 \text{ watermelons per week}
     \]

2. **Calculate the total number of weeks the watermelons will last:**
   - Jeremy starts with 30 watermelons.
   - Since he consumes or gives away 5 watermelons per week, we can find out how many weeks the watermelons will last by dividing the total number of watermelons by the number he goes through each week:
     \[
     \frac{30 \text{ watermelons}}{5 \text{ watermelons per week}} = 6 \text{ weeks}
     \]

Thus, the watermelons will last for 6 weeks.
