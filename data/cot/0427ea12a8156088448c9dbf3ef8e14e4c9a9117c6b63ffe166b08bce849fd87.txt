<reasoning>
To determine how many miles Mira can jog in five days, we will first calculate the distance she jogs in one day and then multiply that by five.

1. **Calculate the distance jogged in one day:**
   - Mira jogs at a speed of 5 miles per hour.
   - She jogs for 2 hours each morning.
   - To find the distance she jogs in one day, we use the formula: 
     \[
     \text{Distance} = \text{Speed} \times \text{Time}
     \]
   - Plugging in the values:
     \[
     \text{Distance} = 5 \text{ miles/hour} \times 2 \text{ hours} = 10 \text{ miles}
     \]
   - Therefore, Mira jogs 10 miles each morning.

2. **Calculate the distance jogged in five days:**
   - Now that we know she jogs 10 miles each day, we can calculate the total distance for five days.
   - We multiply the daily distance by the number of days:
     \[
     \text{Total Distance} = 10 \text{ miles/day} \times 5 \text{ days} = 50 \text{ miles}
     \]

Thus, after calculating the distance for one day and then multiplying it by the number of days, we find the total distance jogged in five days.
</reasoning>
<answer>50</answer>
