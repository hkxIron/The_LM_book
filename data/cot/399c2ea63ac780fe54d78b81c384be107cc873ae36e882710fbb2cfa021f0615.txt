   - Finally, he walks 11 blocks to get back home.

   We add these distances together to find the total distance for one trip:
   \[
   4 \text{ blocks} + 7 \text{ blocks} + 11 \text{ blocks} = 22 \text{ blocks}
   \]

   - Since Ray walks his dog 3 times each day, we multiply the distance of one complete trip by the number of trips:
   \[
   22 \text{ blocks} \times 3 = 66 \text{ blocks}
   \]
