To find out what percentage towards his goal Calvin is, we first need to determine the total number of haircuts he needs to reach his goal. He has already had 8 haircuts and needs 2 more.

   \[
   \text{Total haircuts needed} = \text{haircuts already had} + \text{haircuts still needed} = 8 + 2 = 10
   \]
   Here, we simply added the number of haircuts he has had (8) to the number he still needs (2) to find the total.

2. **Calculate the percentage of the goal already achieved:**
   To find the percentage, we use the formula:
   \[
   \text{Percentage towards goal} = \left( \frac{\text{haircuts already had}}{\text{total haircuts needed}} \right) \times 100
   \]
   Substituting in the numbers we calculated:
   \[
   \text{Percentage towards goal} = \left( \frac{8}{10} \right) \times 100
   \]
   This gives us the fraction of the goal he has achieved, which we then multiply by 100 to convert it to a percentage.

3. **Perform the calculation:**
   \[
   \frac{8}{10} = 0.8
   \]

   \[
   0.8 \times 100 = 80
   \]
   Therefore, this means Calvin has achieved 80% of his goal.
