To determine how long Jerome will ride on the 13th day, we first need to calculate how much distance he will cover in the first 12 days of riding.

     \[
     \text{Total distance in 12 days} = 12 \text{ miles/day} \times 12 \text{ days} = 144 \text{ miles}
     \]

2. **Determine how much more distance he needs to cover on the 13th day:**
   - Jerome's overall goal for the trip is 150 miles.
   - We now subtract the distance he will have ridden after 12 days from his total goal:
     \[
     \text{Distance remaining} = 150 \text{ miles} - 144 \text{ miles} = 6 \text{ miles}
     \]

3. **Conclusion:**
   - Therefore, on the 13th day, Jerome needs to ride the remaining distance of 6 miles to complete his 150-mile goal.
