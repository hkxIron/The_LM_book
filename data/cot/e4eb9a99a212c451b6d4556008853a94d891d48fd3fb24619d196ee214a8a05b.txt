<reasoning>
To solve how many oysters Bob can shuck in 2 hours, we first need to determine his shucking rate in oysters per minute. 

1. **Calculate the rate of shucking:** 
   Bob can shuck 10 oysters in 5 minutes. 
   To find the rate, we divide the number of oysters by the time in minutes:
   \[
   \text{Rate} = \frac{10 \text{ oysters}}{5 \text{ minutes}} = 2 \text{ oysters per minute}
   \]
   This means Bob shucks 2 oysters every minute.

2. **Convert 2 hours to minutes:**
   We know there are 60 minutes in 1 hour, so:
   \[
   2 \text{ hours} = 2 \times 60 = 120 \text{ minutes}
   \]

3. **Calculate the total number of oysters shucked in 120 minutes:**
   Now that we know Bob's rate is 2 oysters per minute, we can find out how many oysters he can shuck in 120 minutes:
   \[
   \text{Total oysters} = \text{Rate} \times \text{Time} = 2 \text{ oysters per minute} \times 120 \text{ minutes} = 240 \text{ oysters}
   \]

Therefore, Bob can shuck a total of 240 oysters in 2 hours.
