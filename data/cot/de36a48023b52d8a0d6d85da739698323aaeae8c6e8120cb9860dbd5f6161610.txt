   \[
   \text{Total hours} = \frac{\text{Total minutes}}{60} = \frac{600}{60} = 10 \text{ hours}
   \]

   John wants to finish the show in 5 days, so we divide the total hours by the number of days:
   \[
   \text{Hours per day} = \frac{\text{Total hours}}{\text{Number of days}} = \frac{10}{5} = 2 \text{ hours}
   \]
