To find the total number of baskets made during the game, we first need to calculate how many baskets each player made based on their points scored.

     \[
     \text{Baskets by Shawn} = \frac{6 \text{ points}}{3 \text{ points per basket}} = 2 \text{ baskets}
     \]

3. **Calculate total baskets made**: 
   - Now that we have the number of baskets made by both Matthew and Shawn, we can find the total.
   - We add the baskets made by both players:
     \[
     \text{Total baskets} = \text{Baskets by Matthew} + \text{Baskets by Shawn} = 3 + 2 = 5 \text{ baskets}
     \]

Therefore, the total number of baskets made during the game is 5.
