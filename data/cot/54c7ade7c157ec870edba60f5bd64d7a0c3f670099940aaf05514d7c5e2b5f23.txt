To find out how much money Mara and Riley spent at the carnival, we need to calculate the cost for each ride they took and then sum these amounts.

1. **Calculate the cost for Mara's bumper car rides:**
   - Mara rode the bumper car 2 times, and each ticket costs $2.
   - Cost for bumper car rides = Number of rides × Cost per ride
   - Cost for bumper car rides = 2 × $2 = $4

   - Riley rode the space shuttle 4 times, and each ticket costs $4.

   - Cost for space shuttle rides = 4 × $4 = $16

3. **Calculate the cost for both of them riding the Ferris wheel:**
   - They both rode the Ferris wheel 3 times, and each ticket costs $5.
   - Total rides for Ferris wheel = 3 rides × 2 people = 6 rides
   - Cost for Ferris wheel rides = Number of rides × Cost per ride
   - Cost for Ferris wheel rides = 6 × $5 = $30

4. **Now, sum up all the costs to find the total amount spent:**
   - Total cost = Cost for bumper car rides + Cost for space shuttle rides + Cost for Ferris wheel rides
   - Total cost = $4 + $16 + $30 = $50
