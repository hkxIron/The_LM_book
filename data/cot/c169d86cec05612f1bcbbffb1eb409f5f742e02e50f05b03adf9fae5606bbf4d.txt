To find out how many toy cars Bobby will have in three years, we need to calculate the growth of his toy cars year by year, using the 50% increase each year. 

1. **Initial amount of toy cars:** Bobby starts with 16 toy cars.

2. **Calculating the increase for the first year:**
   - A 50% increase means we take 50% of 16 and add it to the original amount.
   - 50% of 16 is calculated as:
     \[
     0.50 \times 16 = 8
     \]
   - Therefore, after the first year, Bobby will have:
     \[
     16 + 8 = 24 \text{ toy cars}
     \]

3. **Calculating the increase for the second year:**
   - Now, we start with 24 toy cars and apply the same 50% increase.
   - 50% of 24 is:
     \[
     0.50 \times 24 = 12
     \]
   - After the second year, Bobby will have:
     \[
     24 + 12 = 36 \text{ toy cars}
     \]

4. **Calculating the increase for the third year:**
   - Starting with 36 toy cars, we apply the 50% increase again.
   - 50% of 36 is:
     \[
     0.50 \times 36 = 18
     \]
   - After the third year, Bobby will have:
     \[
     36 + 18 = 54 \text{ toy cars}
     \]

Overall, we have calculated the number of toy cars Bobby will have at the end of each year, leading to the final total after three years.

Therefore, Bobby will have a total of 54 toy cars after three years.
