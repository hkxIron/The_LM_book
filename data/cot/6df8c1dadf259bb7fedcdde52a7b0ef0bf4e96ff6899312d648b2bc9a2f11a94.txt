To determine how much more money Maisy will earn at her new job compared to her current job, we need to calculate her weekly earnings from both jobs.

**Step 1: Calculate weekly earnings at the current job.**

Maisy works 8 hours a week at a wage of $10 per hour. 

To find her weekly earnings, we multiply the number of hours worked by the wage per hour:

\[ 
\text{Current Job Earnings} = \text{Hours} \times \text{Wage} 
\]

Substituting the values:

\[ 
\text{Current Job Earnings} = 8 \, \text{hours} \times 10 \, \text{dollars/hour} = 80 \, \text{dollars} 
\]

**Step 2: Calculate weekly earnings at the new job.**

At the new job, Maisy works 4 hours a week at a wage of $15 per hour and receives an additional bonus of $35 if she exceeds her quota. 

First, calculate her earnings from the hours worked:

\[ 
\text{New Job Earnings from Hours} = \text{Hours} \times \text{Wage} 
\]

Substituting the values:

\[ 
\text{New Job Earnings from Hours} = 4 \, \text{hours} \times 15 \, \text{dollars/hour} = 60 \, \text{dollars} 
\]

Now, add the bonus:

\[ 
\text{Total New Job Earnings} = \text{Earnings from Hours} + \text{Bonus} 
\]
\[ 
\text{Total New Job Earnings} = 60 \, \text{dollars} + 35 \, \text{dollars} = 95 \, \text{dollars} 
\]

**Step 3: Calculate the difference in earnings between the new job and the current job.**

Now, we subtract the earnings from the current job from the earnings at the new job:

\[ 
\text{Difference in Earnings} = \text{Total New Job Earnings} - \text{Current Job Earnings} 
\]
\[ 
\text{Difference in Earnings} = 95 \, \text{dollars} - 80 \, \text{dollars} = 15 \, \text{dollars} 
\]

Therefore, Maisy will earn $15 more at her new job compared to her current job.
