   \[
   5x + 2y + 25 = 55
   \]

   \[
   5x + 2\left(\frac{1}{2}x\right) + 25 = 55
   \]
   - This simplifies to:
   \[
   5x + x + 25 = 55
   \]

5. **Combine like terms:**
   - Combine \( 5x \) and \( x \):
   \[
   6x + 25 = 55
   \]

6. **Solve for \( x \):**
   - Now, we will isolate \( x \) by first subtracting 25 from both sides:
   \[
   6x = 55 - 25
   \]
   \[
   6x = 30
   \]

   \[
   x = \frac{30}{6} = 5
