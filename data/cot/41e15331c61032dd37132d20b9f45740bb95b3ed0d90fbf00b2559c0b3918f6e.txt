To find out how many tires are in the parking lot, we need to consider both the tires on the cars and the spare tires.

   \]

   \[

   \[
   \text{Total spare tires} = \text{Number of cars} \times \text{Spare tire per car} = 30 \times 1
   \]
   Calculating this gives:
   \[
   30 \times 1 = 30
   \]
   So, there are 30 spare tires.

3. **Calculate the total number of tires in the parking lot**: To find the total number of tires, we add the total tires on the cars and the total spare tires together.
   \[
   \text{Total tires} = \text{Total tires on cars} + \text{Total spare tires} = 120 + 30
   \]
   Calculating this gives:
   \[
   120 + 30 = 150
   \]
