To find out how many dollars more the adults' tickets cost in total than the children's tickets, we will follow these steps:

     \[
     \text{Total cost for adults} = \text{Number of adults} \times \text{Cost per adult ticket} = 9 \times 11
     \]

     \[
     9 \times 11 = 99
     \]

     \[
     \text{Total cost for children} = \text{Number of children} \times \text{Cost per children's ticket} = 7 \times 7
     \]
   - Performing the multiplication:
     \[
     7 \times 7 = 49
     \]
   - Thus, the total cost for the children's tickets is $49.

3. **Determine how many dollars more the adults' tickets cost than the children's tickets.**
   - We find the difference between the total cost of the adult tickets and the total cost of the children's tickets:
     \[
     \text{Difference} = \text{Total cost for adults} - \text{Total cost for children} = 99 - 49
     \]
   - Performing the subtraction:
     \[
     99 - 49 = 50
     \]
   - Therefore, the adults' tickets cost $50 more than the children's tickets.
