To find out how much Sophie spends in total, we need to calculate the cost of each type of item she buys and then add those costs together.

1. **Calculate the cost of the cupcakes**:
   Sophie buys 5 cupcakes at $2 each.

   \text{Cost of cupcakes} = 5 \times 2 = 10
   \]

   She buys 6 doughnuts at $1 each. 
   \[
   \text{Cost of doughnuts} = 6 \times 1 = 6
   \]

   Sophie buys 4 slices of apple pie at $2 per slice. 
   \[
   \text{Cost of apple pie} = 4 \times 2 = 8
   \]

4. **Calculate the cost of the cookies**:
   She buys 15 cookies at $0.60 each. 
   \[
   \text{Cost of cookies} = 15 \times 0.60 = 9
   \]

5. **Add all the costs together**:
   Now we can add the costs of all the items to find the total amount spent.
   \[
   \text{Total Cost} = \text{Cost of cupcakes} + \text{Cost of doughnuts} + \text{Cost of apple pie} + \text{Cost of cookies}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Total Cost} = 10 + 6 + 8 + 9 = 33
   \]
