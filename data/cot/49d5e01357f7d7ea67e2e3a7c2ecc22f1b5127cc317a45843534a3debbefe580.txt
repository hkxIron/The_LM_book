<reasoning>
To find out how many pages Bart bought, we will first determine how many notepads he purchased with his $10. 

1. **Calculate the number of notepads Bart can buy:**
   - Each notepad costs $1.25.
   - We divide the total amount of money Bart spent by the price of each notepad to find how many he bought.
   
   \[
   \text{Number of notepads} = \frac{\text{Total amount spent}}{\text{Price per notepad}} = \frac{10}{1.25}
   \]

2. **Perform the division:**
   - To make the division easier, we can convert $1.25 into a fraction: \( \frac{125}{100} \) or simply keep it as 1.25. 
   - Dividing 10 by 1.25 can also be done by multiplying by the reciprocal (which is \( \frac{4}{5} \)):

   \[
   \frac{10}{1.25} = 10 \times \frac{4}{5} = 8
   \]

   So, Bart bought 8 notepads.

3. **Calculate the total number of pages:**
   - Each notepad has 60 pages.
   - To find the total number of pages Bart bought, we multiply the number of notepads by the number of pages per notepad:

   \[
   \text{Total pages} = \text{Number of notepads} \times \text{Pages per notepad} = 8 \times 60
   \]

4. **Perform the multiplication:**
   \[
   8 \times 60 = 480
   \]

Therefore, Bart bought a total of 480 pages.
</reasoning>
