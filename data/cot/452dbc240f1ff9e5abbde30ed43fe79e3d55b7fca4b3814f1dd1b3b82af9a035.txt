   \[

   \[

   \[
   \text{Total spent} = \text{Amount spent on chocolates} + \text{Amount spent on fruits}

   \[
   \text{Total spent} = 50 + 180 = 230
   \]

   To find out how much money Joe has left, we subtract the total amount spent from his total pocket money:
   \[
   \text{Remaining money} = \text{Total pocket money} - \text{Total spent}
