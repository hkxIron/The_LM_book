   A fifth of the remaining balls are blue.
   - Number of blue balls = \( \frac{1}{5} \times 270 \)

   \[
   \frac{1}{5} \times 270 = 54
   \]
   Therefore, there are 54 blue balls.

4. **Calculate the total number of balls that are either red or blue:**
   To find out how many balls are either red or blue, we add the number of red and blue balls:
   \[
   \text{Total red and blue balls} = \text{Red balls} + \text{Blue balls}
   \]
   \[
   90 + 54 = 144
   \]

5. **Calculate the number of balls that are neither red nor blue:**
   Now, we subtract the total number of red and blue balls from the total number of balls.
   - Balls that are neither red nor blue = Total balls - (Red balls + Blue balls)
   \[
   360 - 144 = 216
   \]
