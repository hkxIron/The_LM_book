<reasoning>
To determine how much money Julia is short for buying the CDs, we need to calculate the total cost of the CDs she wants to buy.

1. **Calculate the cost of each type of CD:**
   - Rock and roll CDs: \(4 \text{ CDs} \times 5 \text{ dollars each} = 20 \text{ dollars}\)
   - Pop CDs: \(4 \text{ CDs} \times 10 \text{ dollars each} = 40 \text{ dollars}\)
   - Dance CDs: \(4 \text{ CDs} \times 3 \text{ dollars each} = 12 \text{ dollars}\)
   - Country CDs: \(4 \text{ CDs} \times 7 \text{ dollars each} = 28 \text{ dollars}\)

2. **Add the total costs together:**
   - Total cost = Cost of rock and roll CDs + Cost of pop CDs + Cost of dance CDs + Cost of country CDs
   - Total cost = \(20 + 40 + 12 + 28\)

3. **Perform the addition:**
   - \(20 + 40 = 60\)
   - \(60 + 12 = 72\)
   - \(72 + 28 = 100\)

4. **Determine how much money Julia has and how much she is short:**
   - Julia has \(75\) dollars.
   - Total cost of CDs = \(100\) dollars.
   - Amount short = Total cost - Amount she has = \(100 - 75\)

5. **Calculate the amount she is short:**
   - \(100 - 75 = 25\)

Therefore, Julia is short by 25 dollars.
