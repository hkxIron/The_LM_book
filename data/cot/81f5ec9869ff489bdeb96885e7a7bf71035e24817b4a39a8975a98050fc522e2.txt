To find out how much money the taco truck made during the lunch rush, we need to calculate the total sales from both the family and the subsequent customers.

1. **Calculate the total cost for the family:**
   - The family bought 4 hard shell tacos and 3 soft tacos.
   - The cost of one hard shell taco is $5, and the cost of one soft taco is $2.
   
   First, we calculate the total cost for the hard shell tacos:
   \[
   \text{Cost for hard shell tacos} = 4 \text{ tacos} \times 5 \text{ dollars/taco} = 20 \text{ dollars}
   \]
   
   Next, we calculate the total cost for the soft tacos:
   \[
   \text{Cost for soft tacos} = 3 \text{ tacos} \times 2 \text{ dollars/taco} = 6 \text{ dollars}
   \]
   
   Now, we can find the total cost for the family:
   \[
   \text{Total cost for the family} = 20 \text{ dollars} + 6 \text{ dollars} = 26 \text{ dollars}
   \]

2. **Calculate the total cost for the other customers:**
   - There are 10 customers after the family, and each customer buys 2 soft tacos.
   - The cost for 2 soft tacos is:
   \[
   \text{Cost for 2 soft tacos} = 2 \text{ tacos} \times 2 \text{ dollars/taco} = 4 \text{ dollars}
   \]
   
   Now, we calculate the total cost for all 10 customers:
   \[
   \text{Total cost for 10 customers} = 10 \text{ customers} \times 4 \text{ dollars/customer} = 40 \text{ dollars}
   \]

3. **Calculate the total revenue for the taco truck:**
   - Now we add the total revenue from the family and the subsequent customers:
   \[
   \text{Total revenue} = \text{Total cost for the family} + \text{Total cost for 10 customers}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Total revenue} = 26 \text{ dollars} + 40 \text{ dollars} = 66 \text{ dollars}
   \]

Therefore, the taco truck made a total of 66 dollars during the lunch rush.
