<reasoning>
First, we need to determine the total number of people going to the amusement park. Jeremie is going with 3 friends, so:

1. **Calculate the total number of tickets needed:**
   \[
   \text{Total people} = 1 \text{ (Jeremie)} + 3 \text{ (friends)} = 4 \text{ people}
   \]

2. **Calculate the total cost of the tickets:**
   Each ticket costs $18, so for 4 tickets:
   \[
   \text{Cost of tickets} = 4 \text{ tickets} \times 18 \text{ dollars/ticket} = 72 \text{ dollars}
   \]

3. **Calculate the total cost for snacks:**
   Each set of snacks costs $5, so for 4 people:
   \[
   \text{Cost of snacks} = 4 \text{ sets} \times 5 \text{ dollars/set} = 20 \text{ dollars}
   \]

4. **Calculate the total cost for the trip:**
   Now, we sum the total cost of the tickets and the total cost of the snacks:
   \[
   \text{Total cost} = \text{Cost of tickets} + \text{Cost of snacks} = 72 \text{ dollars} + 20 \text{ dollars} = 92 \text{ dollars}
   \]

Therefore, the total cost for Jeremie and her 3 friends to go to the amusement park and buy snacks is:
</reasoning>
<answer>92</answer>
