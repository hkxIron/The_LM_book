   \[
   30\% = \frac{30}{100} = 0.30
   \]
   
2. Calculate the discount amount:
   \[
   \text{Discount Amount} = \text{Original Price} \times \text{Discount Percentage} = 50 \times 0.30
   \]
   \[
   = 15
   \]

Next, we'll subtract the discount amount from the original price to find the final cost of the dress.

3. Calculate the final cost:
   \[
   \text{Final Cost} = \text{Original Price} - \text{Discount Amount} = 50 - 15
   \]
   \[

   \]
