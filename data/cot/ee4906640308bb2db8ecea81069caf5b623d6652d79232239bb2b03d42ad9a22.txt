<reasoning>
To determine how many episodes Donna can watch in a week, we need to break it down into weekdays and weekend days.

1. **Calculate weekday episodes:**
   - Donna watches 8 episodes each day during the weekdays.
   - There are 5 weekdays (Monday to Friday).
   - Therefore, the total number of episodes she can watch during the weekdays is:
     \[
     8 \text{ episodes/day} \times 5 \text{ days} = 40 \text{ episodes}
     \]

2. **Calculate weekend episodes:**
   - On each weekend day (Saturday and Sunday), she can watch three times the number of episodes she can watch on the weekdays.
   - Since she watches 8 episodes on a weekday, on the weekend she can watch:
     \[
     3 \times 8 \text{ episodes} = 24 \text{ episodes/day}
     \]
   - There are 2 weekend days.
   - Therefore, the total number of episodes she can watch during the weekend is:
     \[
     24 \text{ episodes/day} \times 2 \text{ days} = 48 \text{ episodes}
     \]

3. **Calculate the total episodes in a week:**
   - Now, we add the total episodes watched during the weekdays and the total episodes watched during the weekend:
     \[
     40 \text{ episodes (weekdays)} + 48 \text{ episodes (weekend)} = 88 \text{ episodes}
     \]

Thus, Donna can watch a total of 88 episodes in a week.
