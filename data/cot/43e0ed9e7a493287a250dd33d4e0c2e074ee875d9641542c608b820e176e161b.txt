<reasoning>
To find out how many pages Sally's book has, we need to calculate the total number of pages she reads in 2 weeks. 

1. **Calculate the pages read on weekdays:**
   - There are 5 weekdays in a week (Monday to Friday).
   - Sally reads 10 pages each weekday.
   - Therefore, in one week, the number of pages she reads on weekdays is:
     \[
     5 \text{ weekdays} \times 10 \text{ pages/weekday} = 50 \text{ pages/week}
     \]
   - For 2 weeks, the number of pages Sally reads on weekdays is:
     \[
     50 \text{ pages/week} \times 2 \text{ weeks} = 100 \text{ pages}
     \]

2. **Calculate the pages read on weekends:**
   - There are 2 weekend days in a week (Saturday and Sunday).
   - Sally reads 20 pages each weekend day.
   - Therefore, in one week, the number of pages she reads on weekends is:
     \[
     2 \text{ weekend days} \times 20 \text{ pages/weekend day} = 40 \text{ pages/week}
     \]
   - For 2 weeks, the number of pages Sally reads on weekends is:
     \[
     40 \text{ pages/week} \times 2 \text{ weeks} = 80 \text{ pages}
     \]

3. **Total pages read in 2 weeks:**
   - Now we add the pages from both weekdays and weekends to find the total number of pages read in 2 weeks:
     \[
     100 \text{ pages (weekdays)} + 80 \text{ pages (weekends)} = 180 \text{ pages}
     \]

Therefore, the total number of pages in Sally's book is 180.
</reasoning>
<answer>180</answer>
