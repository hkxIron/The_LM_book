4. **Calculate the total number of students across all grades:**
   - Total students = Students in 3rd grade + Students in 4th grade + Students in 5th grade.
   - Total students = 150 + 112 + 108 = 370 students.

   - The cost of one hamburger = $2.10.
   - The cost of carrots = $0.50.
   - The cost of one cookie = $0.20.

6. **Calculate the total cost for all students' lunches:**
   - Total cost for all lunches = Total students * Cost of one lunch.
   - Total cost for all lunches = 370 students * $2.80/lunch = $1036.
