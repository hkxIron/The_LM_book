<reasoning>
To find out how much profit Carrie earned on the cake, we need to calculate her total earnings for the hours worked and then subtract the costs of the supplies.

**Step 1: Calculate the total hours worked.**
Carrie worked for 2 hours a day for 4 days, so we multiply the number of hours per day by the number of days:
\[
\text{Total hours} = 2 \text{ hours/day} \times 4 \text{ days} = 8 \text{ hours}
\]

**Step 2: Calculate total earnings from her work.**
Carrie was paid $22 an hour. To find her total earnings, we multiply the total hours worked by her hourly wage:
\[
\text{Total earnings} = 8 \text{ hours} \times 22 \text{ dollars/hour} = 176 \text{ dollars}
\]

**Step 3: Determine the total costs for supplies.**
The cost for supplies to make the cake is given as $54.

**Step 4: Calculate the profit.**
Profit is calculated by subtracting the total costs from the total earnings:
\[
\text{Profit} = \text{Total earnings} - \text{Total costs} = 176 \text{ dollars} - 54 \text{ dollars} = 122 \text{ dollars}
\]

Thus, after calculating all the components, we find that Carrie earned a profit of $122 on the cake.
