     \[
     \text{Total cost} = \text{Number of books} \times \text{Cost per book} = 8 \times 5
     \]

     \[
     \text{Total cost} = 40
     \]

   - To find out how much more he needs, we subtract the amount he currently has from the total cost:
     \[
     \text{Amount needed} = \text{Total cost} - \text{Amount Tommy has} = 40 - 13
     \]
   - Performing the subtraction:
     \[
     \text{Amount needed} = 27
     \]
   This shows that Tommy needs to save up an additional $27.
