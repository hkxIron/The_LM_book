     \[

   - We use the formula for time:
     \[
     \text{Time} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Speed}}
     \]
   - Substituting in the values for ascending:
     \[
     \text{Time ascending} = \frac{60 \text{ miles}}{15 \text{ miles per hour}} = 4 \text{ hours}

     \[

     \]

     \[
     \text{Speed while descending} = 30 + 6 = 36 \text{ miles per hour}
     \]

   - Using the time formula again:
     \[
     \text{Time descending} = \frac{72 \text{ miles}}{36 \text{ miles per hour}} = 2 \text{ hours}
     \]

   - Now we add the time taken to ascend and descend the mountain:
     \[
     \text{Total time} = \text{Time ascending} + \text{Time descending} = 4 \text{ hours} + 2 \text{ hours} = 6 \text{ hours}
