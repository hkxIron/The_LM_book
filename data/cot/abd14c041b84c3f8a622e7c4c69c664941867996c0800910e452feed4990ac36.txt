   \[
   \text{Total Daily Pay} = \text{Daily Pay} \times \text{Number of Reps} = 112 \times 50
   \]
   Performing the multiplication:
   \[
   112 \times 50 = 5600
   \]
   Therefore, the total daily pay for all 50 phone reps is $5600.00.

   \[
   \text{Total Pay for 5 Days} = \text{Total Daily Pay} \times 5 = 5600 \times 5
   \]

   \[
   5600 \times 5 = 28000
   \]
