   \[

   \[

   Since you are splitting the cost evenly with your friend, we divide the total yearly cost by 2.

   \[
   \text{Cost per person} = \frac{\text{Total yearly cost}}{2} = \frac{168}{2}
   \]

   Now, let's do the division:
   
   \[
   \frac{168}{2} = 84
   \]
