<reasoning>
To find out how much it costs to feed Belle her treats for a week, we will first calculate the daily cost of the treats and then multiply that by the number of days in a week.

1. **Calculate the cost of dog biscuits per day:**
   - Belle eats 4 dog biscuits every evening.
   - Each dog biscuit costs $0.25.
   - Therefore, the cost for the dog biscuits is:
     \[
     4 \text{ biscuits} \times 0.25 \text{ dollars/biscuit} = 1 \text{ dollar}
     \]

2. **Calculate the cost of rawhide bones per day:**
   - Belle eats 2 rawhide bones every evening.
   - Each rawhide bone costs $1.
   - Therefore, the cost for the rawhide bones is:
     \[
     2 \text{ bones} \times 1 \text{ dollar/bone} = 2 \text{ dollars}
     \]

3. **Calculate the total cost per day:**
   - Now we add the cost of the dog biscuits and the rawhide bones to find the total cost per day:
     \[
     1 \text{ dollar (dog biscuits)} + 2 \text{ dollars (rawhide bones)} = 3 \text{ dollars}
     \]

4. **Calculate the total cost for a week:**
   - There are 7 days in a week, so we multiply the daily cost by 7:
     \[
     3 \text{ dollars/day} \times 7 \text{ days} = 21 \text{ dollars}
     \]

Thus, the total cost to feed Belle these treats for a week is $21.
