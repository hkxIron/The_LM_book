   \[
   \text{Monthly Salary} = \frac{48,000}{12}
   \]

   \[
   \text{Monthly Salary} = 4,000
   \]

   \[
   \text{Monthly Savings} = 0.10 \times \text{Monthly Salary}
   \]

   Substituting in her monthly salary:

   \[
   \text{Monthly Savings} = 0.10 \times 4,000
   \]

   Now, we perform the multiplication:

   \[
   \text{Monthly Savings} = 400
   \]
