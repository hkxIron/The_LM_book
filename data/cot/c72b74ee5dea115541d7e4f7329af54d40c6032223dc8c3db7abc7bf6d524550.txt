1. **Calculate Mr. John's monthly transport fare**:

   \text{Transport Fare} = 5\% \text{ of } 2000 = \frac{5}{100} \times 2000
   \]

   Now we calculate the multiplication:
   \[
   \frac{5}{100} \times 2000 = 0.05 \times 2000 = 100
   \]

3. **Calculate how much Mr. John has left after transport expenses**:
   To find out how much Mr. John has left after his transport fare, we subtract the transport fare from his monthly income:
   \[
   \text{Remaining Income} = \text{Monthly Income} - \text{Transport Fare} = 2000 - 100
   \]

4. **Perform the subtraction**:
   Now we do the subtraction:
   \[
   2000 - 100 = 1900
   \]
