   \[

   Each serving contains 2.5 cups of broth. Therefore, for 8 servings:
   \[
   \text{Total broth} = \text{Number of servings} \times \text{Broth per serving} = 8 \times 2.5 \text{ cups} = 20 \text{ cups}
   \]

3. **Combine the total amount of vegetables and broth:**
   Now, we add the total cups of vegetables and broth together:
   \[
   \text{Total combined} = \text{Total vegetables} + \text{Total broth} = 8 \text{ cups} + 20 \text{ cups} = 28 \text{ cups}
   \]

4. **Convert cups to pints:**
   We know that 1 pint is equal to 2 cups. Therefore, to convert cups to pints, we divide the total cups by 2:
   \[
   \text{Total in pints} = \frac{\text{Total combined}}{2} = \frac{28 \text{ cups}}{2} = 14 \text{ pints}
   \]
