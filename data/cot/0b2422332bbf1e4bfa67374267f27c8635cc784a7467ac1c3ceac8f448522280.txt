To find out how much change Dusty receives after buying the cake slices, we will first calculate the total cost of the cake slices he purchased.

1. **Calculate the cost of single layer cake slices:**
   - Dusty buys 7 single layer cake slices, and each slice costs $4.
   - We multiply the number of slices by the price per slice:
     \[
     \text{Cost of single layer slices} = 7 \times 4 = 28
     \]

2. **Calculate the cost of double layer cake slices:**
   - Dusty buys 5 double layer cake slices, and each slice costs $7.
   - We multiply the number of slices by the price per slice:
     \[
     \text{Cost of double layer slices} = 5 \times 7 = 35
     \]

3. **Calculate the total cost of all cake slices:**
   - Now, we will add the costs of both types of cake slices together:
     \[
     \text{Total cost} = \text{Cost of single layer slices} + \text{Cost of double layer slices} = 28 + 35 = 63
     \]

4. **Calculate the change Dusty receives:**
   - Dusty pays with a $100 bill. To find out how much change he receives, we subtract the total cost from the amount he paid:
     \[
     \text{Change} = 100 - \text{Total cost} = 100 - 63 = 37
     \]

Thus, Dusty receives $37 in change.
