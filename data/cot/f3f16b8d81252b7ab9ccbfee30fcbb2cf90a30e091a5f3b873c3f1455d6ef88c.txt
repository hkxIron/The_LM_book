To find out how much more Sally needs to save, we need to calculate the total cost of her trip to Sea World, including parking, admission, a meal pass, and gas for the drive.

**Step 1: Calculate the total cost of parking, admission, and the meal pass.**
- Parking: $10
- Admission: $55
- Meal pass: $25

Now, let's add these costs together:
\[
\text{Total cost of parking, admission, and meal pass} = 10 + 55 + 25
\]
Calculating this gives:
\[
10 + 55 = 65
\]
\[
65 + 25 = 90
\]

**Step 2: Calculate the distance to be driven and the amount of gas needed.**
- The distance to Sea World is 165 miles one way, so the round trip distance is:
\[
\text{Round trip distance} = 165 \times 2 = 330 \text{ miles}
\]

**Step 3: Determine how many gallons of gas are needed to cover the round trip.**
- The car gets 30 miles per gallon, so we can calculate the gallons needed:
\[
\text{Gallons needed} = \frac{\text{Total distance}}{\text{Miles per gallon}} = \frac{330}{30}
\]
Calculating this gives:
\[
\frac{330}{30} = 11 \text{ gallons}
\]

**Step 4: Calculate the cost of the gas for the trip.**
- The cost of gas is $3 per gallon, so:
\[
\text{Cost of gas} = \text{Gallons needed} \times \text{Cost per gallon} = 11 \times 3
\]
Calculating this gives:
\[
11 \times 3 = 33
\]

**Step 5: Calculate the total cost of the trip.**
Now we need to add the cost of gas to the previous total of parking, admission, and meal pass:
\[
\text{Total trip cost} = \text{Cost of parking, admission, and meal pass} + \text{Cost of gas} = 90 + 33
\]
Calculating this gives:
\[
90 + 33 = 123
\]

**Step 6: Determine how much more Sally needs to save.**
Sally already has $28 saved, so we need to find out how much more she needs:
\[
\text{Amount needed} = \text{Total trip cost} - \text{Amount saved} = 123 - 28
\]
Calculating this gives:
\[
123 - 28 = 95
\]

Therefore, Sally needs to save up an additional $95.
