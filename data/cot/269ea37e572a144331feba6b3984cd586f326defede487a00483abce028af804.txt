1. **Calculate Total Revenue**:

   - She made 25 doughnuts.
   - To find the total revenue, we multiply the number of doughnuts by the price per doughnut:
     \[
     \text{Total Revenue} = \text{Number of Doughnuts} \times \text{Price per Doughnut}
     \]

     \text{Total Revenue} = 25 \times 3
     \]

     \text{Total Revenue} = 75
     \]

   - Dorothy spent $53 on the ingredients for the doughnuts.
   - This amount represents her total costs.

3. **Calculate Profit**: 
   - Profit is calculated by subtracting total costs from total revenue:
     \[
     \text{Profit} = \text{Total Revenue} - \text{Total Costs}
     \]
     \[
     \text{Profit} = 75 - 53
     \]
     \[
     \text{Profit} = 22
     \]
