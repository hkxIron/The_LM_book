     \[
     \text{Number of basketballs} = \text{Total balls} \times \frac{\text{Percentage}}{100}

     \[
     \text{Number of basketballs} = 200 \times \frac{20}{100}
     \]
   - Plugging in the numbers:
     \[
     \text{Number of basketballs} = 200 \times 0.2 = 40
     \]
   - So, Lucien has 40 basketballs.

3. **Finding the total number of basketballs**:
   - Now that we have both amounts, we will add the number of basketballs Lucca has and the number of basketballs Lucien has.
   - Total basketballs = Lucca's basketballs + Lucien's basketballs:
     \[
     \text{Total basketballs} = 10 + 40 = 50
     \]
