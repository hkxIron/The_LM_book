     \[
     \text{Distance for the first 183 days} = 30 \text{ miles/day} \times 183 \text{ days}

     \[
     30 \times 183 = 5490 \text{ miles}

     \[
     \text{Remaining days} = 365 - 183 = 182 \text{ days}
     \]

   - For these days, Tom rides 35 miles per day. We again use multiplication:
     \[
     \text{Distance for the remaining 182 days} = 35 \text{ miles/day} \times 182 \text{ days}
     \]
   - Performing the multiplication:
     \[
     35 \times 182 = 6370 \text{ miles}
     \]

4. **Add both distances to find the total distance for the year:**
   - Now we combine the distances from both parts of the year:
     \[
     \text{Total distance} = \text{Distance for the first 183 days} + \text{Distance for the remaining 182 days}
     \]
     \[
     \text{Total distance} = 5490 \text{ miles} + 6370 \text{ miles}
     \]
   - Performing the addition:
     \[
     5490 + 6370 = 11860 \text{ miles}
     \]
