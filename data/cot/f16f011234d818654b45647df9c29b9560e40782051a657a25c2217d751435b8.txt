To find out how much the bag of potatoes cost, we will first calculate the total amount Mrs. Thompson spent on the chickens and then subtract that from her total expenditure.

1. **Calculate the total cost of the chickens**:  
   Mrs. Thompson bought 3 chickens for $3 each.  
   We use the formula:  
   Total cost of chickens = Number of chickens × Cost per chicken  
   Total cost of chickens = 3 × 3 = $9

2. **Determine the total amount spent on potatoes**:  
   Mrs. Thompson paid a total of $15.  
   To find the cost of the potatoes, we subtract the total cost of the chickens from the total amount spent.  
   Cost of potatoes = Total amount spent - Total cost of chickens  
   Cost of potatoes = 15 - 9 = $6

Therefore, the cost of the potatoes is $6.
