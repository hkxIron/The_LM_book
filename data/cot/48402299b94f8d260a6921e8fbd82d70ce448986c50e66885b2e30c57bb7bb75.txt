To solve this problem, we need to determine how far the ball travels in the air while it is flying and then find out how long it will take the border collie to cover that same distance.

1. **Calculate the distance the ball travels:**
   The formula for distance is:
   \[
   \text{Distance} = \text{Speed} \times \text{Time}
   \]
   In this case, the speed of the ball is 20 feet/second, and it flies for 8 seconds. So we can calculate the distance as follows:
   \[
   \text{Distance} = 20 \, \text{feet/second} \times 8 \, \text{seconds} = 160 \, \text{feet}
   \]

2. **Calculate how long it takes for the border collie to catch up:**
   Now, we know the distance the ball travels (160 feet) and the speed of the border collie (5 feet/second). We can use the same distance formula rearranged to solve for time:
   \[
   \text{Time} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Speed}}
   \]
   Plugging in the values we have:
   \[
   \text{Time} = \frac{160 \, \text{feet}}{5 \, \text{feet/second}} = 32 \, \text{seconds}
   \]

Therefore, it will take the border collie 32 seconds to catch up to the ball.
