1. **Set up the equations based on the problem statement:**
   - In ten years, your age will be \( x + 10 \).
   - In ten years, your brother's age will be \( y + 10 \).

     \[
     x + 10 = 2(y + 10)

   - This can be rewritten as:
     \[
     x + 10 = 2y + 20
     \]

     \[
     x = 2y + 20 - 10
     \]
     \[
     x = 2y + 10
     \]

3. **Next, use the information about the sum of your ages in ten years:**
   - The sum of your ages in ten years will be:
     \[
     (x + 10) + (y + 10) = 45
     \]

     \[
     x + y + 20 = 45
     \]
   - Therefore, we can isolate \( x + y \):
     \[
     x + y = 45 - 20
     \]
     \[
     x + y = 25
     \]

4. **Now we have a system of equations:**
   - From step 2: \( x = 2y + 10 \)
   - From step 3: \( x + y = 25 \)

5. **Substitute \( x \) from the first equation into the second equation:**
   \[
   (2y + 10) + y = 25
   \]
   - Combine like terms:
     \[
     3y + 10 = 25
     \]
   - Isolating \( y \):
     \[
     3y = 25 - 10
     \]
     \[
     3y = 15
     \]
     \[
     y = 5
     \]

6. **Now that we have \( y \), substitute back to find \( x \):**
   \[
   x = 2(5) + 10
   \]
   \[
   x = 10 + 10
   \]
   \[

   \]

   - Your current age is \( x = 20 \).
