<reasoning>
To find out how many men are in the engineering department, we can follow these steps:

1. **Understand the percentages and total:** We know that 70% of the students are men and the remaining 30% are women. We are given that there are 180 women. 

2. **Set up the equation for the total number of students:** 
   Since women make up 30% of the total number of students, we can use the following equation:
   \[
   \text{Number of women} = 30\% \times \text{Total students}
   \]
   We can express this mathematically as:
   \[
   180 = 0.30 \times \text{Total students}
   \]

3. **Solve for the total number of students:** 
   To find the total number of students, we can rearrange the equation:
   \[
   \text{Total students} = \frac{180}{0.30}
   \]
   Now, we calculate this:
   \[
   \text{Total students} = \frac{180}{0.30} = 600
   \]

4. **Calculate the number of men:** 
   Since we know 70% of the students are men, we can use the total number of students to find the number of men:
   \[
   \text{Number of men} = 70\% \times \text{Total students}
   \]
   In mathematical terms:
   \[
   \text{Number of men} = 0.70 \times 600
   \]
   Now, we compute this:
   \[
   \text{Number of men} = 420
   \]

Therefore, the number of men in the engineering department is 420.
