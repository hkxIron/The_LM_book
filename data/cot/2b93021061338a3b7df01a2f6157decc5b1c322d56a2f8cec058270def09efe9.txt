<reasoning>
To find out how many cookies Ann keeps, we first need to calculate the total number of cookies she originally baked and then subtract the number of cookies she gave away.

1. **Calculate the total number of cookies baked:**
   - Oatmeal raisin cookies: 3 dozen = 3 * 12 = 36 cookies
   - Sugar cookies: 2 dozen = 2 * 12 = 24 cookies
   - Chocolate chip cookies: 4 dozen = 4 * 12 = 48 cookies

   Now, we add these amounts together:
   \[
   \text{Total cookies baked} = 36 + 24 + 48
   \]

   \[
   36 + 24 = 60
   \]
   \[
   60 + 48 = 108
   \]

2. **Calculate the total number of cookies given away:**
   - Oatmeal raisin cookies given away: 2 dozen = 2 * 12 = 24 cookies
   - Sugar cookies given away: 1.5 dozen = 1.5 * 12 = 18 cookies
   - Chocolate chip cookies given away: 2.5 dozen = 2.5 * 12 = 30 cookies

   Now, we add these amounts together:
   \[
   \text{Total cookies given away} = 24 + 18 + 30
   \]

   Performing the addition:
   \[
   24 + 18 = 42
   \]
   \[
   42 + 30 = 72
   \]

   So, Ann gave away a total of 72 cookies.

3. **Calculate the total number of cookies Ann keeps:**
   We subtract the total number of cookies given away from the total number of cookies baked:
   \[
   \text{Total cookies kept} = \text{Total cookies baked} - \text{Total cookies given away} = 108 - 72
   \]

   Performing the subtraction:
   \[
   108 - 72 = 36
   \]

Therefore, Ann keeps 36 cookies.
