   - Next, calculate her monthly income by multiplying her weekly income by the number of weeks in a month (4 weeks):

     \]

   - As a freelancer, she also works 40 hours per week but gets paid $40/hour.

     \[
     \text{Freelancing Weekly Income} = 40 \text{ hours/week} \times 40 \text{ dollars/hour} = 1600 \text{ dollars/week}
     \]

     \[
     \text{Freelancing Monthly Income} = 1600 \text{ dollars/week} \times 4 \text{ weeks/month} = 6400 \text{ dollars/month}
     \]

3. **Calculate the total expenses associated with freelancing:**
   - Janet has two types of expenses as a freelancer: FICA taxes and healthcare premiums.
   - FICA taxes cost $25 per week, so for a month:
     \[
     \text{Monthly FICA Taxes} = 25 \text{ dollars/week} \times 4 \text{ weeks/month} = 100 \text{ dollars/month}
     \]

     \[
     \text{Total Monthly Expenses} = 100 \text{ dollars/month (FICA)} + 400 \text{ dollars/month (healthcare)} = 500 \text{ dollars/month}
     \]

     \[
     \text{Net Monthly Income as Freelancer} = 6400 \text{ dollars/month} - 500 \text{ dollars/month} = 5900 \text{ dollars/month}
     \]

     \text{Difference} = \text{Net Monthly Income as Freelancer} - \text{Monthly Income from Current Job}
