1. **Calculate the total cost of the films:**

   - To find the total cost for the films, we multiply the number of films by the cost per film:
     \[
     \text{Total cost of films} = 9 \text{ films} \times 5 \text{ dollars/film} = 45 \text{ dollars}
     \]

2. **Calculate the total cost of the books:**
   - Josh bought 4 books, and each book costs $4.

     \[
     \text{Total cost of books} = 4 \text{ books} \times 4 \text{ dollars/book} = 16 \text{ dollars}
     \]

3. **Calculate the total cost of the CDs:**
   - Josh bought 6 CDs, and each CD costs $3.

     \[
     \text{Total cost of CDs} = 6 \text{ CDs} \times 3 \text{ dollars/CD} = 18 \text{ dollars}
     \]

4. **Sum all the costs to get the total expenditure:**
   - Now, we add the total costs of films, books, and CDs together:
     \[
     \text{Total expenditure} = \text{Total cost of films} + \text{Total cost of books} + \text{Total cost of CDs}
     \]
     \[
     \text{Total expenditure} = 45 \text{ dollars} + 16 \text{ dollars} + 18 \text{ dollars} = 79 \text{ dollars}
