     \[
     \text{Total red crayons} = 11 \text{ red crayons}
     \]

   - Now we add the total number of crayons of each color to find the overall total:
     \[
     \text{Total crayons} = \text{Total orange crayons} + \text{Total blue crayons} + \text{Total red crayons}
