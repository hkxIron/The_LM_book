   \[
   \text{Number of chicks} = 12 \text{ hens} \times 5 \text{ chicks per hen} = 60 \text{ chicks}
   \]

3. **Total number of chickens:** 
   Now we need to find the total number of chickens on the farm. The total includes hens, roosters, and chicks:
   \[
   \text{Total number of chickens} = \text{Number of hens} + \text{Number of roosters} + \text{Number of chicks}
   \]

   \[
   \text{Total number of chickens} = 12 \text{ hens} + 4 \text{ roosters} + 60 \text{ chicks} = 76 \text{ chickens}
   \]
