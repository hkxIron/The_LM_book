To solve this problem, we need to break it down step by step and analyze the information given.

1. **Understanding the Work Rate:**
   We know that 10 people can shovel 10,000 pounds of coal in 10 days. First, we will calculate the work rate for these 10 people.
   
   Work done (in pounds) = 10,000 pounds  
   Number of people = 10  
   Number of days = 10  

   To find the work rate per person per day, we can use the formula:
   \[
   \text{Work rate per person} = \frac{\text{Total work}}{\text{Number of people} \times \text{Number of days}}
   \]
   Substituting in the values:
   \[
   \text{Work rate per person} = \frac{10,000 \text{ pounds}}{10 \text{ people} \times 10 \text{ days}} = \frac{10,000}{100} = 100 \text{ pounds per person per day}
   \]

2. **Finding the Work Rate of Half the People:**
   Now, we need to consider half of the original 10 people, which is 5 people. We can use the work rate we found to determine how much coal they can shovel in a day.
   
   Work rate of 5 people:
   \[
   \text{Total work rate of 5 people} = 5 \text{ people} \times 100 \text{ pounds per person per day} = 500 \text{ pounds per day}
   \]

3. **Determining the Time to Shovel 40,000 Pounds:**
   Now that we know 5 people can shovel 500 pounds of coal per day, we want to find out how many days it will take to shovel 40,000 pounds.
   
   Using the formula:
   \[
   \text{Days needed} = \frac{\text{Total work}}{\text{Work rate}}
   \]
   Substituting in the values:
   \[
   \text{Days needed} = \frac{40,000 \text{ pounds}}{500 \text{ pounds per day}} = 80 \text{ days}
   \]

Thus, it will take 5 people a total of 80 days to shovel 40,000 pounds of coal.
