     \[

     \]

     \[
     \text{Distance} = \text{Speed} \times \text{Time}
     \]
   - Plugging in the values:
     \[
     \text{Distance} = 40 \text{ miles per hour} \times 0.25 \text{ hours} = 10 \text{ miles}
