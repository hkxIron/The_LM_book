To determine how many miles Clover walks in 30 days, we start by calculating the total distance he walks in one day.

   \[
   \text{Total distance in one day} = 1.5 \text{ miles} + 1.5 \text{ miles} = 3 \text{ miles}
   \]

2. Now that we know Clover walks 3 miles each day, we need to calculate how many miles he walks in 30 days.
   - To find the total distance for 30 days, we multiply the daily distance by the number of days:
   \[
   \text{Total distance in 30 days} = 3 \text{ miles/day} \times 30 \text{ days} = 90 \text{ miles}
   \]

Thus, after calculating the distance Clover walks each day and multiplying it by the number of days, we find that he walks a total of 90 miles in 30 days.
