     \[
     \text{Total Application Fees} = 6 \times 25 = 150
     \]
   - This means Linda will need a total of $150.00 to cover the application fees.

   - Linda makes $10.00 per hour babysitting.

3. **Calculate the number of hours she needs to babysit:**
   - To find out how many hours Linda needs to work to earn $150.00, we divide the total amount of money she needs by her hourly wage:
     \[
     \text{Hours Needed} = \frac{\text{Total Application Fees}}{\text{Hourly Wage}} = \frac{150}{10}
     \]
   - Performing the division:
     \[
     \text{Hours Needed} = 15
     \]

Therefore, Linda will need to babysit for 15 hours to cover her application fees.
