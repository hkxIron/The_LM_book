<reasoning>
Let's denote the total amount of money James won as \( x \).

1. First, James donates half of his winnings back to the charity. This means he donates:
   \[
   \frac{x}{2}
   \]

2. After donating half, the amount of money he has left is:
   \[
   x - \frac{x}{2} = \frac{x}{2}
   \]
   This is because he started with \( x \) and gave away \( \frac{x}{2} \).

3. Next, James spends $2 on a hot dog. So, the amount he has left after spending this money is:
   \[
   \frac{x}{2} - 2
   \]

4. We know that after all these transactions, James has $55 left. Therefore, we can set up the equation:
   \[
   \frac{x}{2} - 2 = 55
   \]

5. Now, let's solve for \( x \). First, we add 2 to both sides of the equation to isolate the term with \( x \):
   \[
   \frac{x}{2} = 55 + 2
   \]
   \[
   \frac{x}{2} = 57
   \]

6. Next, we multiply both sides of the equation by 2 to solve for \( x \):
   \[
   x = 57 \times 2
   \]
   \[
   x = 114
   \]

Thus, James won a total of $114.
