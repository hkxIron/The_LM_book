<reasoning>
To find out how many students went on the field trip, we need to calculate the total number of students who traveled in the vans and the minibusses separately, and then sum those totals.

1. **Calculate the number of students in the vans:**
   - There are 6 vans, and each van carries 10 students.
   - We multiply the number of vans by the number of students per van:
     \[
     \text{Students in vans} = 6 \text{ vans} \times 10 \text{ students/van} = 60 \text{ students}
     \]

2. **Calculate the number of students in the minibusses:**
   - There are 4 minibusses, and each minibus carries 24 students.
   - We multiply the number of minibusses by the number of students per minibus:
     \[
     \text{Students in minibusses} = 4 \text{ minibusses} \times 24 \text{ students/minibus} = 96 \text{ students}
     \]

3. **Add the numbers of students from the vans and minibusses:**
   - Now we add the total number of students from the vans and the minibusses to find the overall total:
     \[
     \text{Total students} = \text{Students in vans} + \text{Students in minibusses} = 60 + 96 = 156
     \]

Thus, the total number of students who went on the field trip is 156.
