To determine the total time required to complete 8 loads of laundry, we need to account for both the washing and drying cycles.

1. **Understanding the washing and drying times**:
   - Each load of laundry takes **45 minutes** to wash.
   - Each load then takes **1 hour** (which is 60 minutes) to dry.

2. **Calculating the total washing time for 8 loads**:
   - Since each load takes 45 minutes to wash, for 8 loads, the washing time is:
     \[
     8 \text{ loads} \times 45 \text{ minutes/load} = 360 \text{ minutes}
     \]

3. **Calculating the total drying time for 8 loads**:
   - Each load takes 60 minutes to dry, so for 8 loads, the drying time is:
     \[
     8 \text{ loads} \times 60 \text{ minutes/load} = 480 \text{ minutes}
     \]

4. **Total time in minutes**:
   - Now, we add the total washing time and the total drying time:
     \[
     360 \text{ minutes (washing)} + 480 \text{ minutes (drying)} = 840 \text{ minutes}
     \]

5. **Converting total time from minutes to hours**:
   - To convert minutes into hours, we divide by 60:
     \[
     \frac{840 \text{ minutes}}{60} = 14 \text{ hours}
     \]

Thus, the total time to complete all 8 loads of laundry is 14 hours.
