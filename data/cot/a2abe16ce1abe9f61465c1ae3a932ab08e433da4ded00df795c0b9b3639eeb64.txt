<reasoning>
To find out how much Michael earned from selling his paintings, we need to calculate the total income from both the large and small paintings separately and then sum these amounts.

1. **Calculate the income from large paintings**:
   - Michael sells large paintings for $100 each.
   - He sold 5 large paintings.
   - To find the total income from large paintings, we multiply the number of large paintings sold by the price per large painting:
     \[
     \text{Income from large paintings} = 5 \text{ paintings} \times 100 \text{ dollars/painting} = 500 \text{ dollars}
     \]

2. **Calculate the income from small paintings**:
   - Michael sells small paintings for $80 each.
   - He sold 8 small paintings.
   - To find the total income from small paintings, we multiply the number of small paintings sold by the price per small painting:
     \[
     \text{Income from small paintings} = 8 \text{ paintings} \times 80 \text{ dollars/painting} = 640 \text{ dollars}
     \]

3. **Calculate the total income**:
   - Now, we add the income from large paintings and the income from small paintings to find the total income:
     \[
     \text{Total income} = \text{Income from large paintings} + \text{Income from small paintings} = 500 \text{ dollars} + 640 \text{ dollars} = 1140 \text{ dollars}
     \]

Therefore, Michael earned a total of $1140 from selling his paintings.
