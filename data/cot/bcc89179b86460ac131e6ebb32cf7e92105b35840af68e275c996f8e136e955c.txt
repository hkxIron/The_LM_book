To determine how much of Scarlet's savings she has left after her purchases, we will follow these steps:

1. **Identify the total savings**: Scarlet initially saved $80.

2. **Calculate the total cost of her purchases**: She bought a pair of earrings for $23 and a necklace for $48. We need to add these two amounts together to find the total spent.
   \[
   \text{Total cost} = \text{Cost of earrings} + \text{Cost of necklace} = 23 + 48
   \]
   Performing the addition:
   \[
   23 + 48 = 71
   \]
   So, she spent a total of $71.

3. **Subtract the total cost from the total savings**: Now we subtract the total spent from her initial savings to find out how much she has left.
   \[
   \text{Remaining savings} = \text{Total savings} - \text{Total cost} = 80 - 71
   \]
   Performing the subtraction:
   \[
   80 - 71 = 9
   \]

Therefore, after her purchases, Scarlet has $9 left from her savings.
