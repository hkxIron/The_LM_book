To find the percentage difference in tail size between Eastern and Western rattlesnakes, we will first determine the difference in the number of segments in their tails, and then express this difference as a percentage of the Western rattlesnake's tail size.

   - Eastern rattlesnakes have 6 segments.

   \[
   \text{Difference} = \text{Western segments} - \text{Eastern segments} = 8 - 6 = 2
   \]

   To find the percentage difference, we use the formula:
   \[
   \text{Percentage Difference} = \left(\frac{\text{Difference}}{\text{Western segments}}\right) \times 100
   \]
   Plugging in the values we have:
   \[
   \text{Percentage Difference} = \left(\frac{2}{8}\right) \times 100
   \]

4. **Calculate the fraction:**
   \[
   \frac{2}{8} = 0.25
   \]

5. **Convert to percentage:**
   \[
   0.25 \times 100 = 25
   \]

Therefore, the percentage difference in tail size, expressed as a percentage of the Western rattlesnake's tail size, is 25%.
