   - Here, the distance Jim needs to cover is 1 mile, and his speed relative to Bob is 3 mph.
   - So, Time = 1 mile / 3 mph = 1/3 of an hour.

4. **Convert the time from hours to minutes**: 
   - Since there are 60 minutes in an hour, we multiply 1/3 of an hour by 60 minutes/hour.
   - Time in minutes = (1/3) * 60 = 20 minutes.
