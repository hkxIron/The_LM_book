To determine how much it will cost Eric to rent the carriage house for 20 days, we need to compare the two pricing options available: the daily rate of $50.00 per day and the flat rate of $500.00 for 14 days.

1. **Calculate the cost of renting for 20 days at the daily rate:**
   - The daily rate is $50.00.
   - For 20 days, the total cost would be:
     \[
     20 \text{ days} \times 50 \text{ dollars/day} = 1000 \text{ dollars}
     \]
   - This means if Eric rents the carriage house at the daily rate, he will pay $1,000.

   - The flat rate for 14 days is $500.00.
   - After 14 days, Eric would still need to rent for an additional 6 days. We need to calculate the cost for those additional 6 days at the daily rate:
     \[
     6 \text{ days} \times 50 \text{ dollars/day} = 300 \text{ dollars}
     \]
   - Now, we add the cost of the initial 14 days to the cost of the additional 6 days:
     \[
     500 \text{ dollars} + 300 \text{ dollars} = 800 \text{ dollars}
     \]
   - This means if Eric rents the house using the flat rate for the first 14 days and then the daily rate for the additional 6 days, he will pay $800.

3. **Compare the two total costs:**
   - Daily rate for 20 days: $1,000
   - Combination of flat rate for 14 days and daily rate for 6 days: $800
   - The cheaper option for Eric is $800.
