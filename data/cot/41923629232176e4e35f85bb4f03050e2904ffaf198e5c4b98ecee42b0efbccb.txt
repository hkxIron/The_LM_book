To find out how much money Grace earned in September from her landscaping business, we need to calculate her earnings from each type of work she did (mowing lawns, pulling weeds, and putting down mulch) and then sum those amounts.

   - To find out how much she earned from mowing, we multiply the number of hours by the rate:
     \[
     \text{Earnings from mowing} = 63 \, \text{hours} \times 6 \, \text{dollars/hour} = 378 \, \text{dollars}
     \]

2. **Calculate earnings from pulling weeds:**
   - Grace charges $11 per hour for pulling weeds.
   - She pulled weeds for 9 hours.
   - To find out how much she earned from pulling weeds, we multiply the number of hours by the rate:
     \[
     \text{Earnings from pulling weeds} = 9 \, \text{hours} \times 11 \, \text{dollars/hour} = 99 \, \text{dollars}
     \]

3. **Calculate earnings from putting down mulch:**
   - Grace charges $9 per hour for putting down mulch.
   - She put down mulch for 10 hours.
   - To find out how much she earned from putting down mulch, we multiply the number of hours by the rate:
     \[
     \text{Earnings from putting down mulch} = 10 \, \text{hours} \times 9 \, \text{dollars/hour} = 90 \, \text{dollars}
     \]

4. **Sum all her earnings:**
   - Now we add up all the earnings from each type of work:
     \[
     \text{Total earnings} = \text{Earnings from mowing} + \text{Earnings from pulling weeds} + \text{Earnings from putting down mulch}
     \]
     \[
     \text{Total earnings} = 378 \, \text{dollars} + 99 \, \text{dollars} + 90 \, \text{dollars} = 567 \, \text{dollars}
