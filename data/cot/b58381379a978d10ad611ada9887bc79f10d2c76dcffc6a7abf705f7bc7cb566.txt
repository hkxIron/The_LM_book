   \[

   \[
   \text{Speed over the limit} = 75 - 30 = 45 \text{ miles per hour}
   \]

   \[
   \text{Additional Penalty} = 45 \times 2 = 90
   \]

   \[
   \text{Total Fine before doubling} = \text{Base Fine} + \text{Additional Penalty} = 50 + 90 = 140
   \]

5. **Double the fine**: Since Mark was speeding in a school zone, the total fine is doubled.

   \[
   \text{Total Fine after doubling} = 140 \times 2 = 280
   \]

   \[
   \text{Total with Court Costs} = 280 + 300 = 580
   \]

   \[
   \text{Lawyer Fees} = 80 \times 3 = 240
   \]

   \[
   \text{Total Amount Owed} = 580 + 240 = 820
   \]
