2. **Calculate the werewolf's consumption per week**:

   \[
   \text{Total consumption per week} = \text{Vampire's consumption} + \text{Werewolf's consumption} = 3 + 5 = 8
   \]

4. **Determine how many weeks the village of 72 people will last**:
   To find out how many weeks the village can sustain the vampire and the werewolf, we divide the total number of people in the village by the total consumption per week:
   \[
   \text{Number of weeks} = \frac{\text{Total people in the village}}{\text{Total consumption per week}} = \frac{72}{8} = 9
   \]
