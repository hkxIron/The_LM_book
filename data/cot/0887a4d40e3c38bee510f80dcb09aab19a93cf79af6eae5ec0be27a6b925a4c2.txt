     \[
     \text{Fish caught eastward} = \frac{2}{5} \times 3,200
     \]
     Calculating this:
     \[
     \frac{2 \times 3,200}{5} = \frac{6,400}{5} = 1,280

     \[
     \text{Fish caught westward} = \frac{3}{4} \times 1,800
     \]

     \[
     \frac{3 \times 1,800}{4} = \frac{5,400}{4} = 1,350
     \]

   Now we add the fish caught from both directions:
   \[
   \text{Total fish caught} = 1,280 + 1,350
   \]
   Calculating this:
   \[
   1,280 + 1,350 = 2,630
   \]

4. **Calculate the number of fish left**:
   We subtract the total fish caught from the total number of fish:
   \[
   \text{Fish left} = \text{Total fish} - \text{Total fish caught}
   \]
   Thus:
   \[
   \text{Fish left} = 5,500 - 2,630
   \]

   \[
   5,500 - 2,630 = 2,870
