<reasoning>
To find out how much money Diana earned over the three months, we will calculate her earnings for each month and then sum them up.

1. **Calculate Diana's earnings in July**: 
   Diana earned $150 in July. 

2. **Calculate Diana's earnings in August**: 
   She earned 3 times what she earned in July. 
   So, her earnings in August = 3 * $150 = $450.

3. **Calculate Diana's earnings in September**: 
   She earned twice the amount she earned in August.
   So, her earnings in September = 2 * $450 = $900.

4. **Total earnings over the three months**: 
   Now, we will sum her earnings from each month:
   Total earnings = Earnings in July + Earnings in August + Earnings in September
   Total earnings = $150 + $450 + $900.

   Performing the addition:
   $150 + $450 = $600,
   $600 + $900 = $1500.

Therefore, Diana's total earnings over the three months is $1500.
</reasoning>
