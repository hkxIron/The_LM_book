To find out how many chocolate bars are in a 2 kilogram box, we first need to convert the weight of the box from kilograms to grams since the weight of each chocolate bar is given in grams.

1. **Convert kilograms to grams**: 
   We know that 1 kilogram is equal to 1000 grams. So, to convert 2 kilograms to grams, we multiply by 1000:
   \[
   2 \text{ kg} \times 1000 \text{ g/kg} = 2000 \text{ g}
   \]
   Now we know that the 2 kilogram box contains 2000 grams of chocolate.

2. **Determine the number of chocolate bars**:
   Each chocolate bar weighs 125 grams. To find out how many bars are in the 2000 grams of chocolate, we divide the total weight of the chocolate by the weight of one bar:
   \[
   \frac{2000 \text{ g}}{125 \text{ g/bar}} = 16
   \]
   This calculation shows that there are 16 chocolate bars in the 2 kilogram box.

Therefore, the total number of chocolate bars represented in the box is:
</reasoning>
<answer>16</answer>
