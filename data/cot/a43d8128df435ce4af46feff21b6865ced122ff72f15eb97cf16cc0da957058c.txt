   \[

   Now, we can find the work rate for 15 pugs by multiplying the work rate of 1 pug by 15:
   \[
   \text{Work rate of 15 pugs} = 15 \times \frac{1}{180} = \frac{15}{180} = \frac{1}{12} \text{ houses per minute}
   \]

4. **Determine how long it takes for 15 pugs to clean the house:**
   If the work rate of 15 pugs is \(\frac{1}{12}\) houses per minute, we can find the time to clean 1 house by taking the reciprocal of their work rate:
   \[
   \text{Time to clean 1 house} = \frac{1}{\frac{1}{12}} = 12 \text{ minutes}
   \]
