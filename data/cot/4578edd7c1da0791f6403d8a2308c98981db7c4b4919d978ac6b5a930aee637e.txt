     \[
     \text{Gallons used on highways} = \frac{\text{Miles driven on highways}}{\text{Miles per gallon on highways}} = \frac{210 \text{ miles}}{35 \text{ miles/gallon}} = 6 \text{ gallons}

     \]

   - Now, we add the gallons used on highways and city streets to find the total amount of gas used:
     \[
     \text{Total gallons used} = \text{Gallons used on highways} + \text{Gallons used on city streets} = 6 \text{ gallons} + 3 \text{ gallons} = 9 \text{ gallons}
