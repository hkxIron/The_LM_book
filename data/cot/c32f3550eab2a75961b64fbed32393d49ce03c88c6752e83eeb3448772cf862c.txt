   \[
   \text{Number of lessons} = \frac{18 \text{ hours}}{1.5 \text{ hours/lesson}}

   \[
   \text{Number of lessons} = 12

   \[
   \text{Total cost} = \text{Number of lessons} \times \text{Cost per lesson} = 12 \times 30
   \]
   Performing the multiplication:
   \[
   \text{Total cost} = 360
   \]
