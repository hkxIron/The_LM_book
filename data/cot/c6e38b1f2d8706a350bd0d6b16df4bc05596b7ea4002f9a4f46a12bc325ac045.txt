Tony drives 50 miles round trip each day. Since he works 5 days a week, we can calculate his total weekly mileage:

\[
\text{Weekly miles} = \text{Miles per day} \times \text{Days per week} = 50 \, \text{miles/day} \times 5 \, \text{days/week} = 250 \, \text{miles/week}
\]

\[
\text{Gallons used per week} = \frac{\text{Weekly miles}}{\text{Miles per gallon}} = \frac{250 \, \text{miles}}{25 \, \text{miles/gallon}} = 10 \, \text{gallons/week}

\[
\text{Cost per week} = \text{Gallons used per week} \times \text{Cost per gallon} = 10 \, \text{gallons/week} \times 2 \, \text{dollars/gallon} = 20 \, \text{dollars/week}
\]

**Step 4: Calculate the total cost of gas for 4 weeks.**
To find out how much he spends in 4 weeks, we multiply the weekly cost by 4:

\[
\text{Total cost for 4 weeks} = \text{Cost per week} \times 4 = 20 \, \text{dollars/week} \times 4 = 80 \, \text{dollars}
\]
