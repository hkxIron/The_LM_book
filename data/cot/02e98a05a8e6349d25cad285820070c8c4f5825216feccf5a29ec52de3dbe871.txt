   - Since 1 mph is approximately 1.5 feet per second, we will multiply by 1.5 to convert their speeds.

   \[

   \]

   For the gazelle:
   \[
   40 \text{ mph} \times 1.5 \text{ ft/s per mph} = 60 \text{ ft/s}
   \]

   Since both animals are running in the same direction, we need to find the difference in their speeds to determine how quickly the cheetah is closing the gap:
   \[
   \text{Relative speed} = \text{Cheetah's speed} - \text{Gazelle's speed} = 90 \text{ ft/s} - 60 \text{ ft/s} = 30 \text{ ft/s}
   \]

3. **Determine the initial distance**:
   The initial distance between the cheetah and the gazelle is given as 210 feet.

4. **Calculate the time it takes for the cheetah to catch the gazelle**:
   We can use the formula:
   \[
   \text{Time} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Relative Speed}} = \frac{210 \text{ feet}}{30 \text{ ft/s}} = 7 \text{ seconds}
   \]
