   \[

   \[
   \text{Sale price per item} = \text{Retail price} - \text{Discount per item} = 50 - 40 = 10

   \[

   Now we can calculate the total revenue generated from selling the 1800 items:
   \[
   \text{Total revenue} = \text{Number of items sold} \times \text{Sale price per item} = 1800 \times 10 = 18000
   \]

   The store owed $15,000 to its creditors. To find out how much money they have left after paying off this debt, we subtract the amount owed from the total revenue:
   \[
