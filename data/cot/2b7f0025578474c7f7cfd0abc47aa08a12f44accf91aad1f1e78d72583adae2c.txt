To find out how much money the group earned from their candy bar sales, we'll follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of candy bars sold**:

   \[
   \text{Total candy bars sold} = \text{Number of members} \times \text{Average candy bars sold per member} = 20 \times 8
   \]

   Now, calculating that:
   \[
   20 \times 8 = 160
   \]

2. **Calculate the total earnings from the candy bars**: 
   Each candy bar costs $0.50. To find the total earnings, we need to multiply the total number of candy bars sold by the price of each candy bar.
   \[
   \text{Total earnings} = \text{Total candy bars sold} \times \text{Cost per candy bar} = 160 \times 0.50
   \]

   Now, calculating that:
   \[
   160 \times 0.50 = 80
   \]

Thus, the total money earned from the candy bar sales is $80.
