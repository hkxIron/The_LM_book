To find out how much Goldie earned in two weeks for pet-sitting, we first need to calculate her earnings for each week separately and then add them together.

     \]

3. **Calculate the total earnings for both weeks:**
   - Now, we add the earnings from both weeks together to find her total earnings:
     \[
     \text{Total earnings} = \text{Earnings for last week} + \text{Earnings for this week} 
     \]
     \[

     \]
