   \[
   \text{Bexley's goldfish} = 15 \times \frac{1}{3} = 15 \div 3 = 5
   \]
   So, Bexley brings 5 goldfish.

   \[
   \text{Total betta fish} = 10 + 4 = 14

   - Total goldfish now:
   \[
   \text{Total goldfish} = 15 + 5 = 20
   \]
   - Therefore, the total number of fish is:
   \[
   \text{Total fish} = 14 + 20 = 34
   \]

   - Hershel gifts \( \frac{1}{2} \) of his fish:
   \[
   \text{Fish gifted} = 34 \times \frac{1}{2} = 34 \div 2 = 17
   \]

5. **Calculate the total number of fish remaining:**
   - To find the remaining fish:
   \[
   \text{Fish remaining} = 34 - 17 = 17
   \]
