   \[
   \text{Number of muffins} = \frac{\text{Amount spent}}{\text{Cost per muffin}}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Number of muffins} = \frac{9}{0.75}
   \]

   Dividing $9 by $0.75 is the same as multiplying $9 by the reciprocal of $0.75, which is \(\frac{4}{3}\) (since \(0.75 = \frac{3}{4}\)):
   \[
   \text{Number of muffins} = 9 \times \frac{4}{3} = 12
   \]
