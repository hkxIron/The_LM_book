  \[

  \]

  \[
  9 \text{ dollars/hour} \times 30 \text{ hours/week} = 270 \text{ dollars/week}
  \]

  \[
  270 \text{ dollars/week} \times 52 \text{ weeks} = 14040 \text{ dollars}
  \]

**Step 3: Calculate his total savings over the two years.**
- Combining his allowance from the first year with his earnings from the second year:

  \[
  2600 \text{ dollars} + 14040 \text{ dollars} = 16640 \text{ dollars}
  \]

- He spends $35 a week on himself.
- Over two years (104 weeks), his total expenses are:

  \[
  35 \text{ dollars/week} \times 104 \text{ weeks} = 3640 \text{ dollars}
  \]

**Step 5: Calculate his net savings after expenses.**
- Subtract the total expenses from his total savings:

  \[
  16640 \text{ dollars} - 3640 \text{ dollars} = 13000 \text{ dollars}
  \]

**Step 6: Determine how much more money Thomas needs to buy the car.**
- The car costs $15,000, and Thomas has $13,000:

  \[
  15000 \text{ dollars} - 13000 \text{ dollars} = 2000 \text{ dollars}
  \]
