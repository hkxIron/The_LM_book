\[
\text{Average Speed} = \frac{\text{Total Distance}}{\text{Total Time}}

2. **Convert Total Time to Hours**:

   \[

   \[
   \text{Total Time} = 2 \text{ hours} + \frac{1}{3} \text{ hours} = 2.3333 \text{ hours}

   \[
