To find the total time it takes to groom 5 dogs and 3 cats, we first need to calculate the time it takes for each type of animal and then sum those times.

1. **Calculate the time for grooming dogs**:
   - The time taken to groom one dog is 2.5 hours.
   - To find the time for 5 dogs, we multiply the time for one dog by the number of dogs:
     \[
     \text{Time for 5 dogs} = 5 \times 2.5 \text{ hours} = 12.5 \text{ hours}
     \]

2. **Calculate the time for grooming cats**:
   - The time taken to groom one cat is 0.5 hours.
   - To find the time for 3 cats, we multiply the time for one cat by the number of cats:
     \[
     \text{Time for 3 cats} = 3 \times 0.5 \text{ hours} = 1.5 \text{ hours}
     \]

3. **Add the total time for both dogs and cats**:
   - Now, we will add the time taken for grooming dogs and cats:
     \[
     \text{Total time} = \text{Time for 5 dogs} + \text{Time for 3 cats} = 12.5 \text{ hours} + 1.5 \text{ hours} = 14 \text{ hours}
     \]

4. **Convert the total time from hours to minutes**:
   - Since there are 60 minutes in an hour, we convert the total hours to minutes by multiplying by 60:
     \[
     \text{Total time in minutes} = 14 \text{ hours} \times 60 \text{ minutes/hour} = 840 \text{ minutes}
     \]

Therefore, the total time it takes to groom 5 dogs and 3 cats is 840 minutes.
