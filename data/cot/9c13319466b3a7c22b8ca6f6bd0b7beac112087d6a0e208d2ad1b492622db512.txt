To determine how many yards of fabric Jenson and Kingsley need every 3 days, we will first calculate the total fabric required for each of them per day and then multiply that by the number of days.

     \[
     \text{Fabric for Jenson} = \text{Number of shirts} \times \text{Fabric per shirt} = 3 \text{ shirts} \times 2 \text{ yards/shirt} = 6 \text{ yards}

     \]

   - Now, we add the fabric needed by both Jenson and Kingsley to find the total fabric needed per day:
     \[
     \text{Total fabric per day} = \text{Fabric for Jenson} + \text{Fabric for Kingsley} = 6 \text{ yards} + 25 \text{ yards} = 31 \text{ yards}
     \]

4. **Calculate the total fabric needed for 3 days:**
   - Finally, we need to find out how much fabric they need in total for 3 days:
     \[
     \text{Total fabric for 3 days} = \text{Total fabric per day} \times 3 = 31 \text{ yards/day} \times 3 \text{ days} = 93 \text{ yards}
     \]
