   - Peter bought 9 kilos of tomatoes at $3 per kilo.

   - Total amount spent = cost of potatoes + cost of tomatoes + cost of cucumbers + cost of bananas.

   - Remaining money = initial amount - total amount spent.
   - Remaining money = $500 - $74 = $426.
