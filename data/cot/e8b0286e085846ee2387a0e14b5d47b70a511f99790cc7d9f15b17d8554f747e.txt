To find out how much John spends on paintballs each month, we need to calculate the total cost based on the number of times he plays and the cost of the paintballs.

1. **Determine the number of boxes John buys each month**: 
   - John plays paintball 3 times a month.
   - Each time he plays, he buys 3 boxes of paintballs.

   So, the total number of boxes John buys in a month is:
   \[
   \text{Total boxes} = \text{Number of times played} \times \text{Boxes per play} = 3 \text{ times} \times 3 \text{ boxes/time} = 9 \text{ boxes}
   \]

2. **Calculate the total cost of the paintballs**:
   - Each box of paintballs costs $25.
   
   Now, we can find the total cost by multiplying the total number of boxes by the cost per box:
   \[
   \text{Total cost} = \text{Total boxes} \times \text{Cost per box} = 9 \text{ boxes} \times 25 \text{ dollars/box} = 225 \text{ dollars}
   \]

Therefore, John spends $225 on paintballs each month.
