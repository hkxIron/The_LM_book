   \[

   \[
   \text{Interest} = 10\% \times \text{Total payment} = 0.10 \times 1200
   \]
   This results in:
   \[
   \text{Interest} = 120
   \]

3. **Calculate the total amount Aaron will owe his dad:**  
   To find the total amount Aaron will owe, we add the total payment to the interest:
   \[
   \text{Total amount owed} = \text{Total payment} + \text{Interest} = 1200 + 120
   \]

   \[
   \text{Total amount owed} = 1320
   \]
