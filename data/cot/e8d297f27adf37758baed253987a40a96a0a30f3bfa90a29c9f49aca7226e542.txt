   \]

   \[
   500,000 + 100,000 = 600,000
   \]

   \[
   \text{Tax} = 10\% \text{ of } 600,000 = 0.10 \times 600,000 = 60,000

   \[
   \text{Net Revenue} = 600,000 - 60,000 = 540,000
   \]

5. Divide the net revenue by the number of people:
   \[
   \text{Amount per person} = \frac{540,000}{4} = 135,000
   \]
