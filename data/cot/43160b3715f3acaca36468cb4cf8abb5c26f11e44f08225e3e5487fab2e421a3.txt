<reasoning>
To find out how many hours Nancy has to work to earn $70, we first need to determine her hourly wage based on her current earnings.

1. **Calculate Nancy's hourly wage**:
   She earns $28 for 4 hours of work. To find her hourly wage, we divide her total earnings by the number of hours worked:
   \[
   \text{Hourly wage} = \frac{\text{Total earnings}}{\text{Total hours}} = \frac{28}{4} = 7
   \]
   This means Nancy earns $7 per hour.

2. **Determine how many hours are needed to earn $70**:
   Now that we know her hourly wage is $7, we can find out how many hours she needs to work to earn $70 by dividing the desired earnings by her hourly wage:
   \[
   \text{Hours needed} = \frac{\text{Desired earnings}}{\text{Hourly wage}} = \frac{70}{7} = 10
   \]
   Therefore, Nancy has to work 10 hours to earn $70.
</reasoning>
<answer>10</answer>
