     \[
     \text{Apples in one of Pam's bags} = 3 \times \text{Apples in one of Gerald's bags} = 3 \times 40 = 120
     \]

     \[
     \text{Number of bags Pam has} = \frac{\text{Total apples Pam has}}{\text{Apples in one of Pam's bags}} = \frac{1200}{120}

     \[
