   \[

   \]

   Profit is calculated by subtracting the total value of the cards traded away from the value of the card received:
   \[
   \text{Profit} = \text{Value of card received} - \text{Total value of cards traded away}
   \]

   \[
   \text{Profit} = 21 \text{ dollars} - 16 \text{ dollars} = 5 \text{ dollars}
   \]
