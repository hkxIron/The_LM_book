   0.25x = 40

   \[
   x = \frac{40}{0.25}

   \[
   x = 40 \div 0.25 = 40 \times 4 = 160
   \]

   \[
   \text{Amount paid} = \text{Original price} - \text{Discount}
   \]

   \[
   \text{Amount paid} = 160 - 40 = 120
