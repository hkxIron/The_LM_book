<reasoning>
To find the cost of the discounted subscription, we first need to determine how much the discount is in dollars.

1. **Calculate the amount of the discount:**
   - The normal price of the subscription is $80.
   - The discount rate is 45%, which we can express as a decimal by dividing by 100: 
     \[
     45\% = 0.45
     \]
   - Now, we multiply the normal price by the discount rate to find the dollar amount of the discount:
     \[
     \text{Discount amount} = \text{Normal price} \times \text{Discount rate} = 80 \times 0.45
     \]

   Let's calculate that:
   \[
   80 \times 0.45 = 36
   \]
   This means the discount amount is $36.

2. **Calculate the discounted price:**
   - To find the final price after applying the discount, we subtract the discount amount from the normal price:
     \[
     \text{Discounted price} = \text{Normal price} - \text{Discount amount} = 80 - 36
     \]

   Now, let's calculate that:
   \[
   80 - 36 = 44
   \]
   Therefore, the discounted subscription cost is $44.
</reasoning>
