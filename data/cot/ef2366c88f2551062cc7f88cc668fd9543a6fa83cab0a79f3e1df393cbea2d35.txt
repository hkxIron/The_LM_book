     \text{Total marbles for Mara} = 12 \text{ bags} \times 2 \text{ marbles/bag} = 24 \text{ marbles}

     \[
     \text{Total marbles for Markus} = 2 \text{ bags} \times 13 \text{ marbles/bag} = 26 \text{ marbles}
     \]
   - Thus, Markus has 26 marbles.

3. **Determine how many more marbles Markus has compared to Mara:**
   - To find out how many more marbles Markus has than Mara, we subtract the total number of marbles Mara has from the total number of marbles Markus has:
     \[
     \text{Difference} = \text{Total marbles for Markus} - \text{Total marbles for Mara} = 26 - 24 = 2
     \]
