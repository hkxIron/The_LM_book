Given that Jack collects 2 liters of seawater, this means he has collected 2 * 1000 = 2000 ml of seawater.

The seawater is 20% salt. This means that for every 100 ml of seawater, there are 20 ml of salt.

To find out how many ml of salt are in the 2000 ml of seawater, we multiply the total ml of seawater by the percentage of salt in the seawater. 

Hence, the amount of salt is 20% of 2000 ml, that is, 0.20 * 2000 = 400 ml.

So, when all the water evaporates, Jack will be left with 400 ml of salt.
