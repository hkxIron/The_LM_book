To find out how many feet further down Jack is after going up and down the stairs, we need to calculate the total distance he moved up and down in feet. 

1. **Calculate the total number of steps Jack went up:**
   - Jack goes up 3 flights of stairs.
   - Each flight has 12 steps.
   - Total steps up = Number of flights × Steps per flight = 3 × 12 = 36 steps.

2. **Calculate the total number of steps Jack went down:**
   - Jack goes down 6 flights of stairs.
   - Total steps down = Number of flights × Steps per flight = 6 × 12 = 72 steps.

3. **Determine the net movement in steps:**
   - Jack's net movement = Steps down - Steps up = 72 - 36 = 36 steps down.

4. **Convert steps to inches:**
   - Each step is 8 inches high.
   - Total inches down = Net steps down × Height per step = 36 × 8 = 288 inches.

5. **Convert inches to feet:**
   - There are 12 inches in a foot.
   - Total feet down = Total inches down ÷ Inches per foot = 288 ÷ 12 = 24 feet.

Thus, after going up and down the stairs, Jack is 24 feet further down than when he started.
