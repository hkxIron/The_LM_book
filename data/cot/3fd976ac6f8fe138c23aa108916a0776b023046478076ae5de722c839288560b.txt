   - Guinea Fowls: 80

   - Chickens lost per day: 20
   - Turkeys lost per day: 8

   - Total chickens lost in a week = Daily loss of chickens × Number of days
     \[
     \text{Total chickens lost} = 20 \times 7 = 140
     \]
   
   - Total turkeys lost in a week = Daily loss of turkeys × Number of days
     \[
     \text{Total turkeys lost} = 8 \times 7 = 56
     \]

   - Total guinea fowls lost in a week = Daily loss of guinea fowls × Number of days
     \[
     \text{Total guinea fowls lost} = 5 \times 7 = 35
     \]

4. **Now, calculate the remaining number of each type of bird after one week:**
   - Remaining chickens = Initial chickens - Total chickens lost
     \[
     \text{Remaining chickens} = 300 - 140 = 160
     \]

   - Remaining turkeys = Initial turkeys - Total turkeys lost
     \[
     \text{Remaining turkeys} = 200 - 56 = 144
     \]

   - Remaining guinea fowls = Initial guinea fowls - Total guinea fowls lost
     \[
     \text{Remaining guinea fowls} = 80 - 35 = 45
     \]

5. **Finally, calculate the total number of birds left on the farm:**
   \[
   \text{Total birds left} = \text{Remaining chickens} + \text{Remaining turkeys} + \text{Remaining guinea fowls}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Total birds left} = 160 + 144 + 45 = 349
   \]
