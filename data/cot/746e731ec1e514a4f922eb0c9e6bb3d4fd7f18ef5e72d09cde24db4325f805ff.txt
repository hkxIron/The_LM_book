   \]

   \[
   \text{Time in hours} = \frac{10 \text{ minutes}}{60 \text{ minutes/hour}} = \frac{1}{6} \text{ hours}
   \]

3. **Calculate the required speed**: Speed is defined as distance divided by time. We now have the distance (2 miles) and the time in hours (\( \frac{1}{6} \) hours). We can calculate the required speed:
   \[
   \text{Speed} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Time}} = \frac{2 \text{ miles}}{\frac{1}{6} \text{ hours}} = 2 \text{ miles} \times 6 = 12 \text{ miles per hour}
   \]
