To solve this problem, let's define some variables to represent the years each teacher has been teaching:

Let \( T \) be the number of years Tom has been teaching.
Let \( D \) be the number of years Devin has been teaching.

According to the problem, we know two key pieces of information:

1. The total years of teaching for both Tom and Devin is 70 years:
   \[
   T + D = 70
   \]

2. Devin has been teaching for 5 years less than half the number of years that Tom has been teaching:
   \[
   D = \frac{1}{2}T - 5
   \]

Now we have a system of equations that we can solve. We will substitute the expression for \( D \) from the second equation into the first equation.

### Step 1: Substitute \( D \) in the total years equation
We take the equation:
\[
T + D = 70
\]
and substitute \( D \):
\[
T + \left(\frac{1}{2}T - 5\right) = 70
\]

### Step 2: Simplify the equation
Now, we simplify the equation:
\[
T + \frac{1}{2}T - 5 = 70
\]
This combines to:
\[
\frac{3}{2}T - 5 = 70
\]

### Step 3: Solve for \( T \)
Next, we need to isolate \( T \). First, add 5 to both sides:
\[
\frac{3}{2}T = 70 + 5
\]
\[
\frac{3}{2}T = 75
\]

Now, multiply both sides by \( \frac{2}{3} \) to solve for \( T \):
\[
T = 75 \times \frac{2}{3}
\]
Calculating this gives:
\[
T = 50
\]

### Step 4: Verify the solution
Now, we should verify that this value satisfies both conditions. If \( T = 50 \), we can find \( D \):
Using the first equation:
\[
D = 70 - T = 70 - 50 = 20
\]

Now we check the second condition:
Devin's years should be \( \frac{1}{2}T - 5 \):
\[
D = \frac{1}{2} \times 50 - 5 = 25 - 5 = 20
\]

Both conditions are satisfied, confirming our solution.

Therefore, the number of years Tom has been teaching is:
