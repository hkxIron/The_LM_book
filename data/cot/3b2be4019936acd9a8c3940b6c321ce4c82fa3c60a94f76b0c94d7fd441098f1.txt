To find out how many tickets Russel and Jen used at the circus, we need to calculate the total tickets spent by each of them based on the cost of the games they played and then sum those amounts.

   - The cost of one shooting game is 5 tickets.
   - Therefore, the total tickets used by Jen is calculated as follows:
     \[
     \text{Total tickets used by Jen} = \text{Number of games} \times \text{Cost per game} = 2 \times 5
     \]

     \[
     2 \times 5 = 10
     \]

     \[

     \]
   - Performing the multiplication:
     \[
     3 \times 3 = 9
     \]
   - Thus, Russel used 9 tickets.

   - Now, we add the total tickets used by Jen and Russel:
     \[
     \text{Total tickets used} = \text{Tickets used by Jen} + \text{Tickets used by Russel} = 10 + 9
     \]

     \[
     10 + 9 = 19
     \]
