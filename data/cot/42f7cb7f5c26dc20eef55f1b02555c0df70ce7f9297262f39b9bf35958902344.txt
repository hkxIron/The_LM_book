     \[
     \text{Rayden's ducks} = 3 \times \text{Lily's ducks} = 3 \times 20 = 60

     \[
     \text{Rayden's geese} = 4 \times \text{Lily's geese} = 4 \times 10 = 40
     \]

     \[
     \text{Difference in ducks} = \text{Rayden's ducks} - \text{Lily's ducks} = 60 - 20 = 40

     \[
     \text{Difference in geese} = \text{Rayden's geese} - \text{Lily's geese} = 40 - 10 = 30
     \]

5. **Calculate the total difference in ducks and geese:**

     \[
     \text{Total difference} = \text{Difference in ducks} + \text{Difference in geese} = 40 + 30 = 70
