     \[
     \text{Total time for pants} = \text{Number of pants} \times \text{Time per pant} = 12 \times 3 \text{ hours} = 36 \text{ hours}
     \]

3. **Calculate the total time for both shirts and pants**:
   - Total time is the sum of the time for shirts and the time for pants:
     \[
     \text{Total time} = \text{Total time for shirts} + \text{Total time for pants} = 15 \text{ hours} + 36 \text{ hours} = 51 \text{ hours}
     \]

   - The seamstress charges $30 per hour.
   - Therefore, the total cost is:
     \[
     \text{Total cost} = \text{Total time} \times \text{Cost per hour} = 51 \text{ hours} \times 30 \text{ dollars/hour} = 1530 \text{ dollars}
     \]
