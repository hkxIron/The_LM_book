<reasoning>
To find out how much money Mike will need to pay in total for the car, we will first determine the amount he will owe to the bank after taking a loan of $20000 at a 15% interest rate. 

1. **Identify the loan amount and interest rate**: Mike is taking a loan of $20000 from the bank at an interest rate of 15%. 

2. **Calculate the interest on the loan**: The interest for one year can be calculated using the formula:
   \[
   \text{Interest} = \text{Principal} \times \text{Rate}
   \]
   Here, the principal is $20000 and the rate is 15% (or 0.15 when converted to decimal).
   \[
   \text{Interest} = 20000 \times 0.15 = 3000
   \]
   This means that after one year, Mike will owe $3000 in interest on top of the principal amount.

3. **Calculate the total amount to be paid back to the bank**: To find the total amount Mike will need to pay back to the bank, we add the principal amount and the interest.
   \[
   \text{Total Payment} = \text{Principal} + \text{Interest} = 20000 + 3000 = 23000
   \]

4. **Determine how much Mike will pay in cash**: The total cost of the car is $35000. Since he is taking a loan of $20000, the amount he will pay upfront in cash is:
   \[
   \text{Cash Payment} = \text{Total Car Price} - \text{Loan Amount} = 35000 - 20000 = 15000
   \]

5. **Calculate the total amount Mike will pay for the car**: This includes the cash payment plus the total payment to the bank.
   \[
   \text{Total Amount Paid} = \text{Cash Payment} + \text{Total Payment to Bank} = 15000 + 23000 = 38000
   \]

Thus, the total amount Mike will need to pay for the car is $38000.
